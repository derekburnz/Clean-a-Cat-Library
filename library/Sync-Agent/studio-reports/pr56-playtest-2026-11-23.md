# PR #56 playtest (commit 6034891), 2026-11-23

Run on Derek's PC in a fresh local place built with Rojo. Never saved, never the live place.

## Result
The full loop works on desktop, but the first scrub cannot finish from the front. Only about 7 to 10 of the 16 mud patches face the default camera and the reveal needs 13. The tester had to walk to the back of the tub to clear the rest.

## What was seen
1. Switch off: grey floor and the player. A white "Menu" button shows on the left, so "nothing else appears" is not exact.
2. Switch on: tub and dirty grey cat appear 12 studs in front of spawn. From spawn the player's body hides the cat; the player must walk closer and tilt the camera down.
3. Desktop scrub: from the front, 7 patches cleared in about 6 s, then stuck at 10. Walking to the far side cleared 3 more and the rest popped at 13 of 16. Cat turned light grey and turned whiter with more rubbing. Three colored squares appeared; blue tapped, bow sat on the head, dark box appeared. Tapping it gave "+10 coins" and 3 s later a fresh dirty cat with all 16 patches.
4. First scrub time: about 25 s of automated dragging plus walking. Not comparable to the 8 s target (an automated drag is slower than a hand). The blocker is the hidden patches.
5. Gamepad: no pad attached. A ButtonR2 key press (same code path), cat centered, scripted camera sweep across the front: 7 patches in 15 s, front-facing only.
6. Switch off while playing: tub and cat vanish, camera returns to normal.
7. Console: no game errors, server or client. Only Studio's Assistant plugin failing to load and the expected "no DataStore" notices for an unpublished file.

## Not tested
iPhone SE emulator and all touch checks (the screen-control prompt timed out with nobody at the PC). Studio cannot fake real multi-finger touch, thumbstick plus second finger, or phone frame rate. From reading the code: a touch that starts on the cat locks the camera until the finger lifts, only that finger scrubs, and a drag starting elsewhere still turns the camera. None of this is verified in play.

## Suggested fix
Put the 13-patch reveal within reach of one camera view, or slowly turn the cat in the tub.
