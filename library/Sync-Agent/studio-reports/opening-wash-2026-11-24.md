## Findings
1. The wash view does start (desktop and a06). After "Wash him": Suds hops into the tub; the camera becomes a fixed close-up (Scriptable at world (0, 5.5, -5.5), 6.5 studs on the room side of the tub at (0, 2, -12), looking down 30 degrees, FOV 70); movement held; step bar and tool tray (sponge, brush, camera) appear. Desktop also shows the wash checklist panel; on the a06 it does not (maybe by design). Desktop run: scrub cleared all mud, step 2 brush, step 3 photo; whole wash works.
2. The character gets in the way depending on the side you press "Wash him" from. The camera spot is fixed and the avatar is not moved.
 - From the door side (desktop): avatar 4.6 studs behind the tub on the far side, 11.3 from the camera; shows behind the cat above his ears, blocks none of him.
 - From the room side (a06): stood 3.8 studs from the tub, between it and the camera; avatar ended 3.5 studs from the camera and covered 33% of the screen, hiding 87% of the cat's on-screen area (rays to the cat hit the avatar's back and hair first). The view barely seems to change, mostly your own back with a sliver of cat. Likely Derek's "doesn't enter the minigame".
3. Avatar blocks taps on the cat whenever it is in front. Camera-side: every tested point on the cat hit the avatar's hair accessory box before the cat, even where the cat shows on screen. Door side: nothing blocks the cat; only strokes above his ears land on the avatar. a06 scrub drags cleared no mud even after the avatar was moved away by script, so simulator drags are not getting through as touch (tooling limit); the blocking was measured by rays, not input.
 Fix ideas (Gameplay-Engineer's call): move or hide the avatar when the wash view starts (stand them beside the tub or make them see-through), or make the scrub rays ignore the player's character.
4. There is no tub until "Wash him": the trail leads to an empty pink and green rug. The tub prompt (CleanACat_OpeningTub at (0, 2, -12)) is an invisible part and the tub only appears once he is set down. Players may not see where to go. Worth checking with Design.
5. Smaller: after the brush step a large black block appears beside the tub: the placeholder PH_SpaPrototype.PH_Buttons.Camera at (4.5, 3, -11). No on-screen prompt button for "Pick up" or "Wash him" seen in either run; taps on the object worked. On the a06 the falling box passes behind the avatar's head and lands at its left; Suds in the open box is half behind the avatar's left arm.
