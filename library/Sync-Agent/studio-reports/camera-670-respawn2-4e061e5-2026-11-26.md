Respawn spam, 5 in 10 s: no stuck or spinning camera. Stays Custom on the new character, steady at yaw 3, about 90 degrees off.
WHY (found with a print in the RC session's local copy only): RespawnCameraController sees the camera is off and asks to turn it 6 times per respawn. But activeCamera() returns nil: the PlayerModule's GetCameras() gives no activeCameraController in this Studio build. So every ask takes the fallback, which sets Camera.CFrame at RenderPriority.Camera - 1. Roblox's camera module then overwrites it in the same frame, so the turn never lands. Likely fix: set the camera after the module (priority Camera + 1) and hold it a few frames, or turn the character's facing into the module another way. (Caveat: GetCameras()/activeCameraController may differ in a real client; confirm against a published dev client if possible.)
