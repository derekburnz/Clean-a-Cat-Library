# Creator Store audio search (scrubLoop, catShake), 2026-11-23

Run in Studio's Creator Store search (audio, free, verified creators). Nothing inserted or bought.
Quirks: the verified-only filter did not seem to apply (user uploads came back). The 0.4 to 1.2 s duration filter returned nothing for every shake search, so those were rerun with a 3 s cap.

## scrubLoop (sponge scrub, soap scrub, washing; 1 to 4 s)
1. 9125408737 "Brush Spinning Rubbing On Wood Circular Moti (SFX)", ProSoundEffects, 3.1 s. Steady circular scrubbing. Best loop candidate.
2. 9117187720 "Paint Canvas 11 (SFX)", ProSoundEffects, 1.3 s. Dry brush strokes. 9117186886 "Paint Canvas 2 (SFX)" same set, 1.3 s.
3. 98433355182276 "scrubbing with sponge - sound effect", MishiFan89, length not listed. Closest name but a user upload, not a Roblox audio partner: check its license before use.
"washing" returned only washing machine sounds.

## catShake (dog shake, fur shake, water shake; 0.4 to 1.2 s)
Nothing sounds like a real fur shake.
1. 9120582975 "Water Slosh Short Shake Movement 4 (SFX)", ProSoundEffects, 0.6 s.
2. 9120583261 "Water Slosh Short Shake Movement 7 (SFX)", ProSoundEffects, 0.8 s. Same set: 0.7 s (9120582976), 0.9 s (9120583277).
3. 9120769888 "Wing Flaps 4 (SFX)", ProSoundEffects, 1.9 s. Flutter that could read as fur if trimmed under 1.2 s.
"dog shake" and "fur shake" returned only cloth, footsteps and grass.

ProSoundEffects is a Roblox audio partner, so safe inside Roblox.
Recommendation: brush 9125408737 for the loop; for the shake, a water slosh layered with a short cloth flutter, or the CC0 fallback in the sound list.
