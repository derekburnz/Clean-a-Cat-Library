# Does "[NEW]" before a game name help? (2026-11-24 ET, Growth-Agent)

## Short answer
No evidence that it helps and no rule that bans it. Recommendation: launch as plain "Clean a Cat". Put "new" in the description's update line and the event card instead.

## What I found (six pages read, dates are the page dates)
- **Roblox staff, 2024-12-09 (DevForum, "reducing exposure of low quality content"):** the only official page on titles. Titles, thumbnails, genre and description must accurately reflect the game or the game can be left out of Home, Charts and search; copies of other games are pushed down; avoid irrelevant keywords. Nothing on brackets, tags, emojis or caps. Official.
- **Roblox Creator Hub description guide (updated 2026-11-24, marked experimental, for select creators):** about the description and Google style search, not the in-game title. It uses "[NEW]" only as an example line inside a description's update section, and says tags and hashtags "are unused in our ranking algorithms". Official, but not about titles.
- **RoWatcher article, 2026-07-06 (third party opinion):** titles act like search keywords and the front of the title counts most, so lead with plain words about what you do. No data, nothing on brackets.
- **DevForum threads, 2022 and 2023 (player and developer opinion):** one person suggests a bracket tag such as [CHRISTMAS] or an update tag to catch returning players, others say extra words may confuse people. No numbers, no test, no Roblox answer.
- **Not read:** the thread "Capitalization is the key to attention" came back empty, so caps are unchecked.
- **No measured test of bracket tags in titles exists in anything I found.** Anything claiming one is opinion.

## Why plain is the safer launch name
1. Search likes plain words that say what the game is; a bracket at the front adds nothing for search and pushes the real words back.
2. A "[NEW]" tag goes stale. A game with it three months later breaks the accuracy rule's spirit and looks old-fashioned to returning players.
3. Roblox rules favour accurate titles; a tag is not banned, but a title edit can also get caught by the name filter and may not save without notice (a known trap in our role file).
4. Home does not read the tag, and home tap rate comes mostly from the icon and thumbnails (home-recommendations.md).
5. "New" for returning players belongs where Roblox reads it: the description update line, event cards and the game's own update notes.

## If Derek still wants to test it
Do it after launch, not at launch, so we do not mix it with the first reads: change the title once, log the minute, compare tap rate and search plays for a clean week against the week before, and keep the baseline stable (no ad changes). Treat a lean in a small game as one weak reason (ab-tests.md). Titles cannot be split between players, so it is a before and after read, not an A/B test.

## Decision for Derek
Recommended: plain "Clean a Cat", no bracket tag. Needs Derek's call only if he disagrees.
