- Hook (0 to 2 s): the scrub already under way in the tub close up, a clean stripe of real coat showing through the mud and bubbles rising. Payoff on screen at once, no truck, no loading.
- Beats in: scrub close up (0-6), rinse and the coat colour appearing (6-9), brush puffing the fur and the bow tied (9-12), clean cat shaking off or the before and after card (12-15).

- Dev switches: whatever shows the final scrub and bow look on dev; no pack button, no shop.

## B. Social clip (about 30 s, 1080x1920, subtitles and voice added later by Social-Video-Agent)
- Hook (0 to 2 s): the muddy cat's coat revealed by one scrub stroke, mid action. Opening question in the caption line: what colour is this cat under the mud?

- Beats out: crates and odds, starter pack, Robux, coin totals that look paid, shop, check in, any "free" wording.
- Real gameplay only, no cat clipping the tub, no dark scenes, hide the HUD menus.
