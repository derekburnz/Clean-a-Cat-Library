# Rewards for likes, favourites and group joins: rule check (2026-11-25 ET, Growth-Agent)

For Planning-Agent's rule check (Derek, 6:09 PM ET: a pet for joining the group and liking the game; notify, like and favourite asks spread out). OFFICIAL = on a Roblox page, DEV = developer forum opinion, JUDGMENT = my call.

## Short answer
I found no Roblox rule that forbids rewarding a like, a favourite or a group join, and no Roblox page that allows it. So it is not confirmed either way. Safest version, which is also our design: reward the group join only (it can be checked), and ask for the like in words without making the pet depend on it.

## What I read
- OFFICIAL, Community Standards (about.roblox.com, no date) and Rules of Conduct (help article, latest entry May 15, 2025): nothing on rewards for likes, favourites, ratings or follows. The closest are: "Deceptive, sensational, duplicative, or otherwise misleading content or metadata" used to drive engagement, and a rule against offering Robux payment for "joining groups, referring members" through the group payout system. That second rule is about paying Robux out of group funds, not about an in-game pet.
- OFFICIAL, Creator Hub monetization page: players must never be "misled, confused, or pressured". Nothing on likes or notifications.
- Not read: Roblox's discovery best practices page (the link failed) and the "Permissible Links" post; so a rule could still sit there.
- DEV only, no staff reply marked in any thread: one forum user says asking for likes and giving a code is not a violation (June 2025); another says it is "pretty sure" against the rules (April 2024); a March 2026 thread says it "might not be against the rules" but objects to misleading like rewards. Rewarding a group join is called safe by several developers (opinion, no source).
- DEV technical claims: a like cannot be checked by a game (only the player's own account can see it); favourites and group membership can be checked through public services, though one developer calls the favourite check abuse. Do not rely on either like or favourite checks.

## What it means for the design (JUDGMENT)
1. **Reward the group join, never the like.** The claim button checks group membership only. It is the one action a game can verify and the only one forum opinion treats as safe.
2. **Do not say the pet needs a like.** If the card says "like the game to get the pet" but the claim only checks the group, that is a false statement (a misleading metadata risk, the one rule that does exist). Word it as: "Join our group to get Biscuit the cat. Enjoying the game? A like helps others find it." The like line stands apart and promises nothing.
3. **Our standing rule stays intact for likes.** The monetization-policy skill and my role file say never to reward likes, favourites or follows. That is the owner's own rule from the skill and it is not contradicted if only the group join is rewarded. If Derek wants the pet tied to a like or favourite anyway, it cannot be checked and it is not confirmed allowed, so it would be a risk he takes knowingly.
4. **Group join for a young audience:** the group's wall and chat should be off or tightly moderated, and the card says it is Creampuff Games' group. Some young players cannot join groups under their account settings; the card must not block anything else for them, and they can still get the pet later. Offer a non-group route for restricted players if easy (JUDGMENT, optional).
5. **One fixed pet**, same pet for everyone, no random part, so no odds are needed. Obvious place: the gift stand in the spa, labelled by the pet's picture.
6. **Not shown in the first minutes:** the ask appears at about minute 5 at the earliest (see 7).

## The other points from Planning's note
- **Notify, like and favourite asks, spread out (point 2):** Roblox's pages I read have no rule on when to ask. Keep them in different sessions or minutes: notification prompt after the first clean cat only if the player is 13 or older; like and favourite ask about minute 5, once; group gift stand always on the stand, never a pop-up. One ask at a time, none during a cat in hand. A no is final for that visit. This fits the rule that tutorial steps are tapped, no menu opens itself, and the first-minute exit read.
- **Quests, special visitors and crates (point 3):** earned crates with the same chance list as bought crates is allowed and recommended (see the crate file). Free random rewards need no odds list under Roblox's page, but show it anyway.
- **Events (point 4):** Christmas first, Cat Show Weekend kept in the vault: fine. Plan an event card and a description update line, per playbooks/store-copy-and-event-cards.md, and only after the event is live.
- **Store (point 5):** half dirty, half clean second thumbnail yes, "cat spa" in the description yes. Both fit the promise check.
- **Visiting, friend bonus, no trading (point 6):** friend play counts toward Roblox's co-play days signal; fine. Visiting must not let a visitor take or change anything of the owner's.
- **Server size six (point 7):** no Roblox page or evidence I read argues for or against six. JUDGMENT: six is fine for a cosy spa. Make sure new players are placed into a server that already has players before opening an empty one, so a new player does not land alone on Home arrival; read the playtime funnel by server fill when numbers exist.

## Update 2026-11-25 (later): how other games do it (Derek: "plenty of other games do things for likes")
Same sources as above; nothing new on Roblox's pages. I name only what the pages and forum threads show.
| Way | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Group join gift, claim checks the group only | one fixed reward after joining the group | Not covered by any Roblox page I read. DEV: called safe. Common practice. Checkable. |
| Like goal code, thanks after the mark | "Thanks for 1,000 likes! Code: ____", out after the number is reached, open to every player | Not covered by any Roblox page. DEV: one user says a code for 1,000 likes is not a violation. Common practice. Not tied to any one player's like. |
| Like goal board, promised in advance | "At 5,000 likes everyone gets a code" | Same as above, not covered; common practice. It promises a prize at a future like count, which our skill and playbook rule out (see below). |
| Card that asks for like and group but checks only the group | "Like and join to claim" | Common practice. Risky: the like is not checked, so the words are misleading; the one real rule found is about misleading content used to drive engagement. Avoid this wording. |
| Reward for the player's own like or favourite | check the player's like | A game cannot see a like; favourite checks are called abuse by one developer. Not reliable, not recommended. |
OFFICIAL answer: none of the five is confirmed allowed or banned on Roblox's pages I could read. Only ones named in the rules: misleading metadata to drive engagement, Robux payouts for joining groups, and pressure on young buyers. "Plenty of games do it" is true as common practice, and is not an official OK.

Our own standing rule (monetization-policy skill, store-copy playbook, role file): never reward likes, favourites or follows and never promise a code at a future like count; a thank-you code for a mark already reached, open to everyone, is the most that is safe. That rule belongs to Derek and he may change it.
- **Option A (matches our rule, my pick):** group gift stand with one fixed pet; the like is asked in words, no reward; like goal codes are released only as thank-yous after the number is reached, past tense, open to everyone. The board shows reached marks and past codes, not a future promise.
- **Option B (Derek's idea, common practice, not confirmed allowed):** a board at the stand with future like goals where everyone gets a code. If Derek chooses it: the reward goes to every player whether or not they liked (so no individual like is rewarded and nothing is misleading), wording is plain ("Thanks for 5,000 likes! Code: ____" once reached; the goal line says only the number), no countdown or "hurry" wording, nobody is asked to like to get a personal reward, and the like count on the board is updated by hand or by an approved service because a game cannot read the count itself. Risk Derek takes: Roblox could treat it as engagement manipulation; I found no rule that does, and no staff reply either way.
- **Timeline (Design-Agent): like and group ask at about minute 7, favourite ask in session 2 or after 10 cats:** fine. One ask at a time, once, a no is final for that visit, never while a cat is in hand, and the gift stand stays on the stand so nothing pops up. Keep the like and group asks on separate lines so the card does not read as "like to claim".

## Update 2026-11-25 (later): favourite and group join gate, and the Kids and Select rule (Review-QA's check)
- **Kids and Select rule, OFFICIAL** (Roblox DevForum announcement, Aug 24, 2026, "New Restrictions on Reward-Driven Media Feeds", https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-new-restrictions-on-reward-driven-media-feeds/4829188): a game is removed from Kids and Select only if it combines all three of a media feed (short videos, images or stories), autoplay or looping or infinite scroll, and a reward or implied reward for continued watching. A favourite or a group join is none of these, so this rule does not apply to our gift stand. The announcement says nothing about favourites, likes, ratings or group joins. (The docs page https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/kids-and-select returned 404 today.)
- **Rules of Conduct risk, OFFICIAL text as read:** the closest line is about "deceptive, sensational, duplicative, or otherwise misleading content or metadata" used to drive engagement (Community Standards, https://about.roblox.com/community-standards). An honest card that says what the pet needs (favourite and group) is not misleading. No page I read bans a reward for a favourite, a like or a group join, and none allows it either, so it stays NOT CONFIRMED. The one real rule on group joins is about paying Robux out of group funds, which a pet is not.
- **What lowers the risk (JUDGMENT):** say exactly what is checked (favourite and group, like only asked), no hurry words, a non-gated route for players who cannot favourite or join (a restricted account): a later free pet or the same pet at a small coin price, so no child is left out; the group wall off or moderated.
- **Review before launch, not blocking dev:** gating a free pet on a favourite and a group join is a common practice and not confirmed allowed. Derek decided it in. A small, unconfirmed risk: a moderator could read it as engagement manipulation. Fast rollback: keep the stand behind its own switch.
