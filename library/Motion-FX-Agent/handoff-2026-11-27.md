## How helper and customer motion works now (after today's fixes)
- **`Effects/CustomerMotion.luau`** drives every `CleanACat_NPC_Customer_`/`CleanACat_NPC_Staff_` figure and the Builder.
  - Joints are child Models `<Model>_<Joint>`, each turned about its own X from the Body part.
  - Budget: MAX 10 figures, full rate within 30 studs, half rate to 60, rest pose past that.
  - Debug attribute `MotionRig` on each customer: "no body", "no joints" or "rig <joints>".
  - The carrying arm turns back in when no carrier shows (armIn). `CustomerMotion.atRest(model, fn)` runs a clone at true rest (CustomerLeave uses it).
- **Staff lean (1137):**
  - `rig.lean` turns the torso about `rig.hip`, the bottom of the Body part. The Body part and the other `_Body` parts move together in one BulkMoveTo; the legs use the upright frame.
  - `baseOf(rig)` rebuilds the upright frame from the hip point and the flattened facing.
  - `rig.reach(lean, arm, side, left)` gives the Grip's world frame for the solve.
  - `rig.lean` is set to 0 when the wash stops, and toRest writes the straight torso.
- **`Effects/HelperWash.luau`** (switch helperWash):
  - **Step:** `phase(progress)` takes the server's `WorkerProgress` on `WorkerCat_<id>` and returns soap, scrub, rinse or dry.
  - **Spots:** `target()` gives spots on the coat.
  - **Coat point:** `coatAt` rays down onto the cat (include the cat only).
  - **Solve:** `solve()` (damped least squares) sets lean, arm and side.
  - **Placing the tool:** `place()` clones the tool (`CleanACat_Tool_Shampoo`, `_Brush`, `_Shower`, `_Towel`) to the Grip. The shampoo and shower are aimed with `aimTool`; the brush and towel use the fixed holdFor angles, which Sync passed.
  - **Effects:** the shower beam ends at the first fur hit via a raycast. Puffs fire on beats, and the brush swish plays through the Audio module. Effects run only within 30 studs.
  - **Cleanup:** client copies are named `CleanACat_Fx_HelperTool`, `CleanACat_Fx_HelperWater` and `CleanACat_Fx_HelperWaterEnd`; stopHelper removes them.
