# Clean a Cat: proposed design  (2026-11-24 ET, Planning-Agent, status: PROPOSAL, not agreed)

This is what the one-page design becomes if Derek takes our pick on every question of his plan page, round two (`pre-development-plan.md`). It is written ahead so no time is lost after his answers. **Nothing here is decided. Other threads keep building from `gdd-draft.md` until Derek says yes.** Each line that differs from the draft is marked (new) or (changed). Question ids in brackets point to the plan page. Research: `loop-options.md`. Derek's notes: `playtest-notes.md`.

**Hook (one sentence):** dirty cats line up at your spa, you pick who is next and scrub each one by hand to find out what coat is hiding under the mud.

**Name and first minute** (changed 2026-11-24, decided by Derek the same day in three answers) [firstminute]: the game opens with a rescue. A truck drives past, a plain brown box falls off the back and lands in front of the player, they tap it open and find a messy, muddy kitten. Cleaning him up is the tutorial and once he is clean he becomes Suds, so his name comes from the wash. How the scene plays is DECIDED (Derek 2026-11-24 1:36 PM ET: "A for sure for truck"): a quick intro the player watches, the truck going by and the box falling in front of them, about 4 seconds and no more, then the camera is the player's. The played version with the camera always the player's is dropped. The box is tapped at about second 4 and the scrub starts at about second 12. The box is decided: we proposed a soap crate marked Suds so the opening would not look like My Pet Salon's, and Derek said no ("not a soap crate should be a random brown box", 2026-11-24 12:27 PM ET).

**Core loop**
- Every 30 to 60 seconds: pick a cat from the bench, wash it in 4 to 6 short steps and get coins, a tip and stars, which buy tools, helpers and stations. (changed: a pick and several steps)
- Every session (aim for 20 minutes or more): wash about 12 cats, reveal new coats for the Cat Index, keep a stray for the window, hire or speed up a helper and start a build before leaving. (changed: was 10 to 15 minutes)
- Every week: a different visitor cat each day, rare visitors unlocked by spa stars, new coats on a fixed day and Derek's events.

**How players get cats** [keep]
- Customers bring cats and take them home. Every coat revealed fills a slot in the Cat Index.
- Now and then a stray waits at the back door. A washed stray stays in the display window and earns tips. The very first cat is the muddy kitten rescued in the opening, who becomes Suds and is the same for everyone. He is not in the Cat Index (decided by Derek 2026-11-24: "dont include him on" the index). He has his own card outside the count, does not sit in the window and earns no tips. (changed 2026-11-24) The first index stamp comes from the first customer cat and the first window stray comes at about minute 4.
- The coat is hidden under the mud until the wash is done. (new) The chance of each coat is on a board, cats are free and nothing sold changes the chances.
- No catching, no buying cats and no trading [trading].

**Owned pets: Derek's lean of 2026-11-24, OPEN, nothing is built from it** [keep, crates, petslots]
His words: "the cleaning cats should be how to make money and unrelated to cats/pets your shop owns those should come from crates that are hard to earn or purchased with coins or maybe will think of a different system but human workers and pets like the game attached" (the My Pets window of My Pet Salon). "Maybe you assign one pet per either yourself and one per worker (maybe make it an upgrade slot for the worker they can unlock or you pay to unlock) and the pets for workers can boost how fast they are or money the workers make and make the other an upgrade for coins". He will play with these ideas after more playtests.
What it would change in this page if he keeps it:
- Out: strays that are washed and kept, the display window that earns tips and the first stray at about minute 4. The lines above in "How players get cats" then shrink to: customers bring cats and take them home.
- Stays, by our pick: the hidden coat and the Cat Index for washed cats. Without it the wash loses its surprise and crates become the only thing to chase. Derek has not said whether the index stays.
- In: pets from crates. Our pick for the rules: crates are earned by play or bought with coins and never with Robux, the chance of every pet is on the crate before it is opened, a sure new pet after a set number of crates and no timers, stock counters or near miss tricks. A rare visitor's reward can be a crate. PolicyService is still read per player. The 2x Coins pass means Robux speeds up coins, so the chances are shown on every crate to stay inside Roblox's paid random items rule (monetization-policy, section 2).
- In: pet slots. One for the player, open from the start, and one per worker, unlocked with coins. Our pick: the worker's pet boosts the money that worker makes and coins buy the worker's speed. The guard rail stays: workers with pets earn less than the player's own washing.
- Moves: the tip jar that fills while away has to come from workers and their pets, not from window cats. "No buying cats" becomes "pets are bought with coins through crates, never with Robux". No trading still holds.
- Not settled: whether pets are cats, where they are seen in the spa and whether Suds takes the player's own slot as a free starter.
- Likeness to My Pet Salon is not a concern for Derek (2026-11-24: "even if the game is similar to my pet salon I am confident we can do it better"). So the aim is to do each part better, starting with what he disliked there: the cutscene with no skip, the same swipe three times, 16 step jobs, the pet turning by itself, one customer at a time and the hard selling. Names, characters, art, sounds and wording stay ours.

**The bench: who is next** (new) [queue, impatience]
- Three or four cats wait, each with a card: name, how dirty, what kind of dirt and what it pays. (new 2026-11-24) Each card has one line of our own on how the cat got so dirty, which hints at the kind of dirt.
- A Next cat button takes the first cat in line for players who do not want to choose. (new 2026-11-24) My Pet Salon has both a next pet button and a manual pick (Derek checked 2026-11-24), so the pick itself is not new on Roblox. What ours adds is cats that differ, shown on the card.
- Cats differ so the pick matters: a muddier cat pays more and a fussy cat tips more but waits less.
- A waiting cat gets grumpy and only its bonus tip shrinks. A cat that waits far too long goes home and comes back later. Nothing is ever lost.

**Helpers** (new) [helpers, helperlook]
- Bought with coins and never with Robux. A helper takes a common cat from the bench, washes it slowly and earns fewer stars than the player would.
- Rare cats and strays always wait for the player.
- Helpers are human staff in spa aprons, not cats. They reuse the customer figures the game already builds. (changed 2026-11-24: Derek's lean, "helpers are likely going to be staff like humans", then his "ok" in the project chat to staff plus Suds as mascot. Plan page question `helperlook`, saved as option 1.) Whether the builder kitten for stations becomes a person too is still open.

**Suds, the mascot** (new 2026-11-24, Derek's words: "shouldn't stand", "stays with you as your spa mascot through the game that explains stuff/is used for when you unlock new things") [helperlook, hud, return]
- Suds walks on four paws like every other cat, so he never holds a tool and never washes. He lives on a cushion on the front desk.
- He explains by going there: he trots to a new thing and sits by it. His words show in a help bubble along the bottom of the screen: his face, one short title, one sentence that says why and one button that does the step [sudsbubble]. One bubble at a time, clear of the stick and the jump button, gone after the tap. A Tips switch in Settings turns the bubbles off after the tutorial. He takes the place of the floating gold arrow for things in the world. The player does every tap.
- Rules that keep him inside the tutorial rules and the young audience rules: he shows each thing once and stops when the player has done it, he never opens a window, he never points at anything that costs Robux and he is never sad or cross, also not when the player comes back after days away.
- On day 2 he meets the player at the door and points at the full tip jar and at today's visitor cat.

**The wash** (changed) [steps, joblength, turn, praise, tools]
- Four kinds of step at launch: scrub the mud, rinse with the shower, pick burrs out of the fur and towel dry. Later tools add nail trim, comb, shampoo and the dryer.
- Each cat takes 4 to 6 steps of 8 to 15 seconds and never the same step twice in a row. A Step 2 of 4 line shows progress and a small tick list shows this cat's steps. (new 2026-11-24)
- The cat holds still during a step. The game turns it between steps and two buttons let the player turn it.
- Praise words name what the player did. A combo count pauses on a slip and does not reset.
- Tool upgrades change shape and feel as well as speed: a wider sponge, a fan spray, a comb that shows hidden burrs.

**After each cat** (new) [recap]: a recap with stars, coins and the tip. Three stars is normal and a hidden fourth marks a perfect wash. The full card shows for the first cats and for any new or rare cat, a slim strip for the rest. (new 2026-11-24) The full card sits at the side so the clean cat stays in view, lists one line per reason and keeps its button out of the bottom right corner, where Roblox's badge note appears.

**Rare visitors** (new) [rare]: unlocked as the spa earns stars. A rare visitor brings a rare cat and wants a near perfect wash, with the same short job as any cat. A finished visit gives a lasting reward, for example a small permanent tip bonus and a framed photo for the wall. Chances are on the board and no visitor can be bought.

**First session** (brand new player, phone; times are targets to test; changed 2026-11-24 for the Suds opening. Design-Agent's step by step plan: `Design-Agent/first-session-2026-11-24/opening-and-tutorial-plan.md`)
- 0:00 Loaded on the pavement in front of the spa, facing the street. The camera shows the truck and the box for about 4 seconds and then goes back to the player (decided).
- 0:01 to 0:04 A truck passes and a plain brown box tumbles off the back and lands a few steps away. It wiggles and an arrow says Open.
- 0:04 The player taps the box. A muddy kitten with no name yet pops out, shakes and hops into the player's arms.
- Decided by Derek 2026-11-24: the player lifts the kitten out of the box and carries him to the tub (the carry from Catch a Cat Cafe; the hold pose waits for Derek's example). Placing him in the tub switches to a close up wash view, and the steps are taught there. The scene starts the moment the character loads, with no loading screen and no standing still.
- 0:07 A trail leads through the open front to the tub. The player taps the wash button, which does not say Suds yet.
- 0:12 Scrub, then rinse and towel dry: three short steps.
- 0:30 to 0:45 The clean coat is revealed, the kitten is named Suds and he gets his score, always three stars. He hops onto his cushion on the front desk and is the player's from then on.
- About 1:00 The first customer arrives with the guaranteed easy common cat. Its wash gives the first coins, the first tip and the first index stamp. The Index button pops in.
- About 1:45 Two more customers arrive and the bench opens. From here the player picks who is next.
- About 2:30 First tool upgrade, instant. Suds trots to the Tools button.
- 3:00 to 5:00 A third kind of dirt appears (burrs). The first stray waits at the back door at about minute 4 and moves into the window after its wash [hud, keep].
- About 6:00 First station build, seconds long.
- About 10:00 The first helper can be hired. A third and fourth cat wait on the bench.
- About 15:00 The first long build is offered (hours), on its own slot [longbuild].
- No like or favourite ask, no shop prompt and no window that opens by itself in this session [favourite]. Tutorial steps are tapped by the player.
- The opening plays once per save. A player who leaves in the middle comes back at the tub with the kitten still muddy.
- Switches: the opening and Suds as guide each ship behind their own off switch, so the first test can run the opening against spawning at the tub.

**Checks on the Suds opening** (Planning-Agent, 2026-11-24)
- Fun first 5 minutes: fits. The first tap is at about second 4 and it is a surprise. The cost is the scrub starting about 9 seconds later than spawning at the tub. Roblox judges a game on its first minutes and no source gives a safe number, so both versions are run and the one that holds more new players stays.
- Low bounce: Derek disliked the cutscene with no skip in My Pet Salon. A watched intro is fine if it is capped at about 4 seconds, shows only the truck and the box and hands the camera back before the first tap. Longer than that needs a skip.
- Day 2: fits and helps. A kitten the player rescued is a reason to come back that the old stray was not, and Suds at the door shows the tip jar and the day's visitor.
- Tutorial rules: fit, with the four Suds rules above.
- Clash, small: the first stray no longer comes first, so the window lesson moves to about minute 4 and the bench pick moves from about 1:00 to about 1:45.
- Clash, for Gameplay-Engineer: the server opens bench picks when the save has stars above zero (`SpaService`, the guaranteed common cat rule). Suds's stars would open picks one cat early. The gate has to count customers served.
- Likeness: the box with a muddy cat is how My Pet Salon opens. We flagged it and proposed a soap crate. Derek chose the plain brown box, so that is settled. What keeps the opening ours: the truck, a short intro of about 4 seconds and the kitten earning the name Suds from his wash.
- Tone: the box lands soft, Suds pops out happy and nobody is shown hurt or left behind on purpose.
- Cost: a plain truck with no brand, a brown box and a four legged Suds on the shared cat body. The truck runs on the player's device only, so other players on the street never see it.

**Progression:** tools (instant), stations (seconds to 1 hour), helpers (coins), rooms (hours, 24 at most, offered after about minute 15), rare visitors (spa stars), then a Grand Reopening: move to a bigger spa, keep every cat and gain a permanent tip bonus [reopen]. The Grand Reopening is built after the first test, but the save data allows for it from the start. No paid skips on any timer [skips].

**Economy**
- Sources: coins, tips and stars per cat, tips from window cats, index bonuses and a small bonus per friend in the server.
- Sinks: tools, helpers and their speed, stations, rooms and decor.
- The one pace number stays cats washed to afford the next purchase. The numbers in `docs/design/clean-a-cat/tuning.md` were set for a single 8 second scrub. With a job of about a minute they all need a new pass by Design-Agent once the job length is timed on a phone.
- Guard rail: helpers and window cats together earn less than the player's own washing at the same stage, so washing by hand stays the best way to earn.

**Reason to come back tomorrow** (changed) [return]: empty slots in the index, a tip jar that fills while away and stops at a cap and one different visitor cat each day. No streak and nothing lost by missing a day. Notifications reach only players of 13 and over. Derek noted on 2026-11-24 that many popular games ask for them almost at once, so our pick moves the ask up: once, after the first clean cat at about minute 1, in Suds's bubble, behind a switch [notifyask].

**Social** [friends, visit]: spas sit side by side on one street. Players see each other's windows and reveals but do not walk in at launch. Each friend in the server adds 10% to tips, shown on screen from the start. Not in: stealing, trading, fighting and typed text. Friends as customers [friendcust]: our pick is now to leave it out at launch. Derek checked Restaurant Tycoon 3 on 2026-11-24 and it does not use friends as waiters or customers. It waits for his tap or word and is not to be built.

**Monetization** (follows monetization-policy) [passes]: three game passes at launch: 2x Coins, Second Builder and Golden Sponge. Coins, helpers, rare visitors and cats are never sold, so nothing random is ever paid for. PolicyService is fetched per player from day one.

**Risks**
- Fading: five of the six cleaning games from this summer are at 3% of their peak or less. Mitigation: the collection, helpers and the Grand Reopening give more to chase than the cleaning.
- A longer job can bore: My Pet Salon's same swipe three times did. Mitigation: 4 to 6 short steps, never two alike in a row and Derek's phone test before more is built.
- Waiting pressure can upset young players. Mitigation: only a bonus tip shrinks and no cat is lost.
- The opening delays the first scrub by about 9 seconds. Mitigation: it is about 4 seconds of watching at most, and the first test runs it against spawning at the tub.
- Scope: this is more than the first draft. Mitigation: the slice is one helper, three bench seats, 12 coats and no rare visitor.
- No proof: no source gives a tested number for any hook, wait or job length. Mitigation: ship choices behind switches, run two versions and read Roblox's own numbers.
- Policy: the hidden coat is random. Mitigation: it is free, the chances are shown and coins cannot be bought. The spa keeps an open front and only cats are bathed.

**Lessons from Derek's My Pet Salon screenshots** (2026-11-24): what we take and what we do not is in `my-pet-salon-screens-2026-11-24.md`. In short: the bottom help bubble, the side recap card, the tick list of steps, the dirty story line, an index with shapes, counts per rarity and the next bonus, settings with a tips switch and a smoother on phones switch and about 24 coats on day one if Model-Agent confirms the cost [session]. Not taken: offers on screen in the first session, stock counters, coins for Robux, paid skips and rewards for likes or favourites.

**Rules Derek set on 2026-11-24:** dev first. Everything is uploaded to the dev place and nothing goes to the live game until he confirms it is good. Feature switches stay off on live until he decides the game.

**From his playtests of 2026-11-24** (Planning-Agent/playtest-notes.md): the horse game put a timer where the wash should be and felt like an egg game to him, so in ours the wash is always done by hand and waits belong to builds and upgrades only. Clean Everything keeps its players with maps, counters and friends on top of the tidying, so the spa keeps things to count and unlock in view. Neither game changes the open loop choice, which waits for Restaurant Tycoon 3 and Barber Simulator.

**Ideas from Restaurant Tycoon 3** (Derek's playtest of 2026-11-24, detail in restaurant-tycoon-3-screens-2026-11-24.md): one goal bar at the top with a count, a Show Me button when a rare visitor comes in, an upgrades window that shows locked upgrades and what each requires, upgrades that add new things to do (a new service, a new kind of customer, more room, a sign that brings cats more often) and workers hired and levelled with coins. A choice of spa front comes after the first wash, never before it.

**Ideas from Barber Simulator** (Derek's playtest of 2026-11-24, detail in barber-simulator-screens-2026-11-24.md): the cat holds still and a drag on the right side of the screen turns the view while the other finger scrubs (an idea to test on a phone, not decided) [turn]; a free crate promised for tomorrow near the end of the first session if pets come from crates [return]; later tools handed out on level up. All six playtests are sorted in playtest-summary-2026-11-24.md.

**Prompts** (Derek, 2026-11-24): the big black default prompt buttons block the view on a phone, in My Pet Salon and in our own box pick up. Every prompt in the game is a small button in our own look.

**Out of scope for now:** trading, stealing, fighting, catching cats, paid luck, paid skips, buyable coins, typed cat names, rooms or tubs for avatars, walking into a neighbour's spa, more than one helper in the slice, the Cat Show event and any shared characters with Cat Cafe.
