# My Pet Salon gameplay video: notes, 2026-11-25 ET

Derek recorded 2 minutes 21 seconds of My Pet Salon on his PC and asked us to go through it. The video, six sheets of timed frames and two close ups are in the shared files under Coordinator/references/my-pet-salon-video-2026-11-25/. They stay out of the public repos (the frames show other players' names). Ideas only, nothing copied. Planning-Agent read one frame every 2 seconds and two step endings at 4 frames a second.

## Derek's words
"For the cleaning it looks like when they get it mostly cleaned it just shakes off the rest for foam etc or just calls it done that way people don't have to get it exactly perfect just most of the way. My suggestion is for our game we use a bar meter on the top that they fill when doing the task (trimming, rinsing, foaming these type of tasks only etc..) and if the bar hits full they just shake it off and go next task that way we can make bar hit full around 90% done so people don't have to get it perfect and waste time looking for last part... (it also included the day 2 pet that I got at the start but I like our pet idea of assigning it to workers/1 for the player etc (maybe can make a second pet slot for players/workers a paid pass as an idea)."

## His two calls
1. **A bar at the top for area steps.** Foaming, rinsing, trimming and steps like them get a bar that fills as the player works. It is full at about 90% done. Then the cat shakes off the rest and the next step starts. Gameplay-Engineer is building it. Steps with things to count (burrs, things to pull out) are not in his list, so our reading is that they show a count.
2. **Pets.** He likes our idea (one pet for the player and one per worker) better than My Pet Salon's day 2 pet. New idea, not decided: a second pet slot for players or workers as a paid pass. Plan page question `petpass`, our pick: a game pass for the player's own second slot, workers' second slots for coins.

## Timings
- A step takes about 5 seconds from its title to the next title. The real work inside it is 2 to 4 seconds.
- The rinse on wash 3: work starts at about 1:59.5 and the tool leaves at about 2:01.0, so about 1.5 seconds of spraying. The shave on wash 1: about 1.75 seconds of work.
- More steps does not mean a much longer wash: 5 steps took 34 seconds and 8 steps took 38. Their steps get shorter as the list gets longer.
- This matches what Derek wrote from memory on 2026-11-24 (4 to 6 seconds a step).

## How a step ends (the thing Derek noticed)
- Rinse, close up from 1:58.5: at the last frame of spraying about a third of the foam is still on the pup, by eye. The tool flies away, the pet shakes, the rest of the foam drops to the floor and "Squeaky clean!" shows. So their threshold looks nearer three quarters than 90%. Derek's 90% asks a little more of the player; it is one number to tune in dev.
- Shave, close up from 0:36: fur clumps fly off, the words Awesome, Halfway, Unstoppable and Almost show as progress passes marks, then "So sleek!". There is no bar on these steps. Progress is told by words and by the foam or fur that is left.
- The only bar in the wash is on the cuddles step: a pink heart bar under the step title that fills and then says Loved.

## Other things worth taking, in our own look
- The tool flies in from the tray when a step starts and back when it ends. Nobody picks a tool (Derek's call of 2026-11-24).
- A card at the left through the whole wash: the pet's name, rarity and kind, one line from its owner in quotes, "6 steps to go" and a tick list that fills in. Under it, the perfect streak and its bonus.
- The step title sits at the top centre with "Step 2 of 6" under it.
- A greeting at the start ("Hi" and the name) and "looks amazing" then "Saved" with stars at the end.
- A combo number that climbs with each spot hit, up to x40 in under 2 seconds.
- The camera moves in for small work: down to the paws for the trim, up to the face for the nose and the snack.
- The last step is always one nobody can fail (cuddles or a snack).
- A level up names what it unlocked and gives a flat pay rise.
- The guide tells you when you can afford the next thing.
- A server wide line when a very rare pet walks into someone's salon.
- Stars depend on speed (a speed bonus line, and the streak text says fast 5 star grooms). That answers Derek's question of 2026-11-24 about what loses a star.

## Not taken
- A 2x cash trial offer with a timer on the main screen.
- Daily quests that ask for invites and likes.
- Names, characters, art, sounds and wording stay ours.
