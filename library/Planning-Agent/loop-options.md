## The recommendation
Loop B, the cat collector, with the spa from today's draft as the home and a friend bonus. The cat is the prize and the hand wash is how the player wins it. Nothing is decided until Derek has played the spa games and read the doc.

## What the research found (read November 24, 2026 UTC, tracker numbers)
- Cleaning games peak 17 to 42 days after launch and then empty out. Five of six from June and July 2026 are at 3% of peak or less with 86 to 95% liked. Dig & Clean fell from 270,000 to 235 in about 100 days.
- Weekly updates did not save Clean all the Leaves. Offline earnings, quests and leaderboards did not save Dig & Clean.
- The one survivor, Clean Everything, has eight maps, a rebirth step, play with friends and sessions near 26 minutes. The faders ran 11 to 14 minutes.
- Median day 1 return is 4.3% for sessions under 3 minutes and 10.8% for sessions over 25 minutes (GameAnalytics Roblox report, a correlation). No source shows what timers, daily rewards or notifications do.
- Players under 13 cannot get personal notifications, only update notices.
- The games that last share rarity or trait multipliers, a collection index, a rebirth step, income while away and new things to collect on a fixed day each week.
- Waits in the hits start at seconds and reach hours only after the player is invested.

## The six proposed changes to gdd-draft.md (not applied yet)
1. The player chases the next cat, its hidden coat and a rare trait. Stations and rooms support that.
2. The coat and trait are a surprise on each wash. Chances are always on the board and the cats are free.
3. The 8 hour build moves from minute 6 to after about minute 15 or the first return, on its own slot.
4. The reason to return is empty index slots, a capped tip jar and a different visitor cat each day with no streak. The build notification is no longer the main hook.
5. A Grand Reopening: move to a bigger spa, keep every cat and gain a permanent tip bonus.
6. A small bonus per friend in the server at launch. Helping in a friend's spa comes later.

New target: a first session of 20 minutes or more, which needs about 12 cats, 3 kinds of dirt and 4 tools on day one.

## What the playtests must settle
Seconds to a pet in hand and the length of one wash. The minute it first felt finished. Whether the pet you got was ever a surprise. What would bring you back tomorrow. Whether other players mattered. Clean Everything is worth adding to the list.

## What nobody can tell us
No published number for any hook, no retention numbers for grooming or cleaning games and no known point where a wait pushes players away. We find out by running two versions behind live switches and reading Roblox's analytics.
