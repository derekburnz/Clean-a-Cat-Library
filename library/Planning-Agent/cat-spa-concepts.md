# Clean a Cat: name, how players get cats and the first 5 minutes (updated November 23, 2026 ET, Planning-Agent, status: name locked, design a draft until Derek's playtests are in)

Derek's goal: a first 5 minutes fun enough for a very low bounce rate when Home recommendations start, and a real reason to come back on day 2. Day 7 is handled by Derek's events. Derek is leaning to idea 1: run a cat spa with upgrades, where later upgrades take longer so players return. The research is in [Idea-Research/Roblox spa games and first minutes.md](Idea-Research/Roblox%20spa%20games%20and%20first%20minutes.md), the draft design is in [gdd-draft.md](gdd-draft.md) and the games to play are in [playtest-list.md](playtest-list.md).

## The name is locked: Clean a Cat
Locked on November 23, 2026, chosen over Clean the Cat. Why it is the right one of the two: "verb a thing" is the most common naming pattern on today's charts (18 of 154 top and rising games, including the number one), against 3 for "verb the thing". No Roblox game has the name. Clean is the hot verb right now. The words are plain enough for young players and players who do not speak English at home.

What the name asks of the game: a new player must be cleaning a cat within seconds of spawning. The first 5 minutes below already do that. The words cat spa can still go in the description and on the icon.

Name check: A web check on November 23, 2026 found no game, app or Roblox game named Clean a Cat. The closest trademark records are for CLEAN CAT on cleaning cloths (registered, class 21), kitty litter boxes (dead) and a degreaser (dead), none for games, software or toys. This was a web search of public trademark listings, not a search of the trademark office's own database or legal advice. Details are in Idea-Research/name-check-2026-11-22.md.

## How the name was chosen (the earlier top 3, kept for the record)
Based on today's chart names and a check of Roblox's own search on November 23, 2026.

**How today's games are named.** Of 154 games on today's top 100 and rising lists, 18 use the pattern "verb a thing", more than any other pattern. They include the number one game (Steal An Egg) plus Grow a Garden, Throw a Seed, Net a Fish!, Clean a Painting and Run a Restaurant!. Only 3 use "verb the thing" (Clean The Ballpit!), 4 use "verb thing for animals" and 2 are tycoon names. Short plain words win: steal, grow, throw, clean, sell.

1. **Wash a Cat.** The top pattern, the plainest words and exactly what happens in the first 15 seconds. Washing a cat is also funny on its own. One catch: a brand new game already has this exact name, with 1 player and 19 votes. Roblox allows two games with one name, so we can still use it, but we would share it.
2. **Clean a Cat.** Same pattern, nobody has it and clean is the hot verb right now (Clean The Ballpit!, Clean a Painting, Clean The Bugs). The safest pick if sharing a name bothers us.
3. **Cat Spa.** Nobody has it on Roblox, it matches the ad that did well and it describes the long game of building a spa. It is a thing name, not a verb name, so it is off today's trend. It works well as the second half of a title, for example Wash a Cat with Cat Spa in the description and on the icon.

**The rest of the list, in order:** Wash the Cat (free, but one letter away from Wash the Car, a game with about 2,900 players that tops that search), Scrub a Cat (free), Bathe a Cat (free), Fluff a Cat (free), Groom a Cat (free), Pamper a Cat (free). Scrub, bathe, groom and pamper are harder words for young players and players who do not speak English at home. Cat Spaw is dropped at Derek's request.

**Still owed before the icon is made:** one trademark lookup on the chosen name. A mobile game called Cat Spa exists (Seeplay), which matters only if Cat Spa is the title.

## How players get dirty cats
Derek's loop: get a dirty cat, clean it in different ways, then return it to its owner or put it on display for money and index progress. Catching cats did poorly in his last game.

| Option | How it works | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Customers bring them** (recommended) | People queue at the door holding a dirty cat. A board shows which cats can come and the chance of each. Stars from good service raise the chance of rarer cats | A cat is in the player's hands at second 0. No travel. The pace is easy to tune. Fits a spa | Can feel passive if the queue is ever empty, so there must always be a cat waiting |
| **B. Strays turn up at the back door** (recommended with A) | Now and then a muddy stray waits in a box. Nobody owns it, so once clean it stays in the display window. The first cat of the game is a stray | Gives the player a cat of their own inside the first minute, with no catching | Needs a rule for how often strays come |
| C. A booking book | Pick one of three appointment cards, including mystery carriers and VIPs booked for tomorrow | Adds choice and a day 2 reason | One more screen. Better added after the first build |
| D. A bus drops a crowd | Every ten minutes a bus brings customers for the whole street. Each player gets their own share | A shared moment that looks busy. Good for events | Not needed for the first build |
| E. Go out and find strays | Search a park or alley and carry cats back | Exploring | Catching did poorly in Derek's last game and it moves the fun away from the spawn |
| F. Buy mystery carriers with coins | An egg style purchase with earned coins | Familiar on Roblox | Looks like gambling to parents. Not at launch |

**Recommended: A plus B.** Customers bring most cats and pay when they get them back. Strays arrive now and then and stay on display, where they earn tips and fill the Resident side of the index. C and D can follow later. The playtests should confirm this: question 1 on the playtest list is how each rival game hands the player a pet.

## What the research means for the design
- **Slow spa and salon tycoons stay small on Roblox.** Nail Salon Tycoon, Beauty Salon Tycoon, My Salon Empire and the old pet salon tycoon all peaked between about 800 and 3K players, even with 87 to 94% liked. Placing stations and waiting is not what draws a crowd.
- **Makeover works when it is creative and other players see it.** Dress To Impress (about 10B visits, 90% liked) is the giant. A makeup contest wave launched in late September 2026 and reached 13K to 65K players per game, though most fell fast. Makeup Salon held best at about 4K with 97% liked.
- **Doing the treatment by hand gets saved and liked.** Skincare Spa (wash faces and do makeovers by hand, November 2025) has 23M visits and about 2M favorites, but only a handful of players now. Players wanted it and then ran out of reasons to return.
- **The closest rival is new and small.** My Pet Salon! (wash, brush, dry and cuddle) had about 188 players on November 22. No game named Cat Spa showed up in Roblox search.
- **The biggest games give you the item and the reward at once.** Throw Food For Animals starts you with a free carrot in hand, the first throw gets your first animal and it earns from the first minute. First upgrades cost very little ($30 and $50) and prices then climb about 5 to 27 times per step.
- **Day 2 hooks in the hits are things that finish while you are away.** Grow a Garden prints Grows Offline on its icon. Steal An Egg shows a Growing Eggs menu and money per day offline. Animals in Throw Food For Animals keep earning while you are gone.
- **Home now plays gameplay video.** Since July 29, 2026 a game's video autoplays on Home, and Roblox reports up to 39% more playtime for games with video in its tests (official). A muddy cat turning fluffy in five seconds is the clip.
- **What Roblox measures.** Home looks at the share who press play, the share who leave in under 60 seconds and at 61 to 180 seconds, days played (day 1, days 2 to 7 and days 8 to 28) and playtime (official Discovery docs). Roblox's own onboarding advice: reach the core game inside 5 minutes, no long tutorial, show goals clearly and end the intro on a moment of joy.
- **Benchmarks to aim at.** Roblox publishes no "good" day 1 number. Outside data from 2025 puts the median day 1 return at about 8 to 11% for games with sessions over 7 minutes and the top 5% near 25%. One developer thread calls 11% decent and 15% the goal. Our target: day 1 at 15% or better.
- **From outside Roblox.** Children's salon toys (Toca Hair Salon) end every makeover with a photo and a photo book. Project Makeover lives on the before and after moment. Reviewers of spa games complain about timers, pay walls and supply chores, never about the washing itself. So: keep the hands-on part, end on a photo and skip the chores.

## The game idea (Derek is leaning to this one)
- **What it is:** customers hand you dirty cats and strays turn up at the back door. You clean each cat by hand at the stations it needs, return customer cats for coins and stars and keep strays in the display window. Coins build and upgrade the spa.
- **Sub-genre:** hands-on care game with a collection and timed builds. Not a slow tycoon and not a contest.
- **Day 2 hook:** builds that take longer and longer. The first builds take seconds, and an 8 hour build is on offer in the first session. One notification a day can tell players aged 13 and over that a build is finished. Younger players cannot get personal notifications, so the game shows them what is coming before they leave.
- **Day 7:** Derek's events. A Cat Show weekend with a theme and voting is the natural first one.
- **Set aside:** a pure makeover contest (first reward comes 5 to 6 minutes in and those games fall fast) and a stray rescue town (the washing sits too far from the spawn).

## The first 5 minutes
| Time | What the player does | What they get | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Spawns inside their own spa at the tub. A muddy stray is in it and a sponge is in hand. One arrow says Scrub | Mud flies off under the finger | The hits put the item in hand at spawn |
| 0:15 | Finishes the scrub | The real coat shows and the cat shakes off | The surprise |
| 0:25 | Three brush swipes, then picks one of three bows | The cat puffs up and purrs | A first creative choice |
| 0:40 | Photo moment | A before and after card and 10 coins. The stray hops into the display window and is yours | The moment of joy. A cat of your own inside the first minute |
| 1:00 | The first customer walks in with a dirty cat. The chance board lights up | A goal: Cat Index 1 of 10 | A clear next goal before the 60 second mark |
| 2:00 | Buys Bubble Soap (30 coins, instant) | A wider, faster scrub | A cheap first upgrade, as in the hits |
| 3:00 | Starts the first build, the Brush Table upgrade (10 seconds) | The builder kitten hammers and a countdown shows | Teaches builds with almost no wait, before the 180 second mark |
| 4:00 | A customer's cat needs a blow dry, which is still locked | A reason to build the dryer (150 coins, 1 minute) | The next goal comes from a customer, not a menu |
| 5:00 | The dryer is done and cats pay more | The builder shows the next build at 10 minutes and, later in the session, the first 8 hour build | Sets up the reason to return |

Rules for these 5 minutes: no forced tutorial, no pop-ups, no shop prompt, no like request and no text walls. Arrows and one goal at a time. The spa room loads first so the wait is short. The spa never closes during a build.

## Reasons to come back on day 2 (all fair for young players)
1. **A long build finishes while the player is away** and opens with a small celebration on return. It waits as long as needed and nothing is lost by being late.
2. **Tips while away.** Window cats earn while the player is gone, up to a cap, shown as a jar to empty.
3. **Tomorrow's VIP customer** is shown on the board as a silhouette.
4. **One notification a day at most**, when the longest build finishes, for players aged 13 and over who opted in.
Not used: streaks that reset, countdowns to buy, paid skips and anything truly limited. Roblox's notification rules ban false time pressure.

## What the research could not show
- No guide gives second by second timings for any game's first minutes. Only a playtest can.
- No day 1 retention number exists for any spa or salon game, and no source proves that offline timers raise day 1 return. The hits use them, which is the best evidence there is.
- Player counts are third-party tracker readings and differ between trackers.
