# Playtest list for Clean a Cat (updated November 23, 2026 ET, Planning-Agent)

Claude cannot play Roblox games, and no written guide gives timings for the first minutes. Ten minutes in each game answers what the research could not. Play as a brand new player, on a phone if possible, with a timer running. Player counts are from Roblox's own search on the morning of November 23 and move all day.

## What to write down for every game
1. **How do you get a pet?** Handed to you, a customer brings it, you find it, you buy it or you hatch it. How many seconds does it take?
2. Seconds from spawn until you first did something fun.
3. Is the washing or grooming done by hand (drag, scrub, hold) or by pressing one button?
4. Seconds until the first reward and the minute of your first upgrade.
5. Any build or wait timers, and how the wait felt.
6. What the game told you to do next at minute 1, minute 3 and minute 5.
7. The first pop-up, shop prompt or Robux offer and when it came.
8. The moment you got bored or confused, if any.
9. What it does well, what we should do better and whether anything would make you open it again tomorrow.
A voice memo or a few typed lines per game is plenty.

## If there is time
- [Capybara Cleaning Tycoon](https://www.roblox.com/games/16225881494): an older animal wash tycoon with about 52K votes at 94% liked and 5 players now. Look for what the tycoon format does to the pace.
- [Winter Wonderland Pet Salon Tycoon](https://www.roblox.com/games/8897454112): the old pet salon tycoon (29M visits, peak about 2.9K in 2022). Look for how much is waiting instead of doing.
- Adopt Me! (the Salon and the bath task): how small the washing is in the biggest pet game.
- Steal An Egg: only the first 5 minutes, for the arrows and the menu that shows what grows while you are away.

## What we do with the notes
The answers to question 1 settle how our players get their cats. The timings set the targets for our first 5 minutes. The rest goes into [gdd-draft.md](gdd-draft.md), which stays a draft until these notes are in.
