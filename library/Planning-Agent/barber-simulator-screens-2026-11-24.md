## What we do not take, and why
- Pets from a roulette sold only for Robux (29, 69 and 199 for 1, 3 and 10 spins). It does show the chances, as Roblox requires. Derek looked for a free way to get a pet and found only the egg promised for tomorrow. Ours: every pet can be earned and crates are never sold for Robux.
- A second pet slot for Robux and every pet at once for 1,699 Robux (10, 11). Ours: a worker's slot unlocks with coins.
- A starter pack with a 48 hour countdown and a random pet in it marked 1 in 95 (12). A paid random item with a countdown is two of our rules broken at once.
- A watch an ad button for double cash (9), and a Robux skip on the next building sign (2).
- Sweeping the floor between customers by holding a button while a bar fills (6, 7). It is a wait with a bar, like the horse game's wash. A chore between cats would have to be real hand work or not exist.
- A seven day reward calendar (9). Our pick stays no streak. The one thing we take from it is a pet on day 2.
- A badge note that covers the bottom right during the tutorial (6).
