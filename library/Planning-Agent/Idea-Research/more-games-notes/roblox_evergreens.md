## 2. Adopt Me! (pets, home, trading)

### Takeaway
Adopt Me started as family roleplay (adopting children) and grew once pets, with ages and rarity, were added in summer 2019. A pet that you care for, grow and trade is the whole loop. It also has the clearest record of what goes wrong for young players: scams.

### Cited findings
1. First minutes today: choose the Baby role (double Bucks), equip a starter Dog or Cat, do the orange task icons (food, sleep, play), save 350 Bucks for a Cracked Egg (about 20 to 30 minutes), log in daily. Goals are shown as orange icons and a money number. [opinion, Earnaldo guide, Apr 11 2026, updated Jul 5 2026](https://earnaldo.com/blog/adopt-me-beginner-guide)
2. Core action: tasks keep a pet's needs full; pets grow through stages; four full-grown of one type make a Neon, four Neon make a Mega Neon. Five rarity classes. [opinion, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adopt_Me!). Retired event pets act as trade currency. [opinion, RoWatcher, Aug 6 2025](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-top-roblox-games-keep-players-coming-back-retention-mechanics-that-work)
3. Owns and shows: pets, houses, furniture; a Money Tree for passive Bucks (8 Bucks every 5 minutes while online). Other players see pets walking with you and visit homes (described on the Roblox page: "adopt and raise pets, trade and collect legendary pets, build homes"). [official, Roblox page, read 2026-11-24](https://www.roblox.com/games/920587237/Adopt-Me)
4. Social and safety: trading is the main social act. Trade licenses were required above rare in Nov 2020, trade slots rose 4 to 9 to 18, trade history and "unfair trade" warnings were added. Scams grew from 2021, mainly "trust trades" aimed at young players. [opinion, Wikipedia]. Today's guide still says trust trades are the most common scam. [opinion, Earnaldo above]
5. Update habit: events and partner tie-ins (Pet Rock event Apr 2020 reached 680,000 players); major updates triple player numbers. [opinion, Wikipedia]. Developers said in a Roblox Medium post they expected 250K at most for the Pets update and used a countdown banner, pet GIFs and YouTubers; they advise mobile optimization and features "for all ages." [developer statement, Roblox dev blog, undated, likely 2019](https://medium.com/roblox-developer/outstanding-achievement-developers-discuss-update-that-got-adopt-me-over-400k-concurrent-players-f3592d06a88)
6. Complaints: scams and trading pressure (above). Also "Adopt Me attracts many curious first-timers who leave after one session." [opinion, RoWatcher Aug 2025]. The RoWatcher benchmark in the same article: D1 above 20% is good, top games 30 to 40%, below 15% suggests onboarding trouble. Not Roblox data.

### Inferences (mine)
- The orange task icons are a kid-readable goal system: look for the icon, press it, get paid. The care loop is the reason pets matter.
- Trading is the single biggest safety cost of an otherwise gentle game.

### Gaps
- No D1 or D7 for Adopt Me. No official statement on onboarding found. The Medium post is undated.

## 3. Bee Swarm Simulator and Pet Simulator 99 (collect and grind)

### Takeaway
Both keep players with a pile of layers: rare things, mutations, timers, and a regular content drop. Bee Swarm is a solo developer's slow, polished game; Pet Sim 99 is a weekly-update team game with heavy monetization and a bad record on pay-to-win.

### Cited findings
- Bee Swarm: 50+ bee types in six rarity tiers, mutations, gifted variants; timed challenges (Ant, Stick Bug, King Beetle) reset on timers; its complexity "filters out casual visitors in the first hour." [opinion, RoWatcher, Aug 6 2025](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-top-roblox-games-keep-players-coming-back-retention-mechanics-that-work)
- Bee Swarm updates arrive mainly with seasonal events (Beesmas, Winter 2024 to 2025). Jan 2024 added a 36-hive social space and player trading; 2025 added voice chat, and an Offline Voucher for up to 24 hours of passive progress. Developer said he treats it like "a work of art" and will not change the vision to match community demands. Complaints: slow single-developer pace, endgame repetition, exploiters. [opinion/developer statement via RoWatcher, dated Jan 22 2025 but text reaches 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/onett-s-seven-year-marathon-how-one-dev-keeps-bee-swarm-simulator-s-65k-players-coming-back)
- Bee Swarm launched Mar 23 2018, peak about 259K to 265K, 4.2B visits, 95.6% likes. [tracker]
- Pet Sim 99: tap breakables, earn currency, hatch eggs for random pets, rebirth for permanent multipliers; over 2,000 pets; rarity Normal to Gargantuan; event pets become unobtainable. About 8,075 Robux for all passes. Critics call the eggs loot boxes; Screenwise flagged it. [opinion, RoWatcher monetization piece, metadata Jan 25 2025, text to Mar 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/pet-simulator-99-inside-roblox-s-most-aggressive-monetization-machine)
- Pet Sim 99 updates weekly on Saturdays (Update 74 by Mar 2026). The studio switched to every two weeks at Update 4 because weekly "squeezed development time" and led to rushed changes, then returned to weekly at Update 9. [developer statement via Hold To Reset, undated](https://holdtoreset.com/pet-simulator-99-updates-will-release-more-often)
- Trading: Pet Sim 99 has a Trading Plaza and auction board; a real-money market exists off-platform against Roblox rules. [opinion, same RoWatcher piece]

### Inferences (mine)
- Both place a forever list of "not yet owned" things in front of the player. Bee Swarm's offline voucher and Pet Sim's rebirth are the same trick: progress even when away, or a reset that keeps a permanent bonus.
- The Pet Sim 99 developers' own words show weekly cadence has a cost to quality.

### Gaps
- No first-5-minutes source for either. No D1 data.

## 4. Home and outfit games: Bloxburg, Royale High, Dress To Impress, Brookhaven

### Takeaway
These hold players by letting them own and show a thing: a built house, an outfit, a dorm, a free house and car. Each took a different path to easy first minutes, and the one with the hardest start (Bloxburg) is the one that shrank most after going free.

### Cited findings
- Bloxburg: launched Nov 2014. A 25 Robux paywall was removed Jun 2024; peak 352K after free, low about 6,900 to 19,000 (two sources disagree), 900K+ at the Version 1.0.0 map revamp in Jun 2025, 14K to 16K "now" in the RoWatcher article, 17K on the API today. New players face needs meters, 14 jobs, eight skills, and build learning; the house is built on a 30 x 30 plot. [opinion, RoWatcher x2](https://rowatcher.com/news/welcome-to-bloxburg-at-14k-players-can-roblox-s-building-icon-stage-a-comeback), [Bloxburg vs Brookhaven, Sep 3 2025](https://rowatcher.com/news/bloxburg-vs-brookhaven-two-visions-of-roblox-roleplay). Complaints: stale roleplay content, intimidating build depth, weak retention, spikes fade in weeks. 2026 roadmap: pets, elder age group. Updates faster since late 2025 (winter, IKEA, Valentine's, March).
- Brookhaven: every player gets a house and a car at once; no tutorial, meters or grind; 115+ properties, 190+ vehicles, 80+ cosmetic jobs; weekly Friday updates; owners can ban visitors from their own home. Common Sense Media 8+; critics note a mostly under-12 audience and chat concerns. [opinion, RoWatcher, Dec 12 2024 metadata, text to 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/what-is-brookhaven-rp-why-400k-players-choose-roblox-s-biggest-game). Voldex's chief product officer said Brookhaven works through social roleplay, not a core loop, and that "a third of our returning players are just socializing." [developer statement, Game Developer, May 1 2024](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/roblox-devs-say-socialization-is-what-makes-the-creator-led-sandbox-tick)
- Royale High: launched Apr 2017. Seasonal halo events (Glitterfrost, Royalloween, Everfriend), each with a limited realm and a token quest; trading unlocks at level 75 with a 10 second cooldown on offer changes; Global Player Realms (Dec 30 2025) lets players publish dorms others can visit. Complaint: pay-walled toy-line items seen as exclusionary. [opinion, RoWatcher, undated](https://rowatcher.com/news/royale-high-how-one-teen-developer-built-roblox-s-most-loyal-fanbase)
- Dress To Impress: launched Nov 11 2023 by a teen developer; lobby, theme, six minutes to dress, runway, others rate 1 to 5 stars; peak 1.5M; updates are collaborations every few months (Brat Aug 2024, Wicked Nov 2025); modes Duos and Freeplay (no timers or votes). Complaints: spite voting, VIP pay-to-win worries, cliques. [opinion, RoWatcher, metadata Apr 1 2025, text to Apr 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/dress-to-impress-how-a-teen-developer-s-fashion-game-hit-9-7-billion-visits)

### Inferences (mine)
- Bloxburg's data (paywall gone, peak, then low) and its onboarding load point to "hard start, loyal fans" being a smaller audience, not a failure. It still lasts 12 years.
- Dress To Impress shows a one-act loop (dress for 6 minutes, be rated) can hold a huge audience when the act is social and short. Its Freeplay mode is a safety valve for the people hurt by ratings.

### Gaps
- No first-minutes detail for Royale High or DTI. No safety limits found beyond trade level and cooldown for Royale High.

## 5. Fisch and the older tycoons

### Takeaway
Fisch is the closest "2024 game that held 12+ months": a cast and catch loop with many goals. The old tycoons and builders (Theme Park Tycoon 2, Build A Boat, Pizza Place) last on a different thing: a creative or shared task, with small modern audiences.

### Cited findings
- Fisch: cast, shake, minigame, sell, buy better rods, reach more areas; goals shown are perfect catches, quests, a Bestiary, and level 200 for the Second Sea. Complaints named by the guide: FPS drops on mobile, overpriced bait crates, Second Sea resets rod power and currency. [opinion, Destructoid, undated](https://www.destructoid.com/fisch-roblox-beginners-guide/)
- Fisch developer (Roblox DevForum, Nov 22 2024): "retention of players relies on the ability to play a game in multiple ways"; built the game with several end goals (collect fish, chase best stats, compete); each fish has about 25 mutations; built in about four months after playing about twenty fishing games; he prefers quality over frequency of updates; first event FischFright put it on the front page; he advises early moderators and Discord. [developer statement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-woozynate-makes-a-splash-with-fisch/3269481)
- Fisch tracker: peak 600K in Dec 2024, 59K or 34K today (page conflicts), 90% likes. [tracker]
- Build A Boat For Treasure: launched Nov 2016, 93.4% likes, last updated about Mar 19 2026; it is a build-and-sail game with no collecting loop I could find. [tracker]
- Theme Park Tycoon 2: 18K peak Jul 2025, about 2K now, 89% likes, a park you build and share. [tracker]
- Work at a Pizza Place (2008) and Tower Defense Simulator pages gave no usable tracker numbers. [tracker, failed]

### Inferences (mine)
- Fisch had several goals at once because the developer said so on the record. This matches Roblox's D7 advice (short and long goals).
- The old tycoons keep tiny audiences with high likes, which suggests a quiet loyal base, not growth.

### Gaps
- No developer interview for Theme Park Tycoon 2, Build A Boat, Pizza Place, Tower Defense Simulator, MeepCity, Livetopia.

## 6. What do evergreens share that one-month trend games lack?

### Takeaway
From the sources: (1) a collection or a place that grows over months, (2) goals at three time scales, (3) a dated content rhythm tied to real seasons, (4) players who have a reason to meet each other, and (5) a clear first minute without a wall. The 2026 trend games (see why_cleaning_games_fade.md) have a loop that ends and clones that arrive faster than content.

### Cited findings
- Roblox [official]: D1 retention follows the core loop and first-time experience; D7 follows progression (short and long goals, variety); D30 follows an "ending system" and a cadence of small updates every 2 to 4 weeks and big ones every 2 to 3 months. [Creator Docs, retention page, no date shown, read 2026-11-24](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)
- Survivors (RoWatcher, Apr 9 2026): weekly or biweekly updates, quarterly holiday events, cosmetic monetization, active developer in Discord, a community group ready, and 2 to 3 months of stored content before seeking visibility. Simulators hit "core loop exhaustion within 5 to 10 hours." [opinion]
- Casual sims share low-barrier entry, idle progression, collection and rarity, and chat-while-playing. [opinion, RoWatcher, date unclear](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-simulation-takeover-why-casual-sims-rule-roblox-in-2026)
- Trend counter-evidence from our earlier files: clean games peaked 17 to 42 days after launch and fell 97 to 99%; the one that held had many maps, rebirth and party play.

### Inferences (mine)
- Concrete differences: the evergreens' main goal is not "finish the map." Adopt Me's pets age and are never done, Bee Swarm's bees and mutations take months, Bloxburg's house is never finished, Royale High has a yearly calendar. The trend games finish.
- Evergreens also had a human reason to return (events, trading, visiting). Where trading was the reason, scams followed.

### Gaps
- The RoWatcher survivor list has no method note. Treat it as a hypothesis.

## 7. Which start simple and stay readable for a 7 year old?

### Takeaway
Brookhaven, Adopt Me and Dress To Impress, in that order. They share one press-this-icon, see-the-result act with no menu maze. Bee Swarm and Bloxburg are not 7-year-old games on day one.

### Cited findings
- Brookhaven: house and car at once, no tutorial or meters. [opinion, RoWatcher above]
- Adopt Me: orange icons for pet needs, a visible money number, one currency that is free. [opinion, Earnaldo above]
- DTI: one timer, one theme, one task. [opinion, RoWatcher above]
- Bee Swarm "filters out casual visitors in the first hour"; Bloxburg has meters, jobs and skills. [opinion, RoWatcher above]

### Inferences (mine)
- How they stay readable: one icon type for "do this next", one visible currency, no text-heavy menu to start, and the deep layers (mutations, Neon ageing, build mode) hidden until asked for.

### Gaps
- No usability test or age data from any source. "7 year old" is my reading of the first minutes.

## 8. Owning and decorating a place versus collecting things

### Takeaway
The sources show both and show them together: Adopt Me pairs pets with homes; Royale High pairs outfits with dorm realms; Bloxburg is mostly place. Place ownership gives a reason to visit and be visited; collecting gives a reason to chase. No source ranks one against the other for retention.

### Cited findings
- Bloxburg: house earned by effort. Brookhaven: house handed out. Adopt Me: pets plus furniture. Royale High: Global Player Realms for visitable dorms, added Dec 30 2025. [opinion, sources above]
- Brookhaven owners can ban visitors from their own house. [opinion, RoWatcher]
- Brookhaven has weak structure and "aimless once novelty wears off" per critics; Bloxburg had slow roleplay content for years. [opinion]

### Inferences (mine)
- A place makes the collection visible to others and gives the collection a home. Collection without a place becomes a list (the Pet Sim loop); a place without a goal becomes aimless (the Brookhaven critique).

### Gaps
- No retention number split by "has a house" versus "has pets."

## 9. What Roblox's own pages recommend for the first session

### Takeaway
Roblox says: get to fun in about five minutes, keep the tutorial short, reward the first loop, show what repeating it gives, and end onboarding on a moment of joy with goals visible.

### Cited findings
- Creator Docs retention page (no date shown, read 2026-11-24) [official]: brief tutorial or tooltips, rewarding moment after the first core loop, show progress from repeating, track each step's completion, fun within about 5 minutes, bring in social features and goals.
- Creator Docs onboarding page (no date shown, read 2026-11-24) [official]: teach controls and the core loop, keep early XP thresholds low, balance starter rewards, A/B test tutorial steps, show short, mid and long goals in the world or UI, end with a deliberate moment of joy such as rewards, animations or celebratory effects. [create.roblox.com onboarding](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding)
- Roblox newsroom, Jun 15 2026 [official]: Home recommendations now use day 1, days 2 to 7 and days 8 to 28 retention, plus play-through, session quality and spend; Roblox says thumbnails alone over-rewarded games that did not deliver long-term value. It gave no direct first-session advice. [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)
- A Roblox-hosted developer essay (silky_dev, undated): show the fun at once, keep tutorials short and skippable, attach achievements to first actions, show what unlocks next. [developer statement](https://medium.com/roblox-developer/from-the-devs-the-importance-of-first-impressions-on-player-retention-by-silky-dev-e63a90291279)

### Inferences (mine)
- The docs have no age-specific advice; for 7 year olds the "short tutorial" rule is probably a floor, not a ceiling.

### Gaps
- The Developer Conference talks were not found or read. The GDC 2024 Gamefam talk "The 30-Second Roblox Rule" has only a schedule page, no content. [GDC schedule](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/the-30-second-roblox-rule-how-to-hook-the-next-generation-of-gamers/899126)

## What this means for a hand-wash cat spa (my own inference, not sourced)

1. The washing act should be the one "press the thing, see the result" verb a 7 year old can learn in a minute, shown by one icon type like Adopt Me's task icons. Everything else stays hidden until the player asks.
2. Roblox's docs say reward the first loop and end onboarding on a joy moment. The first cat to leave clean could trigger a short celebration and the first "keep this cat" choice.
3. The evergreens do not finish. The trend cleaning games did. The cat should be the thing that does not finish: cats with ages, coats, moods or rare traits, a cat index with visible empty slots, and a spa that grows. This is how Adopt Me and Bee Swarm stay open ended without trading.
4. Own a place and own a collection together. The spa is the home; the cats kept are the pets. Let visitors see the spa and the kept cats (like Adopt Me homes or Royale High dorms) with owner-set visiting limits (Brookhaven lets owners ban visitors).
5. Skip trading. Adopt Me's own history (trust-trade scams of young players) is the clearest evidence in this file. Use gifts or no exchange; a young-audience review is the studio's call.
6. Plan a dated rhythm: a small cat or spa-item drop every 2 to 4 weeks (Roblox docs) and a holiday theme every quarter (survivor list, opinion). Pet Sim 99's team said weekly updates harmed quality; do not promise a pace you cannot hold.
7. Add an ending system for after the spa is full: new cat breeds, rooms, a prestige step like Pet Sim's rebirth, offline earnings like Bee Swarm's voucher.
8. Watch the three retention windows in Roblox's June 2026 change (D1, D2 to 7, D8 to 28). Weeks 2 to 4 are where clone waves lose Home placement.
9. This is a hypothesis: none of these games is a hand-wash care loop, so no source proves the cat spa will behave like them.
