# Roblox service games: customers come to the player's shop (read Nov 24, 2026)

Method. All numbers read Nov 24, 2026. Tags: [official] Roblox page or Roblox search API or DevForum; [tracker] Rotrends (rotrends.com/game/UNIVERSEID); [opinion] guide, wiki or fan page. Rotrends gives peak size but usually NOT the peak date. Live player counts from the search API move a lot between reads (RT3 read 5,482 and 8,028 in two calls the same hour; "Own a Cafe" read 23, 268 and 1,157), so treat "now" as plus or minus 50%. I could not play any game. No video or review text was readable, so section 7 is thin. Not repeated here: the "Clean the X" template games and the pet-salon census (see why_cleaning_games_fade.md and roblox_animal_spa_grooming_care.md). "Games outside Roblox" were not in this task's topic and are not covered.

### Inferences (mine)
- Time at 10%+ of peak: RT3 about 8% of peak but after 22 months (it peaked Aug 2025, 3 months after paid alpha; it still ranks Top Earning #254). Run a Restaurant is at 11% after 8 months, with 98.7% likes. Own a Grocery Store 15% after 4 months. Compare the Clean-the-X games: 1 to 3% after 4 months. Service games decay slower, but they still decay about 90% from peak.
- Session length separates survivors from fades: 19 to 29 minutes for RT2, RT3, Run a Restaurant, Pet Store Tycoon, Anomaly Cafe; 2 to 11 minutes for the dead pet or salon tycoons (Pet Parlor 2.4, My Salon Empire 8.4, Nail Salon 11.1). This is a correlation on about 15 games.
- Likes do not predict survival: Own a Restaurant (97.4%), Run a Hotel (98.2%), Own a Cafe (96.5%) all faded.

## 2. Restaurant Tycoon 3 and 2 (Ultraw)

### Takeaway
RT3 is the long-lived reference. It was a paid early-access game first, then free, then fed a content drop nearly every week for 18 months, including customer types, staff features and an offline earnings update. The player seats and cooks; staff take the same jobs; the player is the manager.

### Cited findings
- First minutes: claim a plot, pick a starter layout and a cuisine, name the restaurant, then enter a restaurant that is already set up ([Deltia's Gaming guide, undated](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=232199) [opinion]). You click a customer group to seat it, then cook by simple mini-tasks (chop, fry). The Sportskeeda beginner guide says guests "are assigned to you" and you seat them at empty tables, then take the order ([Sportskeeda, undated](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/restaurant-tycoon-3-a-beginner-s-guide) [opinion]). Time to first customer: not stated anywhere.
- Arrival and patience: customers arrive at tables, groups can be of different sizes (a wiki update says one waiter can serve a 16-customer table, Jul 16, 2025). Customers show emoji reactions; unhappy ones signal slow service, limited menu or poor layout (Deltia, [opinion]). I found no hard patience timer or "customer leaves" rule for RT3. A "dine and dash" customer was added May 1, 2026. Max customers in the world was raised from 200 to 400 on Jul 5, 2025 ([RT3 Update History wiki](https://rt3.fandom.com/wiki/Update_History) [opinion]).
- Staff: Deltia says hire a waiter and a chef once you finish a few orders. Staff gain levels; Jun 16, 2025 level 4 workers walk 50% faster; staff can be assigned to floors (Nov 7, 2025), break mode (Jul 10, 2026), staff-only doors and elevators (Feb 20, 2026) ([DevForum bulletin thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/restaurant-tycoon-3/3670704) [official]). 16 staff slots, 8 waiters and 8 chefs (Sportskeeda, [opinion]).
- Special customers (page updated Nov 17, 2026, [Bloxodes RT3 customers](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/restaurant-tycoon-3/customers) [opinion]): Regular (always); Kids (1 in 3, needs Kids Menu); Rude (1 in 200, 75 to 100 Cash for calming it); VIP (1 in 250, large tip); Birthday (needs 1 star, 1 in 365, always tips); Food Critic (needs 2 stars, gives a 0 to 5 rating, pays 200 for 4+); Celebrity (needs 3 stars, raises spawn rate, 1 in 800); a 1 in 20,000 "Rig" gives 10 Diamonds. So rare customers are gated by a star level, which is how a rare tier stays out of the first session.
- Rude customer: stays at the table until the player finishes a 3-option talk (2 to 4 rounds, no timer); polite choices pay up to 100, rude choice at least 25, no tip ([ofzenandcomputing guide, Nov 2025](https://ofzenandcomputing.com/how-to-deal-with-rude-customers-in-restaurant-tycoon-3/) [opinion], date internally inconsistent).
- Retention features with dates ([DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/restaurant-tycoon-3/3670704) [official]): Alpha May 2, 2025 (wiki says May 10), Beta Jun 24, 2025, public free release Aug 20, 2025 (wiki); Rush Hour Aug 18, 2025; Celebrity customers Sep 12, 2025; Franchising Sep 26, 2025; Drive Thru Nov 1, 2025; Winter event Dec 2025; Lunar New Year Feb 13, 2026; Demolition mode Jan 2, 2026; Earn Offline Aug 28, 2026; Improved Objectives Sep 4, 2026; Halloween Oct 16, 2026. Objective cash rewards raised to $75 to $175 (Aug 11, 2025).
- RT3 was Top Paid Access #2 from May to Aug 22, 2025 ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/7094518649)), then went free and peaked in August 2025.
- RT2: workers start slow and level up; the player "does everything yourself" early, then manages; employees do not earn the skill points the player earns when cooking by hand ([Xbox Wire, Aug 13, 2020](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2020/08/13/feed-your-creativity-in-restaurant-tycoon-2-on-roblox/) [opinion]); a "patience meter" that drops when service is slow ([restauranttycoon2worker.pages.dev, undated](https://restauranttycoon2worker.pages.dev/) [opinion]).

### Inferences (mine)
- Rare customers in RT3 are about odds (1 in 250 to 1 in 800) plus star gates, with no paid way to raise odds that I saw (a "3x Customer Luck" boost exists on the page). A Roblox pass for luck would need odds shown, per our paid-random rule.
- Player-does-it-by-hand work earning extra points that staff do not earn is a clean way to keep the player busy after hiring.
- The number of weekly-ish drops (about 40 dated changes on one thread in 18 months) matters more than any one feature.

### Gaps
- No RT3 patience numbers; no first-5-minute timing; no reviews or forum complaints read.

## 3. Run a Restaurant!, My Restaurant!, Own a Grocery Store

### Takeaway
The 2026 style is a bigger sandbox: expand floors, buy appliances, spawn rate rises with seats and floors, workers are cheap multipliers. Customers arrive faster as the player grows; the player never picks one at a time from a visible queue.

### Cited findings
- Run a Restaurant!: launched Apr 1, 2026, 6 players per server; pillars cooking, decorating, farming, ranching; Fishing update about Jul 14, 2026 (Update 10); update 11 being built in early Aug ([earnaldo wiki page](https://earnaldo.com/blog/run-a-restaurant) [opinion]). Rank chart: Up-and-Coming #9 and Top Trending #45 in the first 2 weeks (May 21 to Jun 6) ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/9970645639)). Last update Sep 10, 2026 (two months old), still 3.7K now.
- Arrival (unofficial wiki, last verified Jul 14, 2026 [opinion]): "Each new floor significantly reduces customer arrival time," about 0.5 to 2 seconds between customers at ten floors; jewelry cases raise celebrity spawns; thunderstorm "raincoat" customers pay 50% more; Taco Tuesday special customer; no patience meter or queue described; backlogs cause unhappy customers. Workers: waiters seat, take orders, deliver; chefs cook; cleaners clear plates; staff level up; suggested 1 waiter per 4 tables, 1 chef per stove. Tip jars give passive income; Deluxe Tip Jar pass; High Tech stove and dishwasher cost Robux ([runarestaurant.pages.dev](https://runarestaurant.pages.dev/) [opinion]).
- My Restaurant!: customers spawn every 8 to 18 seconds on floor 1 and every 2 seconds on floor 10; reaching "Rookie" (25,000 customers served) raises regular and VIP spawn ([ProGameGuides, undated](https://progameguides.com/roblox/how-to-get-more-customers-in-my-restaurant-roblox/) [opinion]). VIP orders the most expensive dish and pays more; free chance 0.1%, VIP pass (400 Robux) 0.2%; Royal Table adds 10%, Royal Chair 25%; Royal Bundle 1,999 Robux; payout 150 to 162,000 Cash ([ProGameGuides, undated](https://progameguides.com/roblox/how-to-get-vip-customers-in-my-restaurant-roblox/) [opinion]). Odds are sold per Robux there; that is the pattern our policy forbids without shown odds.
- Own a Grocery Store: 13.3K peak, 9 passes, 13 products, 757K favorites on 16.8M visits (36.9 per 1,000 visits, a very high ratio, [Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10553704684)). Gameplay not read.
- My Restaurant!: 889.8M visits, 142 developer products, still about 200 to 280 players five years after launch ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/1434220026)).

### Inferences (mine)
- Run a Restaurant! sells 65 developer products against 4 passes; the trade is many small buys, probably timers and cash packs. Not verified.
- Spawn-rate scaling by upgrades is the only "more customers" mechanism found. None of the guides describe a visible queue with a player choice.

### Gaps
- First minutes, patience, offline earnings: not found for Run a Restaurant! or My Restaurant!.

## 4. Pet Store Tycoon (2026), pet and salon games, and My Pet Salon!

### Takeaway
Pet Store Tycoon (2026) reached 40.6K and holds about 3.6K with 29-minute sessions, but it is not a service-queue game: it is care plus shop expansion. My Pet Salon! is the closest match to your idea and it is small (peak 452), young (Oct 25) and has no monetization.

### Cited findings
- Pet Store Tycoon (already arrived studios): Roblox page lists feeding, cleaning, playing with pets, stocking shelves, serving customers, unlocking animals, decorating, co-op with friends. It says nothing about workers, VIP or rare pets ([Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/118845698633260) [official]). A codes article (Oct 5, 2026, updated Nov 13) says you spend coins on feeding, toys, and a yard for exercise ([Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/pet-store-tycoon-codes) [opinion]). Charts: Up-and-Coming #51 and Top Trending #77 in Oct 2026; Top Earning #99 Oct 5 to Nov 24 ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10282097014)). Last update Sep 17; 7-day average CCU down 24%.
- My Pet Salon!: pet grooming with mini-games for shampoo, brush, comb, blow-dry, cuddle; combos earn bigger rewards; leveling unlocks tools, moves, pets and "rarer customers with special looks"; pets include puppies, cats, bunnies, turtles, hamsters; codes ([Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/134135989047078) [official]). Peak 452, ~222 now, 86.6% liked ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10772985378) [tracker]; [search API](https://apis.roblox.com/search-api/omni-search?searchQuery=pet%20salon&sessionId=1&pageType=all) [official]: 836 up, 129 down). Whether customers come one or many at a time: not documented.
- Nail Salon Tycoon: 10.2K peak, 94.1% liked, 11.1 min sessions, 6.5M favorites (98 per 1,000 visits), declining 23% a week ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/7812773438)).
- My Salon Empire: salon with cutting, braiding, brushing mini-games; hire a stylist; 8.4 min sessions ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10092472345); earlier note).
- Dead or near dead pet shops: Pet Parlor Tycoon peak 138, Run a Cat Cafe! peak 66, Pet Store Tycoon (2021 id 1840326425) ~5 players with 116.7K up and 22.3K down votes (search API, [official]). The Roblox search for "pet salon" shows about 20 near-identical "Run a X" and "My X!" titles with 0 to 10 players each.

### Inferences (mine)
- "Run a" and "My" titled games from late October are a copy wave with names like yours; most have under 20 votes. The first-mover had 452 peak, so the wave did not feed this one.
- 836 up votes against a 452 peak suggests the audience is not tiny but it has not been pushed by Roblox's charts.

## 5. Roleplay and job games (Pizza Place, Maple Hospital, Bloxburg, Cook Burgers)

### Takeaway
These last for years because other players are the customers and co-workers, not because of shop loops. Do not copy their lifetime; copy their social jobs.

### Cited findings
- Work at a Pizza Place: six dependent jobs (manager, cashier, cook, boxer, delivery driver, supplier). Pay is a paycheck spent on houses and pets; late food is rejected and the pizzeria loses money ([Sportskeeda, undated](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/work-pizza-place-a-definitive-guide) [opinion]). 168K peak, 5.6B visits, 3.0K now, 14.7 min sessions, created 2008 ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/47545)).
- Maple Hospital: roles medic, nurse, patient (a player role) ([Sportskeeda, undated](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/roblox-maple-hospital-how-to-play-features-and-more) [opinion]). 52.9K peak, 1.9B visits, 4.9K now, 86.5% liked, 14.2 min sessions; Top Earning #477 ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/3308103880)).
- Bloxburg: 592.5K peak, 17.3K now, 25.6 min sessions ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/88070565)). I did not find hairdresser job detail.
- Cook Burgers: 9.8K peak, 82.1% liked, now ~410, last update May 2026.
- Anomaly Cafe (alpha, 2026): the player takes the order at the register, cooks by timed mini-games, serves the booth matching a ticket number, then washes the plate; burnt food summons monsters; end-of-shift report shows customers served and cash earned ([All Things How, undated](https://allthings.how/roblox-anomaly-cafe-how-to-take-orders-and-serve-food/) [opinion]). The "[CH2] Cook Pizzas [Anomaly]" game (Oct 23, 2026) hit 24K peak with only 6.6 min sessions and a 73% rating ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10772627574)): a threat twist grows fast but plays short.

### Inferences (mine)

## 6. What the long-lived ones share (cited facts, then inference)

### Takeaway
Seven things appear in every service game that kept 3K+ players for more than 4 months.

### Cited findings that support it
- Long sessions: RT3 26.0, Run a Restaurant 29.4, Pet Store Tycoon 29.4, Anomaly Cafe 24.7, Bloxburg 25.6 minutes ([Rotrends, see table](#1-numbers-table)).
- Frequent dated updates: RT3 about every 1 to 2 weeks across 2025 and 2026 ([DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/restaurant-tycoon-3/3670704) [official]); Run a Restaurant! 10+ numbered updates in 3.5 months (earnaldo [opinion]). The ones that stopped (Own a Cafe last Sep 23; Run a Hotel -40% a week) fell.
- Customer variety unlocked by progress: RT3 star-gated critic, birthday, celebrity, kids, rude; My Restaurant! VIP, celebrity.
- Growth levers are floors, appliances and staff, not clicks: Run a Restaurant! and My Restaurant! spawn rates scale with floors.
- Co-op: 6-player servers, "run it with friends" lines in the descriptions (Pet Store Tycoon, Run a Restaurant!).
- Offline or objectives layer added later: RT3 Earn Offline Aug 28, 2026, objectives Aug 2025 and Sep 2026.
- A paid or hyped launch phase: RT3 Top Paid Access #2 before going free.

### Inferences (mine)
- Updates and content need to be a stream, not a one-time pack.
- Staff are framed as "legs" that carry food while the player manages and cooks.
- Rare customers are a spice tier on top of a base flow of many regular customers, never the base.

### Gaps
- No example of a Roblox grooming game that satisfies these; the only candidate (My Pet Salon!) is 4 weeks old.

## 8. What this means for a hand-wash cat spa (MY INFERENCE, not sourced)

### Takeaway
The data supports the owner's shape (several customers waiting, helpers that are slower, rare VIP cats, optional impatience) as a fit with how the surviving service games work. It does not prove the shape. Nothing I read shows a service game with a visible waiting line where the player picks who is next.

### Inferences (mine; confidence in brackets)
1. Several customers at once is the norm in the big games (spawn every 2 to 18 seconds, tables for groups). One at a time is the odd choice. [high that many at once is standard; medium that choice-of-next adds fun]
2. Helpers are "legs", the player is the manager. In RT2 and RT3, staff start slow and level; the player earns extra by doing the work by hand. For a hand-wash game, helpers should take simple jobs (rinse, dry) or whole easy cats slowly, with the player still best at hard or VIP cats. [medium]
3. Rare customers: use a star or level gate and slim odds (1 in 250 or 500 style) plus a visible "VIP is here" alert (My Restaurant! shows a bottom-of-screen alert). They should pay for a longer job. Do not sell luck for Robux without showing odds. [medium]
4. Patience: I found no hard patience data in any Roblox service game. RT2 has a "patience meter". RT3 uses emoji reactions and star ratings, and "dine and dash" came 15 months after launch. A soft version is safer for a young audience: a mood icon that drops, a smaller tip or star, and a gentle exit, not a punishing loss. [low to medium, no proof either way]
5. Sessions should aim for 20+ minutes. The faded salon games had 2 to 11 minutes. [medium]
6. Scale the flow with upgrades (more waiting spots, faster washing), as Run a Restaurant! does with floors. [medium]
7. Plan a weekly stream of new cat types, rooms or customers from the start; RT3 and Run a Restaurant! both kept this up. [medium]
8. Offline income arrived late (RT3, Aug 2026) and has no early proof of keeping players; skip it in v1. [low]
9. Do not copy "Run a ..." or "My ..." names; the copy wave is already large. [high]

### Gaps
- The owner's own playtest of My Pet Salon!, an actual first-five-minutes read of RT3 and Run a Restaurant!, and any real player complaint text.
