# What is working on Roblox right now (snapshot, November 22, 2026 ET)

Gathered by Planning-Agent for the idea shortlist. Roblox numbers move fast, so re-check before any big decision.

**How to read this.** Roblox's own site and APIs could not be reached from the cloud thread, so live numbers come from third-party trackers. The trackers took their readings at different times and disagree by up to two times on the same game. Treat every player count as a rough size, not an exact figure. Each fact is marked official (Roblox said it, as reported), tracker (third-party count) or opinion (a writer's view).

## The platform
- Daily players were down 9% year on year to 138M in Q3 2026 and hours were down 15%. Official, as reported by GGRecon, Nov 2, 2026. Roblox is smaller than a year ago, so new games fight over fewer hours.
- New games still break through: 28% of the hours in the top 100 games came from games launched in the previous 90 days (August 2026). Official, told at Roblox's developer conference, reported by IBTimes UK, Sep 13, 2026.
- Total creator payouts were about $1.7B for the 12 months to June 30, 2026, but the median creator earned about $1,500. Official, same source. Most games earn very little.
- Recent hits started tiny. Grow a Garden's first version was reportedly built in about three days by one teenager and later grown by a team. Reported, same source.
- Players by age: 35% under 13, 38% aged 13 to 17, 27% adults. Official, as cited by Naavik, Mar 1, 2026.

## The games that matter most for us
**Throw Food For Animals** (launched Sep 18, 2026, all ages).
- Numbers: 417M visits, 2.4M peak players, 94% liked from 232K votes, average session 18 minutes, number one trending Oct 26 to Nov 21. Tracker: rotrends.com, read Nov 22, 2026.
- Loop: throw a food item, an animal comes to it and is yours, place it on your plot where it earns money, buy better food which draws rarer animals, train to throw further into new areas, fill an animal index for bonuses. Guides: bloxodes.com (Nov 1 and Nov 17, 2026) and ofzenandcomputing.com (Nov 12, 2026).
- First minute: you start with a free carrot already in hand. One throw gets your first animal.
- Money: two passes, 2x Cash at 99 Robux and 2x Training at 149 Robux. Source: bloxodes wiki.
- None of the guides mention stealing or fighting between players.
- Copies are already charting: Shoot Arrows For Animals!, Fish For Sea Animals and Throw a Seed.

**Steal An Egg** (the current number one).
- Loop: take eggs, hatch pets that earn money, upgrade your base, steal eggs from other players. Source: player.one, Sep 9, 2026.
- Numbers: about 8.35B visits per a third-party ranking, a peak near 9.9M players in September, average session about 15 minutes.
- Roblox took it offline for about three and a half hours on Aug 24, 2026 and gave no public reason. When it came back, an in-game feed of short AI clips was gone. Source: allthings.how, Aug 25, 2026. A later report says Roblox clamped down on that kind of scrolling feed; that page could not be read, so the detail is unverified.
- Its older cousin Steal a Brainrot peaked at 25.4M players in October 2025 and sits near 50K now. Trend games fall as fast as they rise.

**Clean The Ballpit!** (rising, rank 41 on Statroblox with a 22% daily gain on Nov 22, 2026).
- Loop: scoop up scattered balls, sell a full bag, buy a bigger bag and better cleaning tools up to a vacuum, clear the house room by room. Source: roonby.com, Nov 18, 2026.
- It shows a plain cleaning verb can chart. One data point only.

**My Cat Box** (2020, small). A cat collecting game that added a cat wash side feature in December 2020: cats got muddy and a car wash style station cleaned them for currency. Source: press release on gamedeveloper.com, Dec 2020. It had 5.2M visits at the time. Washing was a side feature there, not the game.

## What the winners share
- **The name is the verb.** Throw Food For Animals, Steal An Egg, Clean The Ballpit!, Sell Fries!, Break and Steal an Egg. A player knows what they will do before pressing play.
- **The fun thing happens in the first seconds** with an item already in hand.
- **One shell underneath:** do the verb, get a creature or item, place it at your base, it earns money, buy a better tool, reach the next area, fill an index.
- **Surprise without a shop.** Which animal comes, which egg hatches. The surprise is earned by playing.
- **Friends can see each other.** Bases sit side by side on a small map.
- **Small first versions.** The loop was proven first and content came later.

## Trends we should not chase
- **Stealing from other players.** It is the biggest trend, but our own rules keep stealing out, the space is packed with copies, and the leader has already had a takedown scare.
- **Horror** (DONT SPLASH, 99 Nights, Dandy's World, Forsaken). Big, but wrong for a young cozy audience.
- **Shooters, anime fighters and sports.** Hard netcode, older audience and strong leaders.
- **Meme names** such as brainrot. They age in weeks and are not our IP.

## Opinion pieces (treat as ideas only)
- RoWatcher says simulators, tycoons and pet collecting are crowded and that sports, racing and open world action are under-served. No sourced revenue split.
- RoWatcher estimates top earners make money mostly from cosmetics and that Rivals, Blox Fruits, Brookhaven and Adopt Me lead. These are third-party estimates; Roblox does not publish per-game revenue.

## Gaps in this snapshot
- Could not read Roblox's own charts, search or game pages from here, so no check of how many small washing or pet care games already exist. That check is needed before a name or icon is made.
- Tracker counts are unverified and differ by snapshot time.
- No data on failed games of each kind (survivorship). The fall of Steal a Brainrot is the only decline measured here.
