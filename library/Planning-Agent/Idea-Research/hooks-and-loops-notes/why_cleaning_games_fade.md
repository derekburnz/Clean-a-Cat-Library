# Why cleaning games fade on Roblox (read Nov 24, 2026)

Method note. All numbers were read on Nov 24, 2026 (UTC) unless stated. Tags: [official] Roblox page or Roblox newsroom, [tracker] Rolimons, RoWatcher, Rotrends, RBLXSTAT, [opinion] guide or press. Live counts and votes come from Roblox's own search API (tagged [official]). Trackers disagree on peaks: Rotrends peaks run low for three games (Leaves, Wash the House, Superstore), so I use Rolimons (exact day) or RoWatcher (month) first and say so. Rolimons "current" figures are cached and sometimes old. Dates to peak count from the created date; where the tracker gives only a month the day count is approximate (~).

## 2. The five biggest: hook, loop after minute 5, updates, why players left

### Takeaway
All five share one hook (a huge messy space, visible progress, a cash-and-upgrade loop) and the same long-run loop: pick up, carry, deliver, buy a bigger bag. Updates were frequent but mostly added maps and passes, and the big games still fell 97%+. Direct "why I quit" text was not retrievable; reasons below are inferred from structure and developer statements.

### Cited findings
- Clean all the Leaves (peak 152K). Hook: mundane rake-and-upgrade loop plus a creepy mystery and a secret ending at 100%. After minute 5: rake, bag, dump at vent, buy faster tools and bigger bags, unlock map areas ([Player.One, Aug 28](https://www.player.one/clean-all-leaves-suddenly-one-robloxs-biggest-games-why-are-players-raking-leaves-163958), [opinion]). Updates: secret ending and badges Jul 30, 2x passes Aug 1, Mansion map about Aug 15, 599 Robux pass Aug 21, Update 5 with Ranked Mode Sep 12 ([Earnaldo log](https://earnaldo.com/blog/clean-all-the-leaves-update-log), [opinion]); Chapter 2 maps Oct 31 and Nov 7, Teams and Spooky updates in October ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/clean-all-the-leaves-events-schedule/), [opinion]). Still updated weekly-ish, now ~3.3K. Likes 86.2%, the lowest of the big five, with 22.5K dislikes ([search API](https://apis.roblox.com/search-api/omni-search?searchQuery=clean&sessionId=1&pageType=all)).
- Clean The Library (83.6K). Hook: shelve scattered books; inspired by a Steam game; developer told Kotaku success came partly from being free and made for playing with friends ([Player.One, Aug 24](https://www.player.one/roblox-adaptations-are-turning-steams-biggest-indie-hits-free-play-competitors-163898), [opinion]). Loop: sort thousands of books, buy upgrades (extra books, speed, jump, magnet), time-trial leaderboard. 10 passes, 48 products; last update Nov 23 ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10226701629), [tracker]). Now ~2%.
- Wash The House (68.6K). Hook: wash a dirty house; reached Up-and-Coming #1 Aug 3-9. After minute 5: incremental upgrades; average session only 14 minutes. 5 passes, 36 products; last update Sep 14, two months before now ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10405010493), [tracker]). Now ~0.6%. This is the clearest case of a stopped update cadence followed by collapse.
- Clean the Supermarket (62.6K). Hook: sort 1,000+ items into color-coded aisles. Loop: carry items, earn cash, spend on permanent Keys (carry capacity, speed) or Abilities; passes 29 to 399 Robux (+50 Max Holding 399, Robot Vacuum 299, Equip +1 Pet 299). Updates: eight dated drops from Jun 27 to Aug 5 (hard mode map, 1v1 versus, new mode, map plus abilities), then the wiki lists none after Aug 5 although a tracker shows an update around Sep 26 ([Bloxodes wiki](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/clean-the-supermarket), [opinion]). Now ~0.7%.
- Clean the Squishies (53.3K). Few details found. Visits 92.5M, favorites 2.5M, last update Aug 22 ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10384841418), [tracker]). Average session 11.4 minutes, the shortest of the five. Now ~3%.
- Reasons players stop, as stated by someone who ran a similar game: a developer of an unnamed cleaning game wrote that day-1 retention was good but day-7 retention was a problem, players "felt finished after the first stage", the first level's ~200 items lacked variety, the game never reached the Roblox homepage at low concurrency, the thumbnail click rate fell to 1.2%, and the genre was "saturated" and the release "late" ([VK Roblox News post, Sep 8, translated](https://m.vk.ru/@roblox__good-razrabotchik-roblox-podelilsya-opytom-rosta-igry-uborschika), [opinion]). It is not one of the five, but it is the only first-hand account found.
- Copycats: clones of Clean the Library include supermarket, toy store, museum and bakery versions ([Player.One](https://www.player.one/roblox-adaptations-are-turning-steams-biggest-indie-hits-free-play-competitors-163898), [opinion]). The search API shows 40 results for "clean", almost all one template.

### Inferences (mine)
- Not found in any source I could read: Reddit, DevForum or in-game comments saying why people left. Treat "grind", "pay wall" and "bugs" as unproven. What the evidence does support: finish-and-leave (progress ends), short sessions, and many copies.
- Leaves is the counter-example to "just add updates": it shipped a map or mode nearly weekly for four months and is still at ~2% of peak.

### Gaps
- No reviews or comments read. YouTube and TikTok blocked reading comments. Squishies loop and update log not found.

## 3. Which held 30% or more for 3+ months

### Takeaway
Of the games measured, only [HOUSE] Clean Everything clearly did, and it did so by growing, not by holding: peak day 103, still about 60% of that peak five months after launch. Clean The Superstore may qualify (15% of peak on Rolimons, 44% on Rotrends) so I rate it unclear.

### Cited findings
- Clean Everything: created Jun 30, peak 14K on Oct 11, now 8.6-8.8K, 7-day average up 24.8%, 30-day change -1.6% (stable by RoWatcher's rule), 95.5% liked, average session 25.9 minutes (97th percentile), 18 passes, 69 products ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10421901778/house-clean-everything/dying), [Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10421901778), [tracker]). Roblox page: eight maps (toy store, pet store, bakery, restaurant, school, cinema, haunted mansion, house), upgrades, abilities, rebirth for permanent bonuses, solo or party play, +10% walk speed per friend ([Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/140576663170542), [official]). Rotrends keeps older names such as "[RESTAURANT] Clean Everything", which suggests the title is changed with each new map (inference).
- Clean The Superstore: peak 12.7K Jul 26, now ~2.0K; 25.8 minute sessions, last update Sep 9 ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/80000420704526), [Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10418117926)). Rolimons and RoWatcher agree on 13K; Rotrends says 4.4K.
- Capybara Onsen, the animal spa game, is at ~10% of peak (section 5).

### Inferences (mine)
- Structural differences of Clean Everything versus the faders: many maps (a reason to return), rebirth (a reset loop), party and friend bonuses (multiplayer), and a slower start (it was small when the July clones were huge, so it did not have a spike to fall from). It has no offline earning, base building or collection that I could find. The 30%-for-3-months claim rests on trackers with no daily curve; I cannot show it never dipped below 30%.
- Sessions of 25+ minutes (Clean Everything, Superstore, Library 29 min) versus 11 to 14 minutes (Squishies, Wash the House, Wash the Car) is the one pattern I would watch, but only two data points sit on the survivor side.

### Gaps
- No early daily numbers for Clean Everything, so whether it ever ran below 30% is unknown. Rolimons would not load it.

## 4. How the hits got players; size of the trend now

### Takeaway
Chart placement did the work: nearly every big game has an Up-and-Coming or Trending rank in the first two weeks, which is Roblox's own recommendation surface. Press and YouTube covered the games after they were already big. In November 2026 the "clean" trend is not falling; a new wave is on, but it has the same shape as the old one.

### Cited findings
- Roblox [official, Jun 2026]: Home "Recommended For You" now uses a 28-day retention view (day 1, days 2-7, days 8-28), plus play-through, session quality, spend and co-play. Roblox says short-term engagement over-rewarded eye-catching thumbnails ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)).
- Chart ranks: Wash The House Up-and-Coming #1 Aug 3-9; Leaves Up-and-Coming #1 Aug 13-27; Library Up-and-Coming #8 Jun 16-Jul 3; Ballpit now Trending in Simulation #5, Top Trending #8, Up-and-Coming #9 ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10405010493), [Rotrends Leaves](https://rotrends.com/game/10539411000), [Rotrends Ballpit](https://rotrends.com/game/10774051115), [tracker]).
- Launch size: Clean the Supermarket debuted around 20K; RoWatcher's digest says either strong algorithm placement or an audience carried over from an earlier title by the same group ([RoWatcher digest, Jun 27](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-weekly-digest-june-27-2026-liminality-leads-a-wild-week), [opinion]). Clean The Bugs is made by Retro Library, the Clean The Library studio; Bugs promised "7,500 likes = new map" in its description ([Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/126810262789538), [official]).
- YouTube: a Sunny and Melon video "Clean ALL The Leaves OR YOU..." dated Aug 25 ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HYSGgpt5ly0)); several Supermarket videos and a TikTok tag #cleanroblox exist, but view counts were not readable (bot-blocked). I cannot say if creators started the waves or followed them.
- Trend now (live, Nov 24): the 40 results for "clean" total ~69.8K players; Ballpit (26.1K) plus Bugs (9.2K) are about half of that ([search API](https://apis.roblox.com/search-api/omni-search?searchQuery=clean&sessionId=1&pageType=all)). RBLXSTAT: Ballpit's 7-day average was up 3,237% in the week to Nov 19 and the game is #47 platform-wide ([RBLXSTAT](https://rblxstat.com/blog/roblox-updates-and-records-2026-11-19), [Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10774051115)). Search "organize" showed Sort the Plushies (485) and Lucky Scoop Order (6.2K) as a lateral branch.

### Inferences (mine)
- The trend is in a second wave, not decline. The first wave's top 5 (Library, Supermarket, Squishies, Wash The House, Leaves: about 420K combined peaks) is now about 7K; the second wave's top 2 are 35K and still rising. If the first wave is a guide, expect Ballpit and Bugs to peak by about day 30-40, which is roughly Dec 1 to Dec 10, then fall.
- Because Roblox now weighs day 8-28 retention, a game with weak weeks 2 to 4 will lose recommendation exactly when its clones arrive.

### Gaps
- Home-recommendation share, ad spend and creator deals are not public; I found no developer statement about it for any top five game.

## 5. Capybara Onsen

### Takeaway
Capybara Onsen is a small, polished idle-merge game with a 97.5% like rate, 200K favorites and about 330 players, roughly 10% of its 3.2K peak in August. Its likes and favorites show people who try it love it; its loop and update record explain why it stays at a few hundred.

### Cited findings
- Dojo Empire; created Jul 15, 2026; peak 3.2K in Aug 2026; now 324 to 335; 12.2M visits; 58.8K votes (97.5%); 200.5K favorites; 7-day average 319 (+4.3%); average session 14.6 minutes; 3 passes, 14 products; Top Earning #462 for Aug 18-Oct 3 ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10506291803), [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10506291803/dying), [tracker]).
- Last update: Sep 4 (guide), Oct 1 (Rotrends) or Aug 25 (an API reading I got, which also showed 3.1M visits and 1,584 players, probably a stale cached snapshot). The Roblox page has no changelog and says "Shutdown = Update!" ([Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/120336108521610), [official]).
- What the player does: buy capybaras, collect Yuzu from water lanes, deposit it for cash, merge matching capybaras to higher tiers (Yuzu, Chicken, Ramen), Lucky Boxes give extra capybaras, auto-collect settings, up to 6 players per server but each plays their own plot, no trading found, offline earning not addressed, no events found, tutorial under two minutes ([allthings.how guide](https://allthings.how/capybara-onsen-roblox-how-to-play-full-guide/), [opinion]).
- Copycats already exist: Capybara Spa Tycoon, My Capybara Onsen, Clean Capybaras, Capybara Cleaning Tycoon and many more in the same search ([search API](https://apis.roblox.com/search-api/omni-search?searchQuery=capybara%20onsen&sessionId=1&pageType=all)).

### Inferences (mine)
- Why a few hundred: it is an idle merge loop with no front-line social layer, no visible events and stale updates; the capybara trend wave (capybara games all over the search) came and left; and the "spa" is just a theme on a merge game, so it has no hands-on care to share or film. High favorites per 1,000 visits (18.9, +67% this week) suggest a core audience that comes back, which fits the steady 300s.
- The trackers give no daily curve, so I cannot show whether it dipped below 330 before.

### Gaps
- No Reddit, DevForum or video commentary found. Peak day, update dates and any content drops are unconfirmed.

## 6. My read and the first-month plan

### Takeaway
It is mainly a loop problem inside a trend wave. The template (pick up, carry, upgrade, one map) is finished in 3 to 5 weeks, and the template is copied faster than a small studio can add content. Content updates alone did not save Leaves; the only survivor has many maps, a reset loop and party play.

### Cited findings
See sections 1 to 4: 17 to 42 days to peak, 97%+ falls, Leaves updated about weekly and still at 2%, Clean Everything at 60% with eight maps and rebirth.

### Inferences (mine; rough confidence medium)
- Loop problem (main cause): one-verb loop ends; developer's own "players felt finished after the first stage".
- Trend problem (second): each wave is replaced by new clones, and Roblox's 28-day retention signal removes weak games from Home.
- Content problem (third): maps help only when they extend a loop with a reason to return (rebirth, party).
- First-month needs for Clean a Cat: (1) at least 5 distinct cats or spaces on day 1 and a cadence of one new cat type or room per week announced in the description; (2) a reason to return that is not "more of the same": a collection of cats with fur and care variety, plus a rebirth or promotion step; (3) co-op with friends (Library and Ballpit both stress it); (4) a first-session goal that ends with a return hook, with day 2 to 7 and 8 to 28 rewards; (5) sessions above 20 minutes; (6) a thumbnail that matches the real game (Roblox says it down-weights click-bait); (7) no pay wall before day 7, because young players leave when blocked. Items 1 to 4 are my inference from the survivor, not proven causes.

### Gaps
- No A/B evidence for any of these. Needs a playtest of Clean Everything and Ballpit, and a daily curve from a tracker with history.
