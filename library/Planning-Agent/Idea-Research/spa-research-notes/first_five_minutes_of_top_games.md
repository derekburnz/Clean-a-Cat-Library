# First five minutes and core loops of top Roblox games (Nov 2026)

Method note: only guide pages that could be fetched are used. Many sites (progameguides, games.gg, destructoid, pockettactics, theclick, Roonby Ballpit page) failed to fetch. Search returned only link lists. No YouTube transcripts or Roblox game pages were read. Coverage is therefore partial: 9 of the 20 listed games have at least some first-minute detail; 11 have none. Nothing below is from memory unless marked "inferred".

## Per-game first five minutes and core loops

### Takeaway
Guides agree most games hand the player one verb and one item immediately (carrot, egg, seed, pickaxe, starter pet) and show the first upgrade as a cheap purchase within minutes. Full second-by-second timing is almost never given by any source ("not found" for exact seconds in every game).

### Cited Findings

**Steal An Egg** (guides Aug to Oct 2026)
- Spawn and guidance: Red arrow trail leads from spawn across the SAFE ZONE line to the Forest. On-screen objectives drive each step ("Go to your Pen!", "Place the egg", "Hatch"). A red "1" badge appears on the Egg and Index buttons. Author played Oct 12-13, 2026, pre-Halloween update. Tutorial is on-screen objectives, not a cutscene. — [allthings.how, updated Oct 23, 2026](https://allthings.how/steal-an-egg-how-to-steal-place-and-hatch-your-first-egg/)
- Timeline: claim 10,000 speed from a FREE! chest (requires favoriting the game and joining the community); follow arrow to Forest; hold E about 1.5 s on the egg nearest the safe line beside a sleeping guard; run back; egg goes to hotbar slot 3; place in pen by clicking ground; press E to hatch into a Common Golden Chicken earning $2/s; place the pet. Starting speed 10, hotbar slot 2 holds 3 traps (not usable in safe zone). — same source
- First purchase: pen sign shows "Unlock: $1K"; once over $1,000 the tutorial prompts "Upgrade your Pen!", which adds a treadmill that increases speed. Author's balance fell $1,454 to $479 (about $975, which the author notes does not match the $1K sign). Next pen upgrade listed at $1M. Author's balance reached $654 within a few minutes. — same source
- Another guide gives the first egg as Chicken: $1/s, 10 s grow time, hatch reward +420 Speed and $100. Grey Trail costs $100 (1.5x speed), Green Trail $5k (2x), Sci-Fi Treadmill $15K. Base treadmill is free (+2 speed per step). Lake needs 900 Speed, Desert about 10K. Guardian of Forest is a Chicken, Lake a Swan. Nests reset every 5 minutes. — [rblxguide, Oct 11, 2026](https://rblxguide.com/games/steal-an-egg/updates/beginner-guide). Note the two guides disagree on first pet income ($2/s vs $1/s), possibly different versions or egg variants.
- Biome Speed thresholds: Lake 900, Desert 10K, Jungle 40K, Snow 170K. Other players can knock you off course with bats or bear traps. Spawn timer five minutes plus a 13 s map closure (Aug 15, 2026 snapshot). — [bloxodes, Aug 17/25, 2026](https://bloxodes.com/articles/steal-an-egg-beginners-guide)
- Roonby: first garden upgrade $1,000 unlocks treadmill; Index on left side shows pet rewards; Fuse Machine needs 3 same pets. — [Roonby, Aug 13, 2026](https://roonby.com/2026/08/13/steal-an-egg-beginner-guide-tips-and-tricks-from-noob-to-pro/)
- Core loop (one line): steal an egg, run it home, hatch a pet that pays cash per second, spend on speed and pen upgrades to reach farther biomes with better eggs. Session loop: nests reset every 5 minutes. (summarized from sources above)
- Shop/pass prompt: Robux items exist but guide says none are needed to progress. — rblxguide above. Pass-prompt timing: not found.

**Break and Steal an Egg** (Oct 8, 2026)
- First minutes: train Speed until you pass the first gate, find a nearby egg your starting Pickaxe can break, break it, pick up the animal, carry it home avoiding guards, confirm it is in the Pen, earn cash, spend on weakest stat (Pickaxe, Speed or Trail). Starting Pickaxe, Speed and Pen exist; no inventory list, no prices, no tutorial described, other players not mentioned. — [Roonby, Oct 8, 2026](https://roonby.com/2026/10/08/break-and-steal-an-egg-progression-guide-tips-and-tricks/)

**Grow a Garden** (undated page)
- Start with 20 Cash, enough for Carrot seeds from the Seed Vendor; garden has 3 rows by 2 columns; equip seed and click fertile spot; harvest with E; carrots are single-use; sell to Merchant (heavier sells for more); loop repeats. No in-game tutorial described. Map lets you see other gardens and teleport between Garden, Seed Vendor, Merchant. Robux can buy any seed any time. Next goals: expensive seeds, Water Can, Basic Sprinkler, Gold/Rainbow variants, weather events. — [Sportskeeda, undated](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/grow-garden-a-beginner-s-guide). Page date not found, so may predate recent updates.
- Core loop: buy seed, plant, wait, harvest, sell, buy better seed.

**Steal a Brainrot** (undated page)
- Start with very little money; buy a Common character from the center ramp area; it walks to your base automatically and earns money; steal from owners who are away and unlocked; bat is in first slot; Base Lockdown button gives 60 s laser protection; Rebirth starts at $1M. No tutorial mentioned. A signboard on the ramp shows next Mythic/Legendary spawn. — [ProGameGuides, undated](https://progameguides.com/guides/complete-steal-a-brainrot-beginners-guide/)

**Throw Food For Animals** (Nov 11, 2026; snapshot already has the carrot and passes, not repeated)
- Tutorial is first step and unlocks a pull-up bar at your base. Free carrot starts a throwing minigame (meter 0 to 20, stop near 20 for distance). Animal picks up the food, you bring it to your base pen where it earns per second. Pull-up bar and training raise Throw Power. Prices: Apple $30, Banana $150, Toast (first Tier 2) $4K, Orange $20K. Base-size button by base entrance adds 2 animal spaces each. Zones: Forest, Savanna, Desert, Jungle, Ocean. Starting cash and first-animal income not stated. — [Roonby, Nov 11, 2026](https://roonby.com/2026/11/11/throw-food-for-animals-beginner-guide-best-upgrades-food-and-money-farming-tips/amp/)
- Price climb visible in the source: $30, $150, $4K, $20K across four foods (inferred: roughly 5x to 27x per step).

**99 Nights in the Forest** (Jun 20, 2025, old)
- No tutorial; "doesn't hold your hand". Build a fire, find a Good Axe by looting, a deer-like creature stalks at night and a flashlight drives it off; missing-poster at base gives clues leading to a cave. Enemies scale with player count. Upgrading Crafting Bench unlocks a new bed that multiplies days passed per night. — [Roonby, Jun 20, 2025](https://roonby.com/2025/06/20/ultimate-beginner-guide-99-nights-in-the-forest-tips-tricks/). Over 17 months old; treat as possibly outdated.

**Sell Fries!** (wiki published Oct 18, 2026; updated Nov 17 and 21, 2026)
- Grab potatoes, peel, fry, hand fries to customers at service window; customers wait about 50 s. Upgrades: Conveyor 60 Cash (recommended first), Potato Robots 1,000, Vacuum 1,000 (8 potatoes per trip), Golden Vacuum 9,800 Cash or 54 Gems. Free class spin first; paid roll 25 Gems. From 8 p.m. anomalies can occur; surviving a night gives a perk pick next morning. Game created Sep 21, 2026. Christmas event Nov 29, 2026. Tutorial not mentioned; spawn not covered. — [Bloxodes wiki](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/sell-fries)
- Price climb: 60, then 1,000 (about 17x), then about 9,800 (about 10x).

**Adopt Me!** (Jul 5, 2026 byline)
- Game asks to pick Baby or Parent (guide says Baby, double Bucks from tasks). Starter pet Dog or Cat; equip immediately; needs show as orange task icons left of screen; best-paying tasks Hair Salon, Pizza Party, Pool Party, Camp; teleport buttons on left; save 350 Bucks for Cracked Egg (guide estimates 20-30 minutes). Egg prices: Cracked 350, Pet 600, Limited 750, Royal 1,450. Trust trades are most common scam. — [Earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/adopt-me-beginner-guide). A care-task loop (feed, bathe, play, walk) is a spa-adjacent loop; another page lists same tasks but is a low-quality generic page. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/how-get-started-adopt-me-roblox-a-step-by-step-guide)

**Dress To Impress** (Sep 16, 2026)
- Round: theme given, dressing area, build core outfit, add details, final minute fixes, runway poses, voting gives Stars. Normal-server voting unpredictable; "farming servers" agree to give five stars. Lobby, first rewards, timers and shop prompts not covered. — [LDPlayer, Sep 16, 2026](https://www.ldplayer.net/blog/roblox-dress-to-impress-beginners-guide.html)

**Fisch** (undated)
- Equip rod, hold mouse to cast, click Shake icons, hold/release to keep fish icon in the reel bar; optional bait from shore NPC; sell at merchant on hill ("I'd like to sell this"); rods are main upgrade, no forced quests. Spawn, starter rod, prices, tutorial and date not found. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/roblox-fisch-a-beginner-s-guide)

- Steal An Egg's free 10,000 speed chest is a stated first reward but requires leaving to favorite and join a community, which is a friction point (inferred).

## Cross-game patterns

### Takeaway
Among documented games the shared features are an item or starter currency already available, a base or pen, an objective prompt or arrow, and a cheap early upgrade. Long cutscenes are not described anywhere in the sources.

### Cited Findings
- Item in hand or starter funds: free carrot (Throw Food), 20 Cash (Grow a Garden), starter pet (Adopt Me), starting Pickaxe/Speed/Pen (Break and Steal an Egg), 10,000 free speed (Steal An Egg). Sources as above.
- Base assigned: Pen (Steal An Egg, Break and Steal an Egg), garden (Grow a Garden), base pen (Throw Food), base (Brainrot). Sources as above.
- Guidance style: Steal An Egg red arrows plus objective text; Throw Food tutorial unlocks pull-up bar; 99 Nights and Fisch have no forced guidance; Grow a Garden and Brainrot guides describe no tutorial. Sources as above.
- Other players in first minutes: stealing and traps (Steal An Egg, Brainrot), viewing gardens (Grow a Garden), trading (Adopt Me), voting (Dress To Impress), server-scaled enemies (99 Nights).
- Cutscene or long tutorial: none described in any fetched source. No source says a tutorial hurts retention.

### Inferences
- Price steps in first minutes: Throw Food $30, $150, $4K, $20K; Sell Fries 60, 1K, 9.8K; Steal An Egg $100, $1K, $5K, $15K, then $1M pen level. Roughly 5x to 17x per tier, and the first buy sits within the first few minutes of earning (inferred; no source gives time to first upgrade except Adopt Me egg at 20-30 minutes).

### Gaps
- Time to first upgrade is not stated by any source except Adopt Me (20-30 minutes to first egg).
- No source evidence on tutorial effect on bounce rate.
