## Per-game offline progress, daily systems, unfinished goals, scheduled content, social pulls

### Takeaway
Best-evidenced hooks: Adopt Me (daily login Bucks cycle, Star Reward Coins with no streak needed, 2x pet aging weekends), Grow a Garden (global 5-minute shop restocks, weekly updates), 99 Nights (3 daily quests, 24-hour refresh), Dress To Impress (timed events, advent calendar with catch-up). Many others returned nothing readable.

### Cited Findings

**Adopt Me**
- [fan wiki, undated] Daily Reward pays Bucks 25 / 50 / 100 / 200 on days 1-4, a gift on day 5, cycle resets after every 5th day; day 30 gives a Cracked Egg; players also earn stars for Star Rewards. Paycheck: Bucks 10 per 10 minutes of play (returned Sep 12, 2025). Money Tree: Bucks 8 every 5 minutes, max Bucks 100 per real day. Lure: a Campfire Cookie in a lure can pay Bucks 10 to 10,000 after four hours. Daily Bucks cap Bucks 300 per in-game day (500 with Pet Handler Pro Certificate); x2 weekends "every few months" double pet aging speed and the caps. Normal aging timers not on the page. — [Adopt Me wiki: Bucks](https://adoptme.fandom.com/wiki/Bucks)
- [official, support page, undated] Login streak "starts once you have logged on for at least 2 days in a row"; Star Rewards depend on the streak day; a missed day breaks the streak and it shows 1 on return; Adopt Me Pets+ subscribers do not lose the streak; for streaks over 30 days, support may restore it once. Per-day rewards and reset time not on the page. — [Adopt Me support](https://support.playadopt.me/support/solutions/articles/153000194015-daily-log-in-streak-faq)
- [fan guide, Sep 10, 2026] Star Reward Coins: one per daily login, not required to be consecutive, missing a day does not reset coins. Milestones: 1 coin Hyper Speed Potion, Small Gift, temporary aura; 2 Small Sip Potion; 3 Big Sip Potion; 5 Build Limit Increase and Streak Saver; 10 Massive Gifts; 14 Royal Fairy Tale Egg; 20 2x Pet Aging and 2x Bucks; 28 Gemstone Egg. No cap mentioned. Conflicts in wording with the support page (which ties Star Rewards to streak day); the two may describe different systems or an update. — [Roonby](https://roonby.com/2026/09/10/star-reward-coins-adopt-me-guide/amp/)
- Social, trading, aging write-ups: not found beyond the above.
