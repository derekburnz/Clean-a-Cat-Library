# Gap fill: first five minutes and core loops of ten Roblox games (as of Nov 22, 2026)

Method note: Web search returned only link lists; only fetched pages are used as evidence. The fetch tool returns a summary of each page, not raw text, so quotes are as summarized. The Roonby Clean The Ballpit page fetch was denied by the permission system, so it was not read. Call budget (about 15) was reached before Adopt Me, My Restaurant and Shoot Arrows For Animals could be covered. No page gave second-by-second timing for any game.

## Clean The Ballpit!

### Takeaway
Only one usable guide (Nov 18, 2026). A tutorial covers picking up balls, filling the bag and selling at a basket; first buy is bag capacity. No prices, bag size or timings are given.

### Cited Findings
- Spawn: you start in the bedroom and corridor of The House (starting map). No level gate, paid entry or code. Starting items or currency not stated. — [Of Zen and Computing, Nov 18, 2026](https://www.ofzenandcomputing.com/clean-the-ballpit-guide-cash-fast-upgrades-companions-lost/)
- First action: finish the tutorial (pick up balls, fill bag, sell at a basket), then collect balls by walking into them or using the scoop. — same
- First reward: cash from selling a full bag at the nearest basket; free cash rewards also appear around the map; amounts not given. — same
- First purchase: bag capacity ("Upgrade bag capacity first, then power, speed and reach, and save the vacuum for later"). No upgrade prices. Big Scoop collects 24 balls at once; vacuum about 280 balls per second. BloxMart (second map) unlocks with cash, cost not given. — same
- Goals shown: fill and sell near a basket, clear sections of The House, unlock BloxMart; newer maps pay more per ball. — same
- Other players: built-in co-op; two players splitting a room fill bags faster; solo works. — same
- Shop/pass prompts: none described; group rewards and boosts mentioned without detail; no codes. — same
- Confusing: bag fills faster than new players expect; sell points are spread out so you must find the nearest; vacuum bought too early means lots of walking to a basket; lost items are separate from balls and easy to miss. — same
- Core loop (one line): collect balls into a bag, sell at a basket, buy bag/power/speed/reach upgrades, unlock better maps.

### Inferences
- The tutorial is the only place controls are taught, so it is likely forced or at least first (inferred; the page does not say forced).

### Gaps
- Not found: seconds to first reward, bag size, prices, how tutorial guides (arrows or text), when shop appears. Roonby, rosenberryrooms and beatcopgame pages not read (the latter two appear to be copies of the same guide in search listings).

## Throw Food For Animals

### Takeaway
Tutorial comes first and unlocks the pull-up bar; food buying is via a shop ring and needs an explicit EQUIP. Starting cash, first animal income and Index unlock condition are stated by no source read.

### Cited Findings
- Eight-step loop: finish tutorial, train Throw Power on pull-up bar, upgrade the bar, throw the free Carrot, aim meter near 20, place animals at base, reinvest, buy base space only when the pen is full. Pull-up bar opens after the tutorial ends. Starting cash, first-animal income: not stated. — [Of Zen and Computing, Nov 12, 2026](https://www.ofzenandcomputing.com/throw-food-for-animals-beginner-guide/)
- Prices: Apple $30, Banana $150, Toast $4K. Guide advises saving for the next tier rather than buying every cheap food, and later recommends Toast as the first real food. Food Shop is the blue-and-white building left of the base entrance; base-size button just right of the entrance. — same
- Animal Index: button on left side of screen; pays a cash bonus for completing a biome's collection; unlock condition and amount not stated. — same
- Buy flow: step into the shop ring at the food stand, buy, then tap EQUIP (buying does not auto-equip). Only animals placed at your base earn money. Pull-ups give XP that builds Throw Power. Carrot free, Apple $30, Banana $150, Toast $4K next; first bar upgrade is the Steel Bar at $50. Placed animals earn while offline. — [Bloxodes, updated Nov 1, 2026](https://bloxodes.com/articles/throw-food-for-animals-beginners-guide)
- Confusing: FAQ calls Throw Power "Strength"; the free Carrot step vs later Toast advice with no switch point; no time or money per step; Index bonus unquantified; players may mix up the power meter (per-throw distance) with Throw Power (grows from bar XP) or expect auto-equip. — Of Zen and Computing and Bloxodes above (the meter-vs-power confusion is the page-summary's inference, not a quoted complaint)
- Other players: not mentioned. Shop/pass prompts: Food stand ring only; no gamepass mentioned in either guide.
- Earlier pass (Roonby, Nov 11, 2026) already has the meter 0-20 and zones; not repeated.

### Inferences
- Likely first three spends in order: Steel Bar $50 (training), Apple $30 / Banana $150 (food), then Toast $4K; this is an inference from listed prices, no source states a first-three order.

### Gaps
- Not found: starting cash, tutorial step-by-step text, income per animal, Index unlock timing, first pass prompt. ProGameGuides, games.gg, theclick pages in search were skipped per instructions. A game.guide "training bars" page appeared in search but was not opened.

## Grow a Garden

### Takeaway
Sources agree on Carrot first and no formal tutorial. Starting cash, growth time and sale amounts are not in these guides. Restock is every five minutes per a May 2025 page; a June 2026 page gives no duration.

### Cited Findings
- First seed Carrot ("you will be able to afford Carrot"); starting cash not stated (cash shown bottom-left); sell by pressing E at the Sell Stall NPC and choosing "Sell your inventory"; seed shop restocks every five minutes; stock is first come first serve; each friend in your garden gives a 10% sales boost; daily quests give a free Seed Pack; no tutorial or pass prompts described. — [Gamertweak, published May 17, 2025, updated May 22, 2025](https://gamertweak.com/how-to-play-grow-a-garden/) (old; may be outdated)
- Updated June 22, 2026 guide: no in-game tutorial described; "first ten minutes" route is claim plot, buy starter seeds, plant everything, harvest and sell, and rebuy higher rarity. Seed Shop and Gear Shop exist. Restock duration not given (links to a live timer). Players can trade and visit gardens. No pass prompts. — [gag.gg, updated Jun 22, 2026](https://gag.gg/guides/beginners-guide)
- Earlier pass (Sportskeeda, undated): 20 Cash start, carrot seed, 3x2 plot. Not repeated.

### Inferences
- The 20 Cash start (earlier pass) and the Carrot-as-affordable statement are consistent (inferred).

### Gaps
- Not found: first plant growth seconds, first sale amount, whether a tutorial exists now. Note a "Grow a Garden 2" exists in search listings (nerdschalk, rblxguide); these were not opened and the sequel's data should not be mixed in.

## Dress To Impress

### Takeaway
New players start in a Casual server with a tutorial (5 minute dressing, 13-item cap); regular rounds are 6 minutes with 1-5 star voting. Day-1 login reward is 15 Cash.

### Cited Findings
- Lobby has winners' podium and leaderboards; next theme revealed near end of the countdown; then moved to Dressing Room; intermission length not given. — [rblxguide, Oct 25, 2026](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/beginners-guide)
- Dressing time: Regular 360 s with an 18-item cap (24 with pass); Casual 5 minutes with a 13-item cap, where new players start; Freeplay no timer. Players walk the runway in join order; others rate 1 to 5 stars; results show stars and Cash earned; top three on podium. — same
- Casual server is default start with a tutorial. Accessory Adjustment unlocks after 30 rounds outside Style Showdown or for 399 Robux. Rank rewards from 1,500 stars. Codes give items, not Cash. — same
- Suggested first minute order: finish Casual tutorial, check Daily Tasks in the Hall of Fame, claim Daily Login (15 Cash day 1, +15 daily), redeem codes, play rounds, spend first Cash on a pose pack not a dress, check Weekly Boutique (four items, Saturdays 11 AM Eastern). Cheapest pose pack 1,800 Cash. Gamepasses: Increased Item Limit 129 Robux, Run Faster 149, Custom Makeup 349, VIP 799. Nothing forced. — same
- Confusing: booth count math, when the game stops sending you to Casual, VIP price listed 299 vs 399; guide is built from wiki data and was not play-tested (the guide's own disclosure). — same
- Earlier pass (LDPlayer, Sep 16, 2026) covers round flow and farming servers.

### Inferences
- At 15 Cash per daily login against an 1,800 Cash pose pack, the first cosmetic purchase takes many days (inferred arithmetic; other Cash earnings per round are not given).

### Gaps
- Not found: theme examples seen in first round, lobby intermission length, voting timer length, Cash per round, what Casual tutorial teaches.

Mother Bear is second priority. — [Earnaldo, last updated May 14, 2026](https://earnaldo.com/blog/bee-swarm-simulator-beginner-guide)
