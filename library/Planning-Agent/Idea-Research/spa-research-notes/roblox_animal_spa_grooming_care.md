# Roblox animal spa, pet grooming, pet care and pet dress-up games (as of Nov 22-23, 2026)

Scope and method note. About 40 tool calls. Labels: [official] = Roblox page text; [tracker] = Rolimons / RoWatcher / Rotrends / Bloxodes; [guide] = third-party guide; [opinion]. Roblox pages (www.roblox.com/games/{id}) are readable for the description, creator and "no running experiences" only; they do NOT show visits, likes or dates in this reader. Rolimons works only for older place ids (client errors on newer ones). I did not find a way to run tracker search boxes, so the inventory comes from web searches plus pages that turned up. It is not a complete census. No game's real "first 5 minutes" was found from a video or hands-on source; the only gameplay detail available is each game's own Roblox description. Read-it-as-is: where a field is not listed below it was "not found". Earlier notes already covered Winter Wonderland (rowatcher), PAWS, Pet Hospital RP, Pet Spa (0 visits), Animal Hospital, Clean the X games; I build on those.

## 3. Grooming and care inside bigger pet games

### Takeaway
In Adopt Me, grooming is a need among about 23: "Dirty" is a 15-Bucks bath task done by placing the pet in a shower or bathtub (placed station, not a minigame), and "Salon" is a 20-Bucks location need where you stay until it completes. It is a minor chore, not the point. No source on Horse Life or Pet Simulator grooming was found.

### Cited Findings
- Adopt Me needs list: Dirty (15 Bucks) = "place them in a shower or bathtub"; Salon (20 Bucks) = "stay at the Salon until it completes"; other everyday needs 10-12 Bucks; location needs (School, Beach Party, Pizza Party, Cat Cafe!, etc.) 16-20 Bucks; Sick (16 Bucks) via Healing Apple, Doctor Heart or Hospital bench; advanced needs unlock after 50 and 100 total needs. Fan wiki, undated. [Bloxodes wiki](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/adopt-me/pet-needs)
- Adopt Me Salon: pink building with oversized scissors on Adoption Island; you can customize pets with spray paint, or work as a stylist or manager to earn Bucks. The guide does not mention pet wear. [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/where-is-the-salon-in-adopt-me-roblox/) (guide; date not shown). Salon update guide at rpgstash could not be loaded.
- Adopt Me scale: 306.8K players now, 45.5B visits, 86.4% liked (Bloxodes, Nov 22-23, 2026). [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/lists/top-trending-roblox-pet-care-games)
- My Cat Box added cat wash in Dec 2020 (earlier note): [Game Developer press release](https://gamedeveloper.com/press-release/popular-my-cat-box-roblox-title-gets-cat-washing-update-improved-clothing-and-more-added-to-their-game). Not re-read; mechanics not found.
- Pet Simulator, per a Sportskeeda list: collect money and boxes, unlock areas, hatch eggs; no grooming. [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/10-best-games-roblox-players-love-pets) (undated guide). The same list contains no grooming or salon games.
- Horse games (Wild Horse Islands 3.1K, Horse Life 2.3K, Horse Valley 476): scale only from Bloxodes. A search for Horse Life grooming returned only real-world horse care, so in-game grooming is not confirmed.

### Inferences
- Adopt Me turns bathing into a daily-chore currency loop; that is the mental model of young players: wash is a routine need with a payout, not a destination.
- Adopt Me's salon and stylist job shows demand for the fantasy inside a pet game, which was not enough for standalone salons to chart.

### Gaps
- Horse Life, Wild Horse Islands, Pet Simulator grooming mechanics not found; My Cat Box wash mechanics not found.
