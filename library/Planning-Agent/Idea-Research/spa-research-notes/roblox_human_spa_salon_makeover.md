# Roblox spa, salon, beauty and makeover games for human or avatar characters (as of Nov 22-23, 2026)

Method and caveats. All stat pages read Nov 22-23, 2026. Source type tags: [OFFICIAL] = Roblox's own search API, [TRACKER] = rowatcher / rotrends / bloxodes / rolearn, [GUIDE] = third-party guide, [PRESS]. Trackers disagree with each other, and rotrends "last update" text is often stale or inconsistent (e.g. "2 months ago" next to an Oct 2026 date), so treat update dates as rough. Not read: roblox.com game pages (they render as "Purchase Completed" stubs with no stats), so the Roblox page numbers for visits, likes and favorites were not obtained. Roblox's omni-search API did work and gave live player counts (Nov 22-23, 2026, official). Research was stopped at about 20 tool rounds; many tiny games have no stats read. Earlier notes on pet/cleaning/job-sim games are in /home/claude/deep-dive/research_notes/Roblox cat game opportunities/roblox_spa_care_cleaning_games.md and are not repeated, except where a number is reused.

## 1. Which games exist, with numbers

### Takeaway
More than 60 experiences with spa, salon, makeup, skincare, hot-spring or makeover themes exist. A few big makeover contests exist (Dress To Impress 54K to 156K now; three makeup-contest launches in Sep-Oct 2026 hit 22K to 65K peaks), but every true spa or salon game (tycoon, job-sim, ASMR, bath house) found has a live count under about 500 and most are under 50. The one spa-named game with real traction was Skincare Spa (Nov 2025), and it has since faded.

## 2. First 5 minutes, loop, upgrades, monetization for the biggest games

### Takeaway
The big makeover games all start the same way: join a round, get a theme, build a look under a timer (about 5 to 6 minutes), walk a runway, get 1-5 star votes. First reward comes at the end of the first round. The sources for the small spa and salon games describe tycoon/job-sim loops (serve customers, buy stations, hire staff) with no comparable hook.

### Cited Findings

- **Dress To Impress** (loop and first minutes). Join a lobby, receive a fashion theme, have six minutes to dress from a large wardrobe, then a runway walk with 1-5 star ratings from others; top three get podium spots and stars feed a 14-tier rank ("New Model" to "Fashion Goddess"). Article does not describe onboarding in detail. Modes: Style Showdown (Mar 2025, 10-player elimination), Duos, Freeplay (no timers or votes). Retention drivers named: frequent collaborations (Charli XCX / Brat Aug 2024, Lady Gaga x Mayhem Aug 2025, Wicked: For Good Nov 2025, Flee the Facility crossover Jan 2026), seasonal updates, story content, item clipping. Peak 1.5M; Sep 2024 average above 250K; 651K in one session during the Brat collab; "current average 37K-54K". Monetization: article names VIP items and a PhatMojo toy line with DLC codes; no pass prices. Source: [Rowatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/dress-to-impress-how-a-teen-developer-s-fashion-game-hit-9-7-billion-visits) [TRACKER/news, publish date metadata inconsistent].
- **Facecard: Makeup Showdown**. Up to 10 players per server; dismiss "What's New" and safety windows, press JOIN, read title card with theme, click through makeup station tutorials (tools, skin tone, mirror, safety), then paint on a blank 3D face (Base, Eyes, Lips, Makeup tabs, layers) and rate each other 1-5. Winning gives stars, cash, ranks; cash and stars buy rotating cosmetics; badge for first win; looks saved to Lookbook. Passes: Increased Layer Limit 129 R$, 2x Facecard Cashback 199 R$, VIP 349 R$. Source: [allthings.how guide](https://allthings.how/facecard-makeup-showdown-how-to-play-on-roblox-full-guide/) [GUIDE]. Update cadence "roughly weekly": Accessory Update (Nov 13-21), Halloween: Nightmare (Oct 24-Nov 7 or Oct 31, dates conflict), Everything Update (Oct 16-23), Meet Aniya & Mina (Oct 10-12). Source: [allthings.how events](https://allthings.how/facecard-makeup-showdown-events-schedule/) [GUIDE, dates internally inconsistent].
- **Makeup Mania** (guide for the BETA / head-based version). Start with blank head, theme banner (Goth, Fairycore, Y2K, Clean Girl) and about 300-second countdown; set Mirror Mode, Undo, zoom; optional untimed Freeplay with no reward; paint in order skin, base, brows, eyeshadow, blush, liner, lips; equip 3D lashes and a hairstyle in Style Studio; timer ends, everyone walks runway, room gives 1-5 stars. Style Studio try-on is free, unowned items show Robux price; guide says most items free and you can finish a round without a pass; game "still in BETA". Source: [allthings.how guide](https://allthings.how/makeup-mania-on-roblox-beginner-guide-to-finishing-a-round/) [GUIDE]. Numbers: see table 1A.
- **Makeup Salon (Fifty Games)**. Roblox description: "Create the perfect makeup look to match the theme" and compare with others. 5 game passes and 5 developer products exist, names and prices not shown. Average session 13.0 minutes (down 8.1% over the period), 7-day average CCU 5,727, rank #292, Trending in Roleplay & Avatar Sim #8; 7-day range 5.0K to 10.5K with 12.7K peak. Source: [Rotrends](https://www.rotrends.com/game/10769514597/Makeup-Salon), [Rolearn](https://rolearn.dev/games/10769514597/) [TRACKER]. First-5-minutes detail: not found.

## 3. Sub-types and which draws the most players

### Takeaway
Round-based makeover/makeup contest with player voting is the dominant sub-type by far: Dress To Impress (49K to 156K now) plus the Sep-Oct 2026 makeup wave (22K to 65K peaks). Tycoons, job sims, ASMR and hot-spring/roleplay games for spa/salon stay at hundreds of players or less.

### Cited Findings
- Contest: see table 1A. Peak CCUs 3K to 65K for 2026 launches, 1.1M to 1.5M for Dress To Impress. [TRACKER]
- Tycoon (place stations, hire staff): Sunsilk peak 1.8K, Beauty Salon Tycoon 809, Nail Salon Tycoon 476 (24h), Salahdin tycoons 156-208. [TRACKER]
- Job-sim with hand minigames (serve customers): My Salon Empire peak 3K; Work at a Hair Salon & Spa and Stylz show no live servers. [TRACKER, ROBLOX PAGE]
- Skincare/ASMR hand minigames: Skincare Spa (peak 1K to 6.5K), [PIMPLES] ASMR Skin Care 141 now, Build Your ASMR Spa 23 now. [TRACKER/OFFICIAL]
- Roleplay hangout / bathhouse / hot spring: NEOHEE HOT SPRING 493-505 now, Capybara Onsen 220, Sura Hot Spring 124, Neighborhood Bath 0 visits. [OFFICIAL, TRACKER]
- Horror salon: Lana's Salon peak 126, 67% liked. [TRACKER]
- Official search for "cat spa" returned no spa game; the closest were Care For Cats (27), My Cat Shop (0), Run a Cat Cafe (6). [OFFICIAL](https://apis.roblox.com/search-api/omni-search?searchQuery=cat%20spa&sessionId=1&pageType=all)

### Inferences
- Beauty as a theme has proven demand in contest form only. Where the player treats a customer (not themselves), nothing has charted.

### Gaps
- No spa-style roleplay hangout of size was found; hot springs are small. Concurrent counts are one-day snapshots.

## 4. Dress To Impress and makeover leaders: why they hold players

### Takeaway
Sources attribute DTI's hold to short rounds with a social vote, many modes, constant collaborations and seasonal updates. The 2026 makeup copies show the format can spike fast but the three fastest fell steeply.

### Cited Findings
- DTI: details in section 2. Reported current average 37K-54K versus peak 1.5M. [Rowatcher news]
- Facecard: peak 56.5K on Oct 17, 2026; last 30-day average 8K down 57.1% from the prior 30 days; now about 7% of peak, tracker verdict "declining" (rowatcher's "dying" threshold is -60%). Like 98.5% from 275K votes. [Rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10769516701/facecard-makeup-showdown/dying)
- Makeup Mania [ORIGINAL]: peak 64.7K (Sep 25 launch month) to 124 now, i.e. about 0.2% of peak. [Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10768023539) (note: the name may be a different universe than "Makeup Mania! [REVAMPED]", which shows 63 live; two Makeup Mania entries exist.)
- Makeup Frenzy: peak 28.6K to 672. MAKEUP ALL STARS: 13.2K to about 2.2K. Makeup Artist: 12K peak (Oct 2026), 1K to 1.4K now.
- Makeup Salon (Fifty Games): 7-day average 5,727, only down 1.1%, so it has held best of the 2026 wave so far; Roblox lists it in Trending #8 of Roleplay & Avatar Sim. [Rotrends]
- Makeover Show: 64.9% liked; Nails Show 49.4% liked; Makeup Artist 68.7%; so low ratings do not stop a spike, but those games are at 30-100 players now.

### Inferences
- Copies of the format in Oct 2026 launched on the same days (Oct 4-5, 2026), which suggests copying of a trend, not independent growth. Hold-up differs: higher like % (Facecard 98.5%, Makeup Salon 96.9%) and weekly updates versus 82-89% and little update history.

### Gaps
- No review or comment text was read. No source explains why Makeup Mania crashed.

## 5. Why small spa and salon games stayed small (evidence found)

### Takeaway
No source states reasons directly. Evidence available is ratings, update dates and descriptions only.

### Cited Findings
- Beauty Salon Tycoon! (Playverse, brand "Play! Beauty"): 74.7% liked, peak 809 in May 2026, 0-1 now. [Rowatcher]
- Makeup Salon Tycoon and Glowing Up Tycoon (same developer): group-join cash boost used as acquisition hook; 1.7M and 4.6M visits; peaks 156 and 208. [Rowatcher]
- Sunsilk Hair Care Lab Tycoon: 109.9M visits over about 3.7 years, but only 1.8K peak and 153 now; rotrends last update Mar 13, 2026 while craftane shows Apr 26, 2023. [Rotrends]
- Nails Show: 49.4% liked, last update May 29, 2023, 113.8M visits, 30 now. [Rotrends]
- Salon Makeover: 140.4M visits, 88.1% liked, 2K peak, 149-252 now; sibling Beauty Salon by same developer has 0 visits. [Rowatcher]
- Skincare Spa: 91.4% liked, 2M favorites, but now single or low double digits. [Rowatcher]
- Many near-empty titles (Spa Day 0, Capybara Spa Tycoon 0, Puppy Spa 5, Run a Puppy Spa 0, Build Your Nail Salon 0, Mega Salon Tycoon 0, My Salon Tycoon 0) show copy-named tycoons with no players. [OFFICIAL omni-search]

### Inferences
- High like % with small peaks suggests players who find these games enjoy them, so the gap seems to be discovery and day-1 hook, not quality. This is an inference; no source says it.
- Dated tycoon format plus long gaps between updates plausibly limits growth, but only Nails Show has a documented stale update (May 2023).

### Gaps
- No direct evidence for pay walls, bugs or slow start for any of these games. Update histories for most not read.

## 6. Spa/salon game that grew fast in 2025-2026

### Takeaway
Skincare Spa (Nov 2025) is the only spa-named case; its peak is disputed (1K vs 6.5K) and it has faded. The fast growers in 2026 are makeup contests, not spas. Of salon-themed names, Makeup Salon (Fifty Games, Oct 2026) is the fastest and most durable.

### Cited Findings
