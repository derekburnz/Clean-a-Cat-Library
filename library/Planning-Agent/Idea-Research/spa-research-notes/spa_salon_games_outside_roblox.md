# Spa, salon, makeover and pet-care games outside Roblox (notes as of 2026-11-22)

Method note: about 20 tool calls. Fetched pages were summarised by a small model, so figures are as reported there. Web search returned link lists only, so no snippets used as evidence. Coverage is PARTIAL: many requested items (first-5-minutes detail, downloads for most titles, treatment-by-treatment satisfaction rankings, Animal Restaurant, Cats & Soup-style peers, Tamagotchi, Toca Pet figures, Crazy Labs/TutoTOONS, ASMR makeover data) were not found. Gaps sections say so. Earlier notes (Cats & Soup, Neko Atsume, PowerWash Simulator, Seeplay's Cat Spa) not repeated.

## 1. Sub-genres and leading games (time management, idle/tycoon, creative toy, makeover puzzle, pet care, cozy management)

### Takeaway
Found data for: time management (Sally's Spa), makeover puzzle (Project Makeover, the revenue leader of its category), children's creative salon toy (Toca Hair Salon 4), virtual pet (My Talking Tom Friends, Nintendogs), cozy bathhouse management (Spirittea), idle animal hotel (Hamster Inn), animal hot spring idle (Capybara Spa). Scale varies hugely: Project Makeover ~$1M/week vs. small indies in the low thousands of ratings.

### Cited Findings
Time management
- Sally's Spa: Beauty Salon Game, developer Games Cafe Inc.; iPhone, free with ads and IAP, 4+ rating; 12K ratings at 4.7 (App Store page read Nov 2026, store data). You tap and drag customers to stations (sauna, massage table, mani-pedi), earn tips, serve tea, sell beauty products, unlock locations (New York, Paris, Rome, Japan, Fiji) by level targets, mini-games and daily rewards. Release date not listed on the page; version history starts 03/08/2022 (store listing, latest 12/24/2025 per page). Original app ID 315733642 suggests an early App Store era title but launch year is not stated on the page. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sallys-spa-beauty-salon-game/id315733642)
- Same page: Page says "no time limit" in the description but recent reviews (summarised by the fetch tool) criticise difficulty spikes, upgrade and assistant costs, and travel timers pushing purchases. Assistants sold at $2.99 to $4.99; coin packs $4.99 and $19.99; 15 Extra Time Boosters $4.99. COPPA and GDPR support added in v5.7 (06/28/2023); coins on failed levels and level replay added Dec 2022 — same App Store page.
- Sally's Salon: Kiss & Make-Up exists, rated 12+ (search listing only; not opened) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1203328439)
Idle / hotel / hot spring
- Hamster Inn (HyperBeard): you check guests in and out, handle room service, collect tips and payments, clean messes, hire staff with special abilities, unlock guests and areas; first session starts with an inn with no rooms, revenue builds quickly. PocketGamer review gives 4.5/5 (reviewer opinion). Rated 4+ (search listing) — [PocketGamer review](https://www.pocketgamer.com/hamster-inn/review/)
- Capybara Spa (developer Shinichiro Ihara, iOS, 4+): feed capybaras food they like, take them to hot springs, daily care, earn coins; 1K ratings at 4.7; has ads; ad removal sold at $3.99 (30 days), $7.99 (90 days), $19.99 (permanent); first version Oct 2021 and still updated Oct 2025 (App Store, store data) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1590870993)
- Other titles surfaced by search but not opened: CapybaraOnsen Idle Game, Kawaii Hot Spring: Idle Tycoon, Animal Hot Springs, Hot Springs Onsen (Steam), Capybara Spa Tycoon (CrazyGames) — listing only.
Hands-on creative salon toy
- Toca Hair Salon 4 (Toca Boca, owned by Spin Master): Common Sense Media rates 5+; kids wash, cut, shave, style hair, add clothing and makeup, a grow tool undoes over-cutting, characters react with faces and sounds, pick backdrop and save snapshot — [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/toca-hair-salon-4)
- Store listing: Hair and Beard Station, Face Station (purchase), Style Station, Photo Booth with photo book, Shampoo Station (wash, towel off, blow dry, removes face paint). IAP: packs $0.99 to $4.99, Toca Hair Salon 4 Plan $5.99, Piknik Unlimited $11.99. Listing seller Sago Sago Toys / Piknik; © 2023 Toca Boca AB — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1485387513)
- Toca Boca overall: "over a billion" downloads, undated in Wikipedia (secondary); Spin Master bought it in 2016 for SEK 263M; standalone apps removed from Apple and Google stores January 2024; Piknik preschool subscription announced October 2023 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toca_Boca)
Makeover puzzle
- Project Makeover (Magic Tavern, Inc.): match-3 plus makeover and room decor; about 800K App Store ratings at 4.5, 13+ listing; IAP $1.99 to $19.99; updates roughly every 2 to 3 weeks adding 60 to 100 levels plus a new makeover, sale, event or obstacle (App Store, Nov 2026) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/1483058899)
- Sensor Tower (market estimate): Project Makeover is #1 in US iOS fashion games Q4 2025, weekly revenue peaking about $1.2M in late September and ending the year about $1.01M; next game (Glow Fashion Idol) peaked about $130K/week — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-ios-top-5-fashion-games-revenue-us-6011f531241bc16eb85bc2b8) (figure from fetch summary; peaks not quarterly totals)
- Common Sense rates Project Makeover 8+ (parents' reviews average 15+); 5 lives, 20-minute refill, gems $1.99 to $99.99 — [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/project-makeover)
Virtual pet
- My Talking Tom Friends (Outfit7): launched June 12, 2020; 22M installs in six days, 60M in two weeks, 100M just over a month (official via PocketGamer.biz, July 20, 2020); 7M+ pre-registrations in six days; franchise 13 billion downloads then — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/my-talking-tom-friends-100-million-downloads)
- Nintendogs (Nintendo DS): Japan April 21 2005, NA August 22 2005; touchscreen pet, walk, brush and wash dogs; microphone for name calling; contests are the main income; real-time clock makes dogs hungrier and dirtier; 23.96M worldwide total (Wikipedia, date inconsistent between 2015 and 2016); Metacritic 83; Game Informer cited lack of an ending; Nintendo said most owners were female (Wikipedia, secondary) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendogs)
Cozy management with a bathhouse
- Spirittea (Cheesemaster Games, publisher No More Robots), released Nov 13 2023; Steam "Very Positive", 84% of 683 user reviews (Steam page as read, Nov 2026). You sweep cobwebs, wash and dry towels, stoke boilers with wood, mix elixirs per spirit taste, seat spirits who get along together, scrub spirits, upgrade to attract more customers — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1931010/Spirittea)
- Windows Central review: quests in any order with no time limits, mini-games (fishing, bug catching, karaoke), befriending townsfolk unlocks a hired helper, over 100 hours content, seasonal collectible variants; complaints: bugs, no accessibility menu, towel shortages — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/xbox/spirittea-review)

### Inferences
- Revenue and download scale sits with puzzle (Project Makeover) and character pet apps (Tom); spa-specific time management and idle titles show only thousands of ratings in what was read.
- The Common Sense and store data suggest Toca-style open play is rated younger (5+) than puzzle monetisation (8+ to 13+).

### Gaps
- Downloads/revenue not found for: Sally's Spa/Salon, Toca Hair Salon 4, Hamster Inn, Capybara Spa, Spirittea sales (only review count), Animal Restaurant, Tamagotchi, Crazy Labs/TutoTOONS, Idle Beauty Salon.
- Launch year of Sally's Spa not found on pages read. Diner Dash not researched.
- Animal Restaurant page only seen as search listing (12+).
