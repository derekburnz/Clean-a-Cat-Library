# Cat-themed and spa/pet-care hits outside Roblox (notes as of 2026-11-22)

Note on method: about 15 tool calls; many fetches were summarised by a small model, so figures are as reported by those pages. Search snippets alone were not used as evidence. Many requested items were NOT found (see Gaps).

## 1. Biggest cat-themed hits on mobile/PC 2024-2026

### Takeaway
Cats & Soup (idle, Neowiz/Hidea) is the largest mobile cat game found (60M downloads, Sept 2024, official). Neko Atsume is the template for low-pressure collecting. Little Kitty, Big City and Stray show cat-as-protagonist works on PC/console. Sensor Tower's Q4 2025 US ranking shows cat games earning via idle, merge and match, not spa.

### Cited Findings
- Cats & Soup: 60 million downloads announced 13 Sept 2024 (official/publisher press via PocketGamer); free with IAP; anniversary content = costumes, limited-edition cat coupons, login event, music player. Article does not name developer (credits Neowiz; "Hidea" appears on Sensor Tower) — [PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/cats-soup/60m-downloads/)
- Cats & Soup ranked #4 US cat game by revenue Q4 2025, about $57K to $61K per week (Sensor Tower market estimate; weekly basis only clearly stated for The Battle Cats) — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-cat-games-revenue-us-608810b928293296047965a0)
- Same ranking, Q4 2025 US: The Battle Cats ~$289K to $310K/week (Ponos); Drop the Cat (ACTIONFIT) $26.5K to ~$183K by mid-Dec; Simon's Cat Match! (Tactile Games) ~$65K; My Cat – Virtual Pet Games (APPSYOULOVE) ~$53K — same Sensor Tower page (estimates)
- Cats & Soup loop per store/press search results: idle cat-run soup shop; the search results also show PocketGamer headlines of 5M, 20M, 40M, 50M downloads and "12M daily users" press release (headlines only; not opened) — [PocketGamer search listing](https://www.pocketgamer.com/cats-soup/)
- Cat Snack Bar (treeplla Inc.): free idle restaurant tycoon where cats cook and serve automatically, even offline; tips, expansion, limited-time events; coins plus gems; IAP from $0.99 to $19.99 incl. $19.99 NoAds pack (App Store listing, ©2023; no launch date or download count stated) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6443895159)
- Neko Atsume (Hit-Point): launched 20 Oct 2014 Japan, English 30 Oct 2015; 10M downloads as of 4 Dec 2015; you buy food/toys/furniture, cats visit, you photograph them for an album and receive fish currency; daily passwords; yard remodels; 86 cats (26 rare) by v1.15.0; "no end game"; won CEDEC best game design Aug 2015; sequel Neko Atsume 2 Oct 2024 (Wikipedia, secondary) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Atsume)
- Little Kitty, Big City (Double Dagger Studio; Steam/Switch/Game Pass): launched 11 May 2024; 464k Steam wishlists pre-launch; 100k copies in 48 hours; 132k Steam units by end of May; GameDiscoverCo estimates ~500k life-to-date across PC and Switch; Switch selling ~30% better than Steam per creator; Game Pass >1M players end of May, >2M by July 2024 — [WN Hub summary of GameDiscoverCo and developer data](https://wnhub.io/news/investment/item-44203); 100k in 2 days also at [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/little-kitty-big-city-tops-100-000-sales-in-two-days)
- Stray (BlueTwelve Studio, Annapurna Interactive): launched July 2022; ~63,000 concurrent Steam players on day one (another cited figure 50,555), Annapurna's biggest launch; also on PS4/PS5 and PS Plus — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/stray-is-already-annapurnas-biggest-launch-ever). Note: 2022, outside the 2024-26 window; no later sales figure verified.

### Inferences
- Revenue leaders among cat mobile games are idle/merge/match, so cat theme is a wrapper; the loop must stand on its own.
- Cat-as-hero cozy PC games sold in the hundreds of thousands, far below mobile idle download counts.

### Gaps
- Cats & Soup lifetime revenue, current downloads (post Sept 2024), and the 12M DAU claim not verified (headline only).
- Cat Snack Bar downloads/revenue not found.
- No 2026 data found for any title; Sensor Tower Q4 2025 is the newest.
- Cat merge games beyond Drop the Cat, and cat cafe games, not investigated.
- What reviewers say keeps players playing: only the Neko Atsume mechanics above; no review corpora read.

## 2. Spa, salon, grooming, pet wash, vet, pet care hits

### Takeaway
PowerWash Simulator is the clear proof that cleaning with instant visual progress sells at scale; hyper-casual "ASMR pet salon" apps exist in large numbers but I found no reliable download or revenue data for them.

### Cited Findings
- PowerWash Simulator (FuturLab): over 12 million players announced 22 April 2024 (official); prior 7M milestone May 2023; DLC tie-ins and a VR version — [FuturLab](https://www.futurlab.co.uk/news/thank-you-for-12-million-powerwash-simulator-players)
- Developers: nozzle sizes, detergents, varied grime; no enemies or timers in core game; "Satisfying" is the word players use most; Challenge Mode adds clocks/limited supplies for those wanting pressure; inspired partly by de Blob's immediate visual progress; later added crouch to spot dirt (developer interview) — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/powerwash-sim-devs-on-making-cleaning-fun-advanced-cro-1847319502)
- ASMR Pet Salon (iOS, seller Xiang Li, © Neko Games): 4+ rating, cleaning a cat and a pig, few levels (App Store; no rating count or downloads shown) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/asmr-pet-salon/id1623187135)
- The App Store search returned many near-identical ASMR clean/makeover/PawSpa titles (listing evidence of saturation, no metrics) — [search listing example](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6756268774)

### Inferences
- The hyper-casual ASMR niche is crowded with small apps, so a differentiator beyond washing is needed.

### Gaps
- No download or revenue data for pet grooming, vet, or ASMR spa apps; no data.ai/AppMagic write-up found.
- PowerWash Simulator sales beyond 12M (April 2024), and PowerWash Simulator 2 results, not verified.
- Vet/pet care hits (e.g. other pet-care sims) not researched.

## 3. Existing "Cat Spa" or similar (name clash)

### Takeaway
A mobile game titled "Cat Spa" already exists, so the exact title is a clash risk on iOS/Android.

### Cited Findings
- "Cat Spa" on the App Store: developer Seeplay Inc. (seeplay.co.kr); free with IAP; idle management sim where cat fairies run a massage parlor/spa, hire cat staff, serve animal customers, unlock and decorate rooms (massage, lounge, hair salon), collect customer snapshots and stories; 419 ratings, 4.6 average (PH storefront); oldest listed version 3.0.4 dated 02/09/2022 (launch date not stated; ©2018 line) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ph/app/cat-spa/id1543729168)
- Android listing for "cat spa" appears in search results (trashbox.ru) — not opened.

### Inferences
- It is small by rating count (419 in one storefront; other storefronts not checked), but the same concept (cat staff, spa, decorate, customer stories) is already used; a trademark search was not done.
- Roblox title search and USPTO/EUIPO not checked; recommend a distinctive name.

### Gaps
- Download count, revenue and Google Play numbers for Cat Spa not found.
- Other "cat spa" titles (Steam, Switch) not searched.

## 4. Moment-to-moment mechanics that feel satisfying

### Takeaway
Only PowerWash Simulator has a developer-sourced account: immediate visible progress, tool choice, no fail state. Details on wipe-to-reveal, brushing, purring reactions and before/after were not found in sourced form.

### Cited Findings
- Instant visual feedback and no timers/enemies drive the appeal; players call it "Satisfying" — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/powerwash-sim-devs-on-making-cleaning-fun-advanced-cro-1847319502)
- Layering complexity (tool/detergent/grime types) keeps cleaning interesting; optional challenge mode for pressure — same source.
- Article says little about when cleaning gets boring.

### Gaps
- No designer or reviewer source found for foam/rinse stages, knot brushing, purring reactions, before/after reveals, or boredom points. Not filled from memory.

## 5. Long-term hooks without pressure or gambling

### Takeaway
Sourced hooks: collection albums, decorating, visiting/rare characters, daily login rewards, limited-time events, offline idle earnings, customer stories.

### Cited Findings
- Neko Atsume: photo album collection, rare cats, daily passwords, remodel, no end game — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Atsume)
- Cats & Soup: costumes, limited-edition cats, login events — [PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/cats-soup/60m-downloads/)
- Cat Spa (Seeplay): unlockable decorated rooms, customer snapshots and stories, reward for checking in often — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ph/app/cat-spa/id1543729168)
- Cat Snack Bar: offline earning, tips, events; monetisation includes $19.99 NoAds and piggy bank (opinion-free listing data) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6443895159)

### Inferences
- Limited-edition cats and login events are time-pressure mechanics; a no-pressure design would use permanent collections instead.

### Gaps
- No review-based evidence on which hook retains players best.

## 6. Proven with children and touch controls

### Takeaway
Little direct evidence found. Listings: ASMR Pet Salon is rated 4+ and Cat Spa/Cat Snack Bar listings show touch-friendly idle/tap play. No child-specific studies found.

### Cited Findings
- ASMR Pet Salon age rating 4+ — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/asmr-pet-salon/id1623187135)
- Cats & Soup App Store age rating shown as 12+ in the search listing title (IAP/content descriptors) — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/cats-soup/id1581431235?uo=4)

### Gaps
- No COPPA/child-audience performance data, no touch-control design study. Note that IAP-heavy models (gems, NoAds packs) are a regulatory consideration for children, not researched.

## 7. Cats as a theme in ads/UA

### Takeaway
No published data found showing cat creatives out-perform others.

### Cited Findings
- AppsFlyer 2024 creative report: animation has 26% higher IPM than ads without it; gameplay plus animated characters gives highest IPM on ad networks. Does not mention cats or animals (AppsFlyer, industry report) — [AppsFlyer](https://www.appsflyer.com/resources/reports/creative-optimization-2024-report/)
- Cat game category is large enough that Sensor Tower publishes recurring "top 5 cat games" charts per country (listing) — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-cat-games-revenue-us-608810b928293296047965a0)

### Inferences
- Animated characters plus gameplay is the supported creative pattern; "cat" as a specific CTR booster is unproven.

### Gaps
- No Liftoff, SocialPeta, or Reforged Labs figure on animal/cat creatives (found report titles only, not opened).

## Naming/trademark risks
- "Cat Spa" (Seeplay) exists; "Cats & Soup", "Cat Snack Bar", "Neko Atsume", "Little Kitty, Big City", "Stray", "Simon's Cat", "The Battle Cats", "My Cat" are existing titles/marks to avoid. "PowerWash Simulator" is a mark; do not use "simulator" phrasing mimicking it. Mechanics may be borrowed; names, characters and art not.
