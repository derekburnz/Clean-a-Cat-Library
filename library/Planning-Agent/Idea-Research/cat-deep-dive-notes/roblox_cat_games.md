## Which cat-themed Roblox games have meaningful players today?

### Takeaway
Only a handful of cat games have real audiences today: Cat Battles (launched Oct 5, 2026; about 130M visits, 4-5K live now after a 100K peak), 7 Days Cat-Sitting (launched Sep 15, 2026; about 137M visits, about 5K live), and two older roleplay games, Warrior Cats: Ultimate Edition (about 4.8-6.6K live) and Kitten Game (about 4.5-4.7K live). Everything else cat-named is tiny (under 100 live). No cat cafe or cat tycoon game with meaningful players was found.

## Which cat games launched in 2025/2026 and grew fast, and which faded?

### Takeaway
The fast risers were Cat Battles (peak 100K, launched Oct 5, 2026) and 7 Days Cat-Sitting (peak 41.4K, launched Sep 15, 2026). Both already dropped to about 5K live, a roughly 90% fall from peak within 6-9 weeks. Other 2025-26 cat launches peaked in the low thousands to 13.6K and are near zero.

### Cited Findings
- 7 Days Cat-Sitting gained 825.3% to 34,005 players in the week Sep 28-Oct 4, 2026, the biggest weekly gain on that tracker. — [RoWatcher week in numbers](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-week-in-numbers-2026-w40) [tracker]
- Same page lists "Jump for Animals!" down 61.8% (2,887 players) and "Grow a Pet Fighter!" down 70.8% (2,331) as that week's losers. [tracker]
- Cat Battles: 15,025 playing on Nov 2, 2026 per GGRecon; 4.0-4.8K now. Peak 100K in October. [tracker]
- Cat Invasion (Dec 25, 2025): peak 6.2K, now about 50-60 live, 98% liked. Save Your Cat (Jun 8, 2026): peak 13.6K, now about 52-109. Steal From Kittys (Feb 6, 2026): peak 2.8K, now about 22. Find and Pet Cats [270] (May 29, 2026): peak 1.3K, now 35, last updated Sep 19, 2026. [tracker, links above]
- Writer's view on Cat Battles: the only game in GGRecon's top five with a like ratio under 80%, "not everyone is charmed", but growth "speaks for itself." [opinion] — [GGRecon, Nov 2, 2026](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/fastest-growing-new-roblox-games-november-2026/)

### Inferences
- Reasons for fading are not stated by any source read. Observed pattern only: small solo-style games with high likes (Cat Invasion 98%) still faded, so quality alone did not sustain them; frequent updates (Cat Battles weekly, last update Nov 14) are the main difference in the two bigger games.
- The "cat steal" and "cat incremental" clones peaked low, which fits the earlier snapshot's view that copies of a trend are weak.

### Gaps
- No source explains why any specific cat game faded. No retention data (D1/D7) found.
- Trackers' peak figures are not dated beyond "Oct 2026" for Cat Battles.

## Which sub-types are crowded, and which are empty?

### Takeaway
In what was found: cat roleplay (as a cat) and cat arena/fighter/survival are occupied; "cat steal/collect" clones are many but weak. Cat cafe/tycoon, cat care or spa, and cozy cat decorating had no strong game in anything found. Pet care in general is dominated by Adopt Me and horse games.

### Cited Findings
- Bloxodes' "pet care" subgenre page (page 1 of 50 games, no explicit cat game in the list): Adopt Me 317.9K live, 45.5B visits; Wild Horse Islands 3.4K; Horse Life 2.6K; Strideway 1.7K; PAWS Pet Hospital 159 live, 74M visits, 85%; Rescue Pets 85; Raise a Yippee 5; Raise Puppies 3; Littlest Pet Shop 3. Most pet-care entries are under 100 live. [tracker] — [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/lists/top-trending-roblox-pet-care-games)
- Cat cafe tycoon: the only hit by search was Cat Cafe Tycoon by a small first-time group with no running servers when read. [Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/100285104400469)
- "Cat" in the name is common among tiny games (Cat Simulator 1 live, Save Your Cat, Steal From Kittys, Find and Pet Cats, Cat Invasion).

### Inferences
- Crowded: cat roleplay as the cat; cat steal/collect clones; cat chaser/obby small games.
- Open (no strong game seen): cat cafe/hospitality tycoon (this studio's own lane), cat grooming/spa/care for kids, cozy collect-and-decorate cat games. Open could also mean low demand; no source shows demand either way. Cat Battles shows cats attract clicks in action; the spa idea was not tested by any game found.

### Gaps
- Could not run tracker tag searches for "cat", so crowding rests on search hits. No official or tracker count of how many cat-named games exist.
- No cat spa or grooming game was found; unclear whether none exist.

## How do the top cat games make money?

### Takeaway
Cat Battles: two gamepasses plus coin-bought random crates. Kitten Game: gamepasses. No other cat game's monetization was found.

### Cited Findings
- Cat Battles passes: VIP 199 Robux (25% more coins and daily win bonus), Double Coins 349 Robux (stacks with VIP). Crates give furs, sumo gear, emotes; drop rates not listed. Abilities bought with coins (Pounce and Hiss 750, Hairball 12,000). [fan wiki] — [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/cat-battles)
- Player.One says the article does not mention real-money purchases; the crate system uses earned coins. — [Player.One](https://www.player.one/robloxs-cat-battles-was-built-three-days-roblox-assistant-players-are-still-shaping-its-164531)
- Kitten Game: free, some items and gamepasses cost money, passes give boosts. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/how-to-play-roblox-kitten-game)
- Cat Battles codes (RELEASE, NEWGAME, CATS) give free coins. — [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/fastest-growing-new-roblox-games-november-2026/)

### Inferences
- Randomness here is coin-earned crates, not paid ones, per what was found.

### Gaps
- No Robux prices or revenue for Warrior Cats UE, 7 Days Cat-Sitting or others. Roblox does not publish per-game revenue.
