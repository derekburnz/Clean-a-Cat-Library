# Roblox spa, salon, pet care, cleaning and job-sim games (as of Nov 22-23, 2026)

Scope note: Roblox's own pages were not read. All numbers are tracker readings (rowatcher, rotrends, bloxodes), taken Nov 22-23, 2026 unless stated. "Peak" is the tracker's all-time peak concurrent players. Many tracker pages do not give exact dates. Research stopped at about 27 calls; the tracker search boxes were not usable, so searches were done through web search plus tracker pages. Coverage of small games is therefore partial.

## 1. Do cat spa / pet spa / pet salon / groomer / vet games already exist on Roblox?

### Takeaway
No cat spa game with real traction was found. The only two pet spa/salon games found are tiny (a brand-new Pet Spa with 0 visits, and a 2022 Pet Salon Tycoon with 29.3M lifetime visits and a peak of 54 players). The vet/animal hospital area is split: a small old vet sim (PAWS Pet Hospital) and a horror hit (Animal Hospital) that is not comparable. Washing/grooming as the main verb exists only in tiny games. Searched: "Roblox cat spa game", "pet grooming salon", "pet care games", "pet hospital/groomer/vet clinic". I could not run the trackers' own search boxes, so very small games may be missed.

### Cited Findings
- Pet Spa, by "Animals favourite Helpers!": released Oct 2026, 0 visits, 0 players, 0 likes. Tracker. Effectively empty. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10769134433/pet-spa)
- Winter Wonderland Pet Salon Tycoon, by Simple Games Incorporated: created Feb 2022, 29.3M visits, peak 54 players (Feb 2026), 0 now, 86.8% liked (30K likes, 121K favorites). Loop per tracker text: wash and style pets, hire staff, buy furniture, raise salon rating, expand. Washing is the main verb. Tracker. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/3362869464/winter-wonderland-pet-salon-tycoon)
- PAWS Pet Hospital, by THEAM STUDIOS: released Sep 2023, 74.0M visits, 118 players now (159 on bloxodes the same day), peak about 1K (Aug 2026), 85.1% liked. Diagnose and treat animals. Tracker. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/5131771588/paws-pet-hospital), [Bloxodes pet care list](https://bloxodes.com/lists/top-trending-roblox-pet-care-games)
- Pet Hospital RP, by Animal Hospitality: created Aug 6, 2026, 290K visits, 10 players, peak 87, 80.1% liked from 965 votes; Pet Care subgenre, all ages. Tracker. — [Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/10638570149)
- Bloxodes "Top Trending Roblox Pet Care Games" list (page 1 of 2, 50 games, undated, read Nov 22-23, 2026) is dominated by Adopt Me (45.5B visits, 317.9K players, 86.4%) and horse games. Wild Horse Islands 695.5M visits / 3.4K players; Horse Life 375.6M / 2.6K; Strideway 43.5M / 1.7K; Horse Valley 464.4M / 605; PAWS Pet Hospital 74M / 159; Rescue Pets 14.2M / 85 / 93.2%; My Pet City RP 4.2M / 17; Raise Puppies 9M / 3. Tracker. Every non-Adopt-Me pet care game on page 1 is under 4K players. No spa, salon or grooming title appears in page 1. — [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/lists/top-trending-roblox-pet-care-games)
- Rotrends lists "Pet Care" as a genre; its homepage showed no spa/salon/groom/wash/vet titles in its top list (Nov 22-23, 2026). Tracker. — [Rotrends](https://rotrends.com)
- Adopt Me has a salon feature inside it (guides explain where it is), so grooming exists there as a side feature. Opinion/guide. — [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/where-is-the-salon-in-adopt-me-roblox/)
- My Cat Box added a cat wash feature in Dec 2020 (side feature, from the earlier snapshot). — [Game Developer press release](https://gamedeveloper.com/press-release/popular-my-cat-box-roblox-title-gets-cat-washing-update-improved-clothing-and-more-added-to-their-game)
- A mobile-app "Cat Spa" (Apple App Store, rated 4+) and "Pet Salon DIY" exist outside Roblox, which shows the idea sells elsewhere. Not Roblox data. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/cat-spa/id1543729168)
- Animal Hospital (developer Animal Anomaly): released May 10, 2026, peak 736,948 players on Jul 5, 2026 per Rolimons, 463M+ visits, 95% rating. It is survival horror (treat animal patients, avoid anomalies, Sanity mechanic). Not a care sim. Reported by GosuGamers; tracker numbers. — [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78741-animal-hospital-becomes-roblox-s-latest-horror-sensation-here-s-everything-you-need-to-know)
- Rowatcher also lists Sage Hollow Animal Hospital, Animal Hospital Outfits and similar copies. Titles only, numbers not read. Tracker. — [Rotrends](https://www.rotrends.com/game/10770447464/Sage-Hollow-Animal-Hospital)

### Inferences
- Open on direct competition: no cat/pet spa game was found with more than a peak of about 54 players. But "open" also means no proven demand on Roblox; the old tycoon in this niche (29.3M visits over 4 years) never charted.
- Animal Hospital shows an "animal care" setting can chart, but through horror and co-op, not pampering.
- Not found: any dedicated Roblox cat spa. Absence on trackers is not proof; small games can be missed.

### Gaps
- Roblox search was not read, so total count of small pet spa/wash games is unknown.
- Launch day and exact CCU history of Pet Spa unknown (0 visits).
- Bloxodes page 2 of the list not read.

## 2. Human spa, salon, nail, hair, makeover games

### Takeaway
Dress To Impress style makeover is a top-40 game (earlier snapshot), but spa/salon tycoons found in this pass are small. Newer salon games are job-sims with minigames.

### Cited Findings
- My Salon Empire, by The Doux (textured hair care brand): announced around Aug 30, 2026. Players run a salon in Atlanta, consult clients, shampoo, braid, cut, style; add chairs and hire staff. No player numbers in the article; no Robux purchase required per the brand. Reported by TheGrio. — [TheGrio](https://thegrio.com/2026/08/30/the-doux-roblox-my-salon-empire/)
- My Salon Empire loop (guide, updated Sep 9, 2026): order stock, shelve, price products, open shop, seat customers in Standard/Washing/Dryer chairs, play cutting/braiding/brushing minigame, sweep hair, take payment, hire stylist ($1,000 in-game). Free income from daily rewards, playtime gifts, mystery boxes. Premium Active gives +10% coins and XP; Friend Boost scales with friends in server. Guide, not official. — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/how-to-run-your-salon-in-my-salon-empire-roblox/)
- Beauty Salon Tycoon! [UPD], by Play! Beauty (Playverse): released Jan 2026, 1.8M visits, peak 809 (May 2026), 0 now, 74.7% liked. Serve customers, hire staff, unlock floors. Tracker. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/9531004126/beauty-salon-tycoon-upd)
- Dress To Impress (makeover/fashion contest) is in the top 40 (earlier snapshot, Nov 22, 2026). Numbers not re-read here.

### Inferences
- Salon tycoons are small and low-retention (peaks under 1K for the one measured). The big beauty-adjacent game is a fashion contest, a different loop.
- Brand-built salon (My Salon Empire) shows companies use Roblox as marketing; no traction numbers.

### Gaps
- No numbers found for My Salon Empire; no tracker data on nail, hair, makeup games beyond the above. Dress To Impress numbers not re-checked.

## 3. Cleaning and "satisfying" games charting in late 2026

### Takeaway
Cleaning is a clearly live trend in 2026 with several 60K-87K peaks, but each shows a fast spike and a steep fade. Two of three measured are at about 1% of peak within 4 to 5 months.

### Cited Findings
- Clean All the Leaves, by Muffin Interactive: created Jul 21, 2026; 67M+ visits about a month after launch; about 86,800 players at the time of the article (Aug 28, 2026). Loop: rake leaves, bag, dump at vent for cash, buy faster tools and bigger bags, unlock areas, secret ending at 100%. Reported by Player.One; CCU from Creator Exchange. Current numbers not read. — [Player.One](https://www.player.one/clean-all-leaves-suddenly-one-robloxs-biggest-games-why-are-players-raking-leaves-163958)
- Clean the Supermarket, by Tidyverse: released Jun 2026, 61.4M visits, peak 63K (Jul 5, 2026), 384 to 493 players now, 92.9% liked (about 82K likes). Restock a supermarket by sorting 1,000+ items, buy upgrades. 30-day average 432, down 17.1%; now about 1% of peak. Tracker. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10334731049/clean-the-supermarket), [RoWatcher trend](https://rowatcher.com/games/10334731049/clean-the-supermarket/dying)
- Clean The Library, by Retro Library: released May 2026, 99.8M visits, peak 84K (Jun 2026), 2K now, 92.7% liked (147K likes). Sort thousands of books, upgrades and abilities, leaderboard time trials; inspired by a Steam game, Librarian: Tidy Up the Arcane Library. Tracker. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10226701629/clean-the-library)
- Wash The House, by Crystal Clean: released Jun 2026, 94.2M visits, peak 69K (Aug 2026), 362 now, 93.0% liked. Tracker. Loop not read. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10405010493/wash-the-house)
- Clean The Ballpit! (earlier snapshot): rank 41, +22% daily on Nov 22, 2026. The only late-2026 riser found here. Copies of the "Clean the X" template include Clean The Superstore and others in tracker search results. — [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/stats/games/clean-the-superstore-10470464510)
- Pressure Wash Simulator, by Small World Games - Tycoons!: June 2021, 141.1M visits, peak 115 (Mar 2026), 0 now, 85.4% liked. A long-lived older wash game that never charted high. Tracker. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/2694265705/pressure-wash-simulator)
- Car wash games (Washiez Car Wash, Car Wash Tycoon, Wash the Car) exist; numbers not read. — [RoWatcher search result](https://rowatcher.com/games/2563713593/washiez-car-wash)
- Earnaldo has guide posts comparing "Clean the Supermarket vs Clean the Library" (undated 2026 guides; page would not load). — [Earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/clean-the-supermarket-vs-clean-the-library)

### Inferences
- Peak figures of 63K-87K, then collapse to under 2K, mean "clean the X" is a template that gets copied in weeks and fades in about 2 to 5 months. Likes of 92-93% show players enjoy it while it lasts.
- Visits (60-100M) are big relative to peak, which means mostly short-session visits.
- Plain old wash sims (Pressure Wash Simulator) never took off, so the 2026 hits depend on the new short-loop, bag-and-sell format, not on washing itself.

### Gaps
- Current numbers for Clean All the Leaves not read; ASMR/"satisfying" labeled games not found by name; Clean The Ballpit exact visits not re-read.

## 4. Service and job sims

### Takeaway
Sell Fries! (Sep 2026) is the clearest recent job-sim hit; Animal Hospital is the large job-sim-in-horror hit. Both mix the service verb with night-time threat.

### Cited Findings
- Sell Fries!, launched Sep 21, 2026: 127.3M visits, 13.3K playing now, 270.9K favorites (Bloxodes, Nov 2026); earlier GGRecon count 96M visits, 85% liked (Nov 2). Peel and fry potatoes, serve window, buy automation, survive nights 8pm to morning, pick a perk. Passes: Golden Vacuum 170 Robux, 2x Gems 180 Robux; Gems also bought with Robux. Tracker/wiki. — [Bloxodes wiki](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/sell-fries)
- Animal Hospital (see section 1): job sim plus horror, 736,948 peak.
- Older job tycoons (Work at a Pizza Place, SizzleBurger Restaurant) still appear in guides. Numbers not read. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/roblox-games-based-on-real-life-jobs)

### Inferences
- What is new compared with older tycoons: automation purchases, a timed shift, perks, and a threat layer (horror). Cozy-only job sims were not found charting.

### Gaps
- Cook Pizzas not found or read. Restaurant and cafe tycoon numbers not gathered.

## 5. Crowded, dominated or open?

### Takeaway
- Pet spa / cat spa: open (0 serious competitors found), unproven demand.
- Pet care generally: dominated by Adopt Me (317.9K players vs next non-horror under 4K, Bloxodes).
- Cleaning: crowded with near-identical "Clean the X" copies, high spikes, fast fade.
- Salon tycoons: small, scattered. Salon/makeover contest: led by Dress To Impress.
- Job sims: hot but led by horror-flavoured hits.

### Cited Findings
- Evidence above from Bloxodes list and RoWatcher pages.

### Inferences
- Open niche plus a proven loop template ("verb in name, bag and sell, upgrade") is the opening; the risk is no evidence players search for it.

### Gaps
- No search-volume data.
