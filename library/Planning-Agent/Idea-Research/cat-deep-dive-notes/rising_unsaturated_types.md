## 3. How long trend waves lasted

### Takeaway
Reliable measured timelines were scarce; only Steal a Brainrot has a dated rise and fall in what I read. Waves are short for meme/steal games.

### Cited Findings
- Steal a Brainrot: launched May 2025, peak 25.8M CCU Oct 2025 (about 22 weeks to peak per RoWatcher; PocketGamer.biz reports "first game above 25M"); Nov 2026 weekly avg 46,904, -33.2% wk/wk. Roughly 99% below peak. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals) (page's date metadata looks wrong; opinion/tracker); [RoStats w47](https://rostats.io/charts/2026-w47); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players)
- Escape Tsunami For Brainrots: created Dec 15, 2025, peak ~5M, later ~164K (-97%). Swing Obby for Brainrots: launched Feb 18, 2026, peak ~87K, ~57K (-34%). — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)
- Garden Horizons (a Grow a Garden-style PC-launched game): launched Feb 22, 2026; peaked above 1M concurrent during the first update, about 23K by early March 2026 (a ~98% fall in about 2 weeks) after dev exits and a duplication exploit. — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/garden-horizons-player-count-drop-what-happened-and-is-the-game-dead/)
- Grow a Garden: PC Gamer reported 8.9M concurrent from a game "made by a teenager in about three days" and GamesRadar 21M concurrent; no decay curve found. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/a-roblox-farming-game-made-by-a-teenager-in-like-three-days-had-8-9-million-players-online-at-the-same-time-steam-peaked-at-11-5-million-across-all-games-on-the-same-day); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/mmo/with-21-million-concurrent-players-roblox-hit-grow-a-garden-is-bigger-than-half-of-steam-combined-but-players-are-already-flooding-another-far-weirder-competitor)
- 99 Nights in the Forest peaked at 14.2M concurrent per PC Gamer headline; still 119K-260K in Nov 2026 (earlier-snapshot) and 145K wk avg Nov 16 (RoStats). — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/with-a-peak-player-count-of-14-2-million-99-nights-in-the-forest-has-an-audience-other-multiplayer-games-would-kill-for-to-find-these-behemoth-playerbases-you-need-to-be-on-a-platform-like-roblox/); [RoStats](https://rostats.io/charts)
- DONT SPLASH (created Oct 16-17): 162M visits in 17 days, 30.5K avg players by w47 at -43.4% wk/wk, about 4.5 weeks after launch. — [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/fastest-growing-new-roblox-games-november-2026/); [RoStats](https://rostats.io/charts/2026-w47)

### Inferences
- Rough pattern from the data above: viral games reach a quarter of peak within weeks to months (Garden Horizons ~2 weeks, Tsunami months), while a survival/co-op game (99 Nights) is still charting ~a year on. Not a measured weeks table.

### Gaps
- No weekly time series for Grow a Garden, Steal An Egg, Fisch, Fish It; I could not compute weeks to peak / to 25% for them. Peak numbers conflict (Grow a Garden 8.9M vs 21M; Steal a Brainrot 25.4M in earlier snapshot vs 25.8M RoWatcher).

## 5. Roblox official statements and discovery changes in 2026

### Takeaway
Roblox names no genre it wants more of. It does describe "novel games" as new mechanics, distinctive visuals or underrepresented genres, and moved Home toward 28-day retention.

### Cited Findings
- Mar 9, 2026: Today's Picks became Standout Games (curated for novel games); "Deep play through rate" signal added; ads blended into RFY. Mar 26: 7-day qualified play sessions signal. Apr 2: Standout Games mainly video previews. May 14: video tests in RFY. Jul 29: gameplay video autoplays on RFY; up to 39% playtime increase for games with video (May-Jul tests). [official] — [DevForum, How we are improving Home](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571)
- Same post: novel games bring "new gameplay mechanics, distinctive visual styles, or underrepresented genres"; Roblox wants deeper-engagement games, not only short repeat-play titles; genre sorts and "because you played X" explored, not confirmed. [official] — same link
- Jun 15, 2026: Recommended For You moved from 7-day to 28-day retention signals (day 1, 2-7, 8-28); play-through, session quality and spend now separate signals; creators can see them in Creator Analytics. [official] — [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)
- Aug 6, 2026 (live for all Aug 20): Home weighs retention and monetization value; no genres named. [official] — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042)
- Q3 2026 context and 28% of top-100 hours from games under 90 days old: see earlier-snapshot.md.

### Gaps
- RDC 2026 talks on under-served genres not found; one DevForum post on retention (4684575) redirected to maintenance and was unread. No official genre priorities found.
