# Makeover contests boom while spas stall

On Roblox, beauty games draw crowds only when players make a look of their own in a short timed round and other players vote on it. Games where the player treats a customer, human or animal, have all stayed small. On November 22 to 23, 2026, Dress To Impress held about 50K to 156K live players and four makeup contests launched in September and October 2026 had peaked at 22K to 65K, while every true spa, salon or grooming sim found sat under about 500 live players and no cat spa game showed up at all (official Roblox search and trackers). The contests fade fast too, and the one pet version of the contest peaked at 2,409 players in 2022 (tracker), so the format is no sure thing for cats. Today's popular all-ages games open alike: one verb and one free item within a step of spawn, an arrow or short tutorial, a base of your own and a cheap first upgrade. Roblox's docs name two bounce windows, under 60 seconds and 61 to 180 seconds, and count day 1 as its own Home signal, but give no target number. The day 2 hooks seen most in popular games are things that finish while the player is away, daily gifts and near goals in a collection. "Cat Spa" did not show as a Roblox game in search, but the name is taken on the App Store. Two limits shape every answer here: written guides gave almost no timings for first minutes and no day 1 retention number exists for any spa game, so playtests and the studio's own funnel data must fill both gaps.

## Top contests peaked above 20K while every spa and salon sim stayed under 7K

More than 60 human spa, salon, makeup and makeover games and more than 20 animal grooming, vet and pet care games were found. This is not a full census. Roblox's search returns 40 results per query and trackers could not be searched by keyword. Unless a line says otherwise, every Roblox figure in this section is a tracker reading or an official Roblox search count taken on Nov 22 to 23, 2026. Trackers often disagree with each other, and where they do both figures are shown.

### The contest format is a theme, a timer and a vote

The big games all run the same round. In Dress To Impress a player joins a lobby, gets a theme, has six minutes to dress from a large wardrobe, walks a runway and is rated 1 to 5 stars by the room. The top three take a podium and stars feed a 14-step rank (tracker news article, date unclear, [Rowatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/dress-to-impress-how-a-teen-developer-s-fashion-game-hit-9-7-billion-visits)). The 2026 makeup games swap the wardrobe for a blank 3D face. In Facecard: Makeup Showdown up to 10 players press JOIN, read a theme card, click through short station tips, paint with layers and rate each other 1 to 5. Wins pay stars, cash and ranks and each look is saved to a Lookbook (fan guide, Nov 2026, [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/facecard-makeup-showdown-how-to-play-on-roblox-full-guide/)). Makeup Mania gives a theme and a countdown of about 300 seconds (fan guide, Nov 2026, [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/makeup-mania-on-roblox-beginner-guide-to-finishing-a-round/)). The first reward in all three comes at the end of the first round, about five to six minutes in. Money comes from optional passes: 129, 199 and 349 Robux in Facecard and 129 to 799 Robux in Dress To Impress (fan guides, Oct to Nov 2026, [rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/beginners-guide)).

### Why contests drew crowds and care sims did not

No source states a reason. No reviews, comments or traffic data could be read for any of these games. What follows is a reading of ratings, dates and store text.

The plainest split is who the look is for. In every beauty game that passed 10K players the player makes something of their own and other players judge it. In the care sims that stayed small the player serves a scripted customer. The contests also hand out a full loop in one short round: a theme, a deadline, a walk in front of others and a score. The tycoons and job sims describe a slower loop of stations, staff and upgrades.

Dislike is not the cause. **The small care sims are well liked: My Salon Empire 94.4%, Nail Salon Tycoon 94.1%, Sunsilk 91.6%, Skincare Spa 91% and Winter Wonderland 86.8%** (trackers, Nov 2026, table above). Meanwhile contests with poor ratings still spiked: Makeover Show peaked at 10.4K with 64.9% liked and Nails Show at 3.2K with 49.4%. Players who find the care sims enjoy them. The gap looks like reach and the first-day hook, and that is this report's inference, not a sourced finding.

Fresh content tracks with holding. Sources credit Dress To Impress with constant collabs, seasonal updates and new modes (tracker news article, [Rowatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/dress-to-impress-how-a-teen-developer-s-fashion-game-hit-9-7-billion-visits)). Facecard shipped four named updates between Oct 10 and Nov 21, 2026, about one a week (fan guide with some date conflicts, [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/facecard-makeup-showdown-events-schedule/)). Winter Wonderland was last updated about two years ago (tracker). Many spa-named tycoons are copies with no players at all.

Timing helped the makeup wave. Four of the 2026 contests launched on Oct 4 or 5, 2026, which points to studios copying a live trend. Roblox opened an avatar makeup category in March 2026 (official newsroom post, title seen only, [Roblox](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/03/avatar-makeup-launch-roblox-marketplace)). Neither is shown as a cause.

Two facts cut against copying the contest for cats. First, the contests do not hold. Makeup Mania fell from 64.7K to 124 players in about two months, and Facecard's 30-day average was down 57.1% with the game under a tenth of its peak in spite of a 98.5% like ratio (tracker, Nov 2026, [Rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10769516701/facecard-makeup-showdown/dying)). Only Makeup Salon held, with a 7-day average of 5,727 players, down 1.1% (tracker, Nov 2026, [Rotrends](https://www.rotrends.com/game/10769514597/Makeup-Salon)). Second, the pet version has been tried. Choose Ur Pet drew 121.7M visits since 2020 but never passed 2,409 players, and the newer Pet Show had no visible traffic.

Hand treatments have a thin record of their own. Skincare Spa is the one spa-named game that got traffic, and it sells popping, washing and a makeover. A skincare brand's Roblox space built on face cleaning and pimple popping minigames passed 3M visits in under 30 days in early 2025 (developer-reported via press, 2025, [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91335294/gen-alpha-getting-zits-acne-brands-roblox)). Cleaning games built on a similar feel rated 92% to 93% liked and peaked at 63K to 84K players in mid 2026, then fell to under 3% of peak (tracker, Nov 22 to 23, 2026, [RoWatcher Supermarket](https://rowatcher.com/games/10334731049/clean-the-supermarket), [RoWatcher Library](https://rowatcher.com/games/10226701629/clean-the-library)). The verb is liked. Nothing shows it holds players alone.

Five patterns repeat across the games with a known opening. **The player gets one verb and one free thing at once**: a carrot to throw, 20 Cash for a seed, a starter pet, a pickaxe, a rod. **The player owns a place within the first minute**: a pen, a garden, a base, a plot. **Guidance is in the world and not in a text box**: arrows, goal lines, task icons or a quest giver, and no guide describes a cutscene or a long forced tutorial. Some hits have no tutorial at all. **The first thing earned keeps paying**: a hatched pet or placed animal earns each second, which gives a visible number that rises while the player learns the next step. **The first upgrade is cheap and the next is far**: first buys sit at $30 to $1,000 of in-game cash, then prices step up by about 5 to 27 times a tier, such as $30, $150, $4K and $20K for the first four foods in Throw Food For Animals (fan guide, Nov 11, 2026, [Roonby](https://roonby.com/2026/11/11/throw-food-for-animals-beginner-guide-best-upgrades-food-and-money-farming-tips/amp/)). The price ratios are this report's sums from guide prices.

Two smaller points matter for a young audience. Other players are in view from the start in most of these games, as rivals, voters, visitors or co-op partners. And the confusion that guides flag is about where to go and what a button does: buying food in Throw Food For Animals does not equip it, and sell points in Clean The Ballpit! are spread out and hard to find (fan guides, Nov 2026, links in table). No guide describes a forced shop or pass prompt in the first minutes, though no guide was looking for one.

**The guides gave almost no timings.** No source states seconds to first reward, time to first upgrade or the moment of the first shop prompt for any game. The only time figures found are an estimate of 20 to 30 minutes to the first egg in Adopt Me, round lengths of 5 and 6 minutes in Dress To Impress, a 10-second grow time for the first chicken in Steal An Egg and one author's note that their balance reached $654 "within a few minutes". The claim that popular games reach the first reward inside a minute is a fair reading of the step lists. It is not measured.

## Roblox counts bounce at 60 and 180 seconds and sets no D1 target

Roblox's Discovery doc lists the signals behind Home's Recommended for You row. The most important is play-through rate, the share of players who play after seeing the game on Home. **First play bounce rate is a negative signal with two windows: sessions under 60 seconds and sessions of 61 to 180 seconds.** Play days per user are split into Day 1, Day 2 to 7 and Day 8 to 28. Playtime per user is capped at 60 minutes per user per game per day (official, page undated, read Nov 2026, [Discovery docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/discovery.md)). On Jun 15, 2026 Roblox widened the view from 7 days to 28 days (official, [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)). In March 2026 it added a "deep play through rate" and a count of 7-day qualified play sessions, which it says "helps capture whether a game retains users after the first session" (official, 2026, [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571)). Since Jul 29, 2026 gameplay video autoplays on Home, with playtime up by as much as 39% for games with video in tests (official, same DevForum post).

These signals count organic players only. Engagement and retention from players first won through ads are left out of Home ranking, and ads do not lower a game's rank (official, page undated, [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/discovery-faq)). The first three minutes that matter for Home are the ones seen by players who arrive from Home.

### What Roblox tells developers to do in the first session

Roblox's retention doc names three levers for day 1: the core loop, the first-time experience and performance. It says to get players into real play quickly, "ideally in 5 minutes or less" (official, undated, [Retention doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/analytics/retention.md)). Its onboarding pages add detail without numbers. Use prominent in-world visuals in place of pop-up text. Highlight the next target by dimming the screen. Keep a hint on screen until the step is done. Give feedback for every action. Keep early XP thresholds low. Give free starter items but leave a reason to return. Show short, mid and long goals in plain view. End onboarding "with a deliberately designed moment of joy" (official, undated, [Onboarding](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding.md), [Techniques](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques.md)). The mistakes it lists are long prescriptive tutorials, text-heavy instructions, front-loading everything, silent actions and hints that come too early, too late or too often. One example shows the method: in a planting game most players pressed the seed button within 10 seconds, so the highlight was set to appear at 11 seconds. Roblox also points to its Player Funnel, Experiments and Configs tools for testing single tutorial steps.

This lines up with the popular games above, which teach by arrows and goals and let the player act at once. One opinion piece claims drop-off is concentrated in the first 90 seconds and blames loading-screen text, locked first actions and dialogue walls, but it gives no data (opinion, Aug 24, 2026, [Rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/your-first-90-seconds-are-destroying-roblox-retention)). No official source was found on load-time targets, low-end phones, cutscenes or the timing of shop pop-ups.

### No official D1 number, and none at all for spa games

Roblox publishes no "good" day 1 figure. Its benchmarks are relative to similar games shown in a developer's own dashboard and do not feed ranking (official, undated, [Discovery docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/discovery.md)). The only hard numbers found are third-party and old. In GameAnalytics data ending July 2025, **median D1 rose with a game's average session length: 4.31% for games with sessions of 0 to 3 minutes, 7.91% at 7 to 12 minutes, 10.24% at 13 to 18 minutes and 11.46% at 19 to 24 minutes.** The top 5% of games reached about 11% to 25% across those bands, and the median session was 6.62 minutes (tracker, 2025 data, over 12 months old, [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/cn/reports/2025-roblox-report)). One developer with 11% D1 was told by a peer that it was "pretty decent" and to aim for 15% or more (developer-reported, Sep 30, 2024, [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/day-1-retention/3177419)). A blog's claim that games need D1 above about 30% before running ads sits above GameAnalytics' top 5% and has no method behind it (opinion, 2025 to 2026, [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-ads-in-2026-the-minimum-budget-that-actually-works)).

**No day 1 retention figure was found for any spa, salon, makeover or pet care game.** The single nearby data point is an average session of 13.0 minutes for Makeup Salon (tracker, Nov 2026, [Rolearn](https://rolearn.dev/games/10769514597/)). That band had a median D1 near 10% in the 2025 data, but this is a link between two unrelated sources and not a measure of that game.

### What brings players back on day 2 in the popular games

The return hooks that could be verified fall into four kinds. All come from fan guides or fan wikis unless marked.

Things that finish while the player is away are the most common in the current hits. Grow a Garden's icon says "Grows Offline" and its developer said offline growth brings players back to see what grew (developer quote via opinion newsletter, May 27, 2025, [GameDiscover](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)). Animals placed at a base in Throw Food For Animals keep earning while the player is gone (fan guide, Nov 1, 2026, [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/articles/throw-food-for-animals-beginners-guide)). Steal An Egg shows a line such as "You earn $16M/Day offline!" and eggs that grow over time (fan guide, Aug 27, 2026, [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/steal-an-egg-how-to-make-eggs-grow-faster-in-roblox/)).

Daily gifts are the mark of the long-lived games. Adopt Me pays 25, 50, 100 and 200 Bucks on days 1 to 4 and a gift on day 5 (fan wiki, undated, [Adopt Me wiki](https://adoptme.fandom.com/wiki/Bucks)). It also runs Star Reward Coins, one per login day with no need for days in a row, ending in an egg at 28 coins (fan guide, Sep 10, 2026, [Roonby](https://roonby.com/2026/09/10/star-reward-coins-adopt-me-guide/amp/)). The developer's support page ties Star Rewards to a streak that resets on a missed day, so the two sources conflict or describe two systems (official support page, undated, [Adopt Me support](https://support.playadopt.me/support/solutions/articles/153000194015-daily-log-in-streak-faq)). Dress To Impress gives 15 Cash on day 1 and 15 more each day after, plus daily tasks (fan guide, Oct 25, 2026, [rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/beginners-guide)). 99 Nights in the Forest gives three daily quests that refresh every 24 hours (fan guide, Nov 14, 2025, [Roonby](https://roonby.com/2025/11/14/all-daily-quests-how-to-finish-them-in-99-nights-in-the-forest/)).

Near goals left open at the end of a session are the third kind: an animal index that pays a bonus for a full set in Throw Food For Animals, a rank ladder in Dress To Impress and an egg the player is saving for in Adopt Me. Fresh content on a clock is the fourth: a weekly boutique every Saturday at 11 AM Eastern in Dress To Impress and Saturday updates in Grow a Garden in 2025. That fourth kind is the studio's day 7 tool.

The spa and salon games use the weaker forms. My Salon Empire lists daily rewards, playtime gifts and mystery boxes, and several pet games lean on codes and group-join gifts (fan guide, Sep 9, 2026, [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/how-to-run-your-salon-in-my-salon-empire-roblox/); official game page, read Nov 2026, [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/14895218677)). None of them shows an earn-while-away loop or a collection in the sources read.

**No number shows that any of these hooks lifts day 1 retention.** No D1 or D7 figure was found for Grow a Garden, Adopt Me or any other game named here. The case for offline progress rests on a developer quote and one newsletter's opinion.

The rules found leave room for gentle hooks. Roblox ships its own daily reward package with a streak setting and states no limits on it (official, undated, [Engagement rewards](https://create.roblox.com/docs/resources/feature-packages/engagement-rewards)). Its notification rules ban "false time pressure" and bait-and-switch rewards, allow one notification per user per day per game and send under-13 players only experience update notices (official, updated Nov 20, 2026, [Notifications](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-notifications.md)). For a young audience that last rule closes most of the notification route, so the reason to return has to be set inside the first session. Hooks that reward a return and take nothing away for a missed day fit every rule found. Adopt Me's coin track and a Dress To Impress advent calendar that lets players pick up where they left off are the gentlest examples seen (fan guides, Sep 2026 and Dec 2025, [Beebom](https://beebom.com/dress-to-impress-advent-calendar/)).

## Outside Roblox, hands-on toys and no-timer cleaning fit young players best

Spa and salon games outside Roblox split into six types. Scale differs widely and download figures were found for almost none of the spa titles.

Hands-on creative toys are the best fit for a young all-ages crowd. **Toca Hair Salon 4** lets children wash, towel, blow dry, cut, regrow, dye and style, with characters that react with faces and sounds and a photo booth that saves the result. Reviewers rate it for ages 5 and up (opinion, undated, [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/toca-hair-salon-4)). What to borrow: every tool is a direct hand action, the customer reacts at once, there is no fail state and the session ends in a photo. What to avoid: reviewers call the tools finicky for small fingers and note there is no undo.

No-timer cleaning is the proof that visible progress alone can carry a game. PowerWash Simulator passed 12 million players by Apr 22, 2024 (official, [FuturLab](https://www.futurlab.co.uk/news/thank-you-for-12-million-powerwash-simulator-players)). Its makers credit instant visual progress, a choice of tools and no timers or enemies, with pressure offered only as an optional mode (developer-reported, 2021, [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/powerwash-sim-devs-on-making-cleaning-fun-advanced-cro-1847319502)).

Makeover puzzle games earn the most. Project Makeover led US fashion games on iOS at about $1M to $1.2M a week in late 2025 (tracker estimate, Q4 2025, [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-ios-top-5-fashion-games-revenue-us-6011f531241bc16eb85bc2b8)). Its hook is a before and after reveal: a plain, unhappy character who "sparkles" after the work. Reviewers fault it for tying looks to self-worth and for lives that refill on a 20-minute timer (opinion, undated, [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/project-makeover)). A cat can carry the reveal without that message, since a muddy cat made fluffy says nothing about a child's looks.

Time management spa games supply the station list. Sally's Spa has the player drag customers between a sauna, a massage table and a mani-pedi chair, earn tips and unlock new cities, with a 4+ age rating and 12K ratings at 4.7 (official store listing, read Nov 2026, [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sallys-spa-beauty-salon-game/id315733642)). Recent reviews on the same page complain of difficulty spikes, costly helpers and timers that push purchases.

Idle animal shops are the closest match to the cat spa theme. A mobile game named Cat Spa has cat staff run a massage parlor, with rooms to unlock and decorate and customer snapshots and stories to collect (official store listing, read Nov 2026, [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/cat-spa/id1543729168)). Hamster Inn starts with an empty inn and fast early income, and its reviewer praises the balance of active and idle play but faults thin offline income (opinion, undated, [PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/hamster-inn/review/)). Capybara Spa pairs feeding with hot springs and has 1K ratings at 4.7 (official store listing, read Nov 2026, [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1590870993)). Neko Atsume, with visiting cats, a photo album and no end, passed 10M downloads by Dec 4, 2015 (secondary source, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Atsume)).

Cozy management with a bath house is the praised mix. Spirittea has the player scrub spirits, wash towels, stoke boilers and seat guests who get along, with 84% positive from 683 Steam reviews (official store page, read Nov 2026, [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1931010/Spirittea)). The complaints in its reviews are about logistics such as running out of towels, not about the washing, and hired helpers are the fix (opinion, 2023, [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/xbox/spirittea-review)).

Virtual pets add the real-time need. In Nintendogs the dogs get hungrier and dirtier on a real clock and the player brushes and washes them by touch. It sold 23.96M copies (secondary source, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendogs)).

No source ranked single treatments such as washing, brushing, massage or masks by how satisfying players find them. No source gave day 1 retention for any of these games. The complaints that repeat are timers, lives, costly helpers and paid packs, which are the same pressure tactics a young all-ages game on Roblox would want to stay clear of.

### Five sub-genre options and what backs each

**Hands-on treatment sim.** The player washes, brushes and dries each cat by hand, as in Toca Hair Salon, Skincare Spa and My Salon Empire. It gives the fastest first verb and matches Roblox's advice to let players act at once. Its Roblox record is thin: one spa game that drew 23M visits and faded and no proof of holding.

**Wash and reveal with a collect and earn shell.** Each cleaned cat joins a collection and earns while the player is away, as placed animals do in Throw Food For Animals. This joins the liked cleaning verb to the day 2 hook that today's all-ages hits share. The risks are a crowded shell and the fast fade seen in cleaning games.

**Makeover contest with cats.** Players style a cat to a theme in a timed round and vote. This is the one beauty format with proven crowds, and it puts the first reward five to six minutes in. Two pet versions exist and neither charted, the human versions fade within weeks and public voting among young players adds social risk.

**Spa tycoon or idle management.** The player buys stations, hires cat staff and decorates rooms, as in Winter Wonderland and the Cat Spa mobile game. It supplies long-run goals and decor. As the core loop it has the weakest record on Roblox: no title above 3K players in five years.

**A mix led by hands-on treatment.** The evidence leans here. The first minutes that work on Roblox are one verb with instant feedback, which hands-on washing gives. The day 2 hooks that work are a collection with open slots and earnings that build while away, which the collect shell gives. Decor and staff from the tycoon type serve as slower goals, and a cat show serves better as an event than as the core. This is a reading across separate proofs. **No game found has all of these parts together, on Roblox or off it, and nothing shows the mix holds players.**

One rule bounds any spa layout. Roblox treats social hangouts with private spaces such as bedrooms or bathroom stalls as 18+ only, while games where roleplay is central are handled apart (official, undated, [Content maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/content-maturity)). How treatment rooms are classed needs a check in the maturity questionnaire.

## Conclusion

The record does not say that spa games fail on Roblox. It says that one way of building them has been tried many times and stayed small: a station tycoon or job sim in which the player serves scripted customers and returns only for codes and daily coins. The two things that have worked nearby are different in kind. Beauty games scaled when the player's own creation was judged by peers, and cozy all-ages games scaled when the first thing earned kept paying and a collection stayed half full. A cat spa built on hand treatments has never been paired with either on Roblox, so the idea is closer to untested than to disproven.

That shifts where the risk sits. The first five minutes are the better understood half: Roblox's two bounce windows, its onboarding advice and the openings of the current hits all point the same way. Day 2 is the open question, and it is the one no outside source can answer for this genre. The useful numbers will be the studio's own from the first test build: the share of Home players who leave before 60 and before 180 seconds, the step in the funnel where they leave and the share who return the next day, read against medians of about 4% to 11% by session length in the only benchmark found.
