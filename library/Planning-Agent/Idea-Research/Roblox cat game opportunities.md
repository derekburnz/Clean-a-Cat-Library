# Cats draw clicks, loops hold players

A cat spa has room on Roblox but no proof of demand. As of November 22, 2026, no cat spa or pet spa game with real players was found (tracker pages, read Nov 22 to 23, 2026), and the nearest tries, pet salon and vet sims, have stayed tiny for years. The charts are led by steal-and-collect games, with ragdoll physics, cleaning games and one co-op river trip rising while most older mid-chart games fall. Cats do pull traffic: two new cat games each passed 129M visits within about 10 weeks of launch (trackers, Nov 2026), but both sit near 74% liked and have lost about 90% of their peak players. A strong click rate on a cat spa ad shows interest in the picture, not in a spa loop, and no source found links ad clicks to players who stay. The less crowded lanes that cats fit are a wash-and-reveal pet game, a co-op trip and a cozy collect-and-decorate game, and the last of these is empty in a way that looks more like low demand than a gap. Every fast wave measured here lost 90% or more of its peak within weeks to months. The evidence is tracker counts and like ratios only: no retention, revenue or search data was found for any cat or spa game.

## Steal games lead while ragdoll, cleaning and a river trip rise

Roblox is smaller than a year ago, so new games fight over fewer hours. **Daily players were 138M in Q3 2026, down 9% year on year, and hours were down 15%** (official figures as reported by GGRecon, Nov 2, 2026, [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/roblox-daily-players-fall-q3-2026/)). New games still break in fast. On Nov 18, 2026, **30 of the 99 games on one chart had been created in October or November 2026 and held 16% of players** (tracker, [GGRecon, Nov 19, 2026](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/roblox-top-games-didnt-exist-before-october/)).

The top of the chart for the week to Nov 16, 2026, by average players, was Steal An Egg at 732K, Brookhaven at 316K, Throw Food For Animals at 267K, Adopt Me at 243K, Blox Fruits at 242K and 99 Nights in the Forest at 145K (tracker, [RoStats](https://rostats.io/charts)). Stealing from other players is the biggest trend and is ruled out here by the studio's own rules. The all-ages game that matters most is Throw Food For Animals: created Sep 18, 2026, it still held third place about eight weeks later.

The rising wave in week 47 (data through Nov 21, 2026) is clear. Crash and ragdoll physics games climbed: BloxNG reached 32,321 average players, up 298% week on week, and Ragdoll Soccer 43,476, up 66%. A co-op trip game, 100 Miles Down the River, jumped to 28,611, up 3,660%. A cleaning game, **Clean The Ballpit!, reached 19,942, up 431%** (tracker, [RoStats week 47](https://rostats.io/charts/2026-w47)). In the same week the older mid-chart fell: DONT SPLASH down 43%, Forsaken down 35%, Steal a Brainrot down 33%, Break and Steal an Egg down 13% and Fisch down 12% (same source). Some of that drop is Halloween events ending, so it is partly seasonal.

Counting only the games on the charts that could be read, which is a floor and not a census, the types sort like this. Egg and steal games, "+1" games, sports and the throw-or-shoot-for-animals shell are crowded: at least four egg copies and three to four animal-collect copies showed up within about two months. Ragdoll physics is filling fast, with four chart games created between Oct 13 and Nov 5, 2026 (tracker, [GGRecon, Nov 19, 2026](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/roblox-top-games-didnt-exist-before-october/)). Cleaning and co-op trips look early, with one or two rising leaders each in the latest week. Fishing is flat to falling.

Roblox names no genre it wants more of. It did move its Home feed from 7-day to 28-day retention signals on Jun 15, 2026 (official, [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)), and on Mar 9, 2026 it began curating "novel games", which it defines as new mechanics, distinctive visual styles or underrepresented genres (official, [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571)). A game that holds players for a month is now worth more on Home than one that spikes for a week.

## Recent waves lost 90% or more of peak within weeks to months

The fade is fast and it hits well-liked games too. The percent figures below are this report's own sums from the tracker counts cited.

Cleaning is the closest match to a spa or wash game, and it shows the pattern most plainly. Clean the Supermarket was released in June 2026, peaked at 63K players on Jul 5, 2026 and had 384 to 493 players in late November, about 1% of peak, with 92.9% liked (tracker, read Nov 22 to 23, 2026, [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10334731049/clean-the-supermarket)). Clean The Library peaked at 84K in June 2026 and held about 2K, with 92.7% liked (tracker, same dates, [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10226701629/clean-the-library)). Wash The House peaked at 69K in August 2026 and held 362, with 93.0% liked (tracker, same dates, [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10405010493/wash-the-house)). **Players liked these games and still left within two to five months.** Clean All the Leaves, created Jul 21, 2026, had about 86,800 players on Aug 28, 2026 (tracker figure as reported by [Player.One](https://www.player.one/clean-all-leaves-suddenly-one-robloxs-biggest-games-why-are-players-raking-leaves-163958)); its current count was not read.

Meme and copy games fell harder. Steal a Brainrot launched in May 2025, passed 25M concurrent players in October 2025 (press report of tracker data, [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players)) and averaged 46,904 in week 47 of 2026, more than 99% below peak (tracker, [RoStats week 47](https://rostats.io/charts/2026-w47)). Garden Horizons launched Feb 22, 2026, peaked above 1M and was near 23K by early March 2026, a fall of about 98% in two weeks (press report, [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/garden-horizons-player-count-drop-what-happened-and-is-the-game-dead/)). DONT SPLASH, created in mid October 2026, was already falling 43% a week about five weeks after launch.

Two kinds of game held. 99 Nights in the Forest, a co-op survival game, still averaged 145K in the week to Nov 16, 2026 long after its peak (tracker, [RoStats](https://rostats.io/charts)). Old cat roleplay games hold small steady crowds for years, as the next section shows. No source read explains why any single game faded or held, and no weekly player series was found for Steal An Egg, Fisch or Grow a Garden, so this is a pattern from a handful of cases and not a measured rule.

## Cats pull visits fast, but the new cat hits sit near 74% liked

Only four cat games have real crowds today, and no cat cafe, cat tycoon or cat care game is among them. The list below is what turned up in searches, since no tracker's full "cat" listing could be pulled.

The other rows come from the Rotrends pages for [7 Days Cat-Sitting](https://rotrends.com/game/10766506199), [Warrior Cats](https://rotrends.com/game/1278035280/Warrior-Cats-Ultimate-Edition), [Kitten Game](https://rotrends.com/game/4689722433/Kitten-Game), [Save Your Cat](https://rotrends.com/game/10290366643) and [Cat Invasion](https://rotrends.com/game/9419242500/Cat-Invasion).

Three things stand out. First, a cat on the icon can bring a very large first wave: Cat Battles was built in about three days (developer-reported) and Roblox featured it in a standout new games roundup on Nov 16, 2026 (official, as reported by [Player.One, Nov 18, 2026](https://www.player.one/robloxs-cat-battles-was-built-three-days-roblox-assistant-players-are-still-shaping-its-164531)). Second, that wave did not stay. **Cat Battles is about 95% below its peak seven weeks after launch and 7 Days Cat-Sitting about 88% below after ten.** Both sit near 74% liked, against 91% to 93% for the old roleplay games. Third, a high like ratio alone did not save the small ones: Cat Invasion has 98.1% liked and about 50 players.

The only cat cafe tycoon found, by a small first-time group, had no running servers when read (official game page, read Nov 22 to 23, 2026, [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/100285104400469)). So the studio's own lane, cat hospitality, is empty of strong rivals. No player reviews or comments for any cat game could be read, so why the new hits rate lower is not known.

Outside Roblox, the cat games that earn most are idle, merge and match games. In Q4 2025 in the US, The Battle Cats led at about $289K to $310K a week and Cats & Soup was fourth at about $57K to $61K (tracker estimate, [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-cat-games-revenue-us-608810b928293296047965a0)). Cats & Soup had 60M downloads by Sep 13, 2024 (official, publisher figure via [PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/cats-soup/60m-downloads/)). None of the top five is a spa game. The cat is the wrapper and the loop does the work.

## The cat spa lane is empty and nothing found shows players want it

**Is there room?** Yes. The only two pet spa or salon games found on Roblox are a Pet Spa released in October 2026 with 0 visits (tracker, [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10769134433/pet-spa)) and Winter Wonderland Pet Salon Tycoon, created in February 2022, with 29.3M visits in total and an all-time peak of 54 players (tracker, read Nov 22 to 23, 2026, [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/3362869464/winter-wonderland-pet-salon-tycoon)). Roblox's own search could not be read, so very small games were likely missed.

**Is there demand?** Not shown either way. Three facts point to weak demand for the plain version. The one pet salon tycoon never charted in almost five years. The pet care list is one giant and a long tail: Adopt Me had 317.9K players and every other game on the first page had under 4K, with no spa, salon or grooming title on it (tracker, read Nov 22 to 23, 2026, [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/lists/top-trending-roblox-pet-care-games)). Human salon tycoons are small too: Beauty Salon Tycoon, released January 2026, peaked at 809 players (tracker, [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/9531004126/beauty-salon-tycoon-upd)). Washing and grooming have so far worked on Roblox as side features, inside Adopt Me and in My Cat Box's cat wash update of December 2020 (developer press release, [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/press-release/popular-my-cat-box-roblox-title-gets-cat-washing-update-improved-clothing-and-more-added-to-their-game)).

Three facts point the other way. Cats bring traffic, as shown above. Cleaning verbs rate 92% to 93% liked and reached peaks of 63K to 87K in 2026. And outside Roblox, PowerWash Simulator passed 12M players by Apr 22, 2024 (official, [FuturLab](https://www.futurlab.co.uk/news/thank-you-for-12-million-powerwash-simulator-players)), with its makers crediting instant visible progress and no timers (developer-reported, [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/powerwash-sim-devs-on-making-cleaning-fun-advanced-cro-1847319502)). A mobile game named Cat Spa already runs the exact theme as an idle sim. It is small where measured, with 419 ratings at a 4.6 average in one storefront (store listing, read Nov 2026, [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ph/app/cat-spa/id1543729168)). No download or revenue data was found for any pet grooming or pet spa app.

**What the ad result does and does not show.** The notes hold no numbers from the studio's own cat spa ad, so this report cannot size it. Roblox's Ads Manager reports spend, impressions, clicks, plays, cost per play, playtime and earnings, and its docs list no retention figure for ad players (official, page undated, read Nov 2026, [Roblox docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/sponsoring-experiences)). No official click rate benchmark was found. One developer reported 2.6% to 2.7% on game thumbnails on Jul 27, 2026 (developer-reported, [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/need-advice-on-my-games-ad-performance-and-next-steps/4760265)) and one blog calls 1% to 2% realistic (opinion, page dated Jan 2025 and edited Jun 2026, [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-ads-in-2026-the-minimum-budget-that-actually-works)). The fair test of "did well" is the studio's other ads in the same weeks. **No source found shows that a high click rate predicts retention.** One developer reported that ad players stayed 11 to 12 minutes against 28 to 30 minutes for others and returned next day at about 3% against 5% to 8% (developer-reported, one shooter game, Aug 2026, [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-ads-traffic-degrades-the-engagement-metrics-used-for-discovery-causing-paid-acquisition-to-harm-organic-ranking/4838351)). Roblox also states that play from ad-acquired users is left out of Home ranking signals and that ads do not lower a game's rank (official, page undated, [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/discovery-faq)). Ad traffic can get a game considered for Home, but its rank there rests on organic players only (official, page undated, [Discovery docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/discovery.md)).

**The risks, in order of weight.** Demand for a spa loop is unproven and the past plain versions stayed tiny. Format matters more than theme: the old Pressure Wash Simulator has 141.1M visits since 2021 but an all-time peak of 115 players (tracker, [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/2694265705/pressure-wash-simulator)), while the 2026 cleaning hits use a short collect, sell and upgrade loop. That short loop is the one that fades to about 1% of peak in months. The recent job-sim hits lean on a horror layer, which is off the table here: Sell Fries! had 13.3K players in November 2026 with night-time threats (tracker and fan wiki, [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/sell-fries)) and Animal Hospital peaked at 736,948 players on Jul 5, 2026 as survival horror (tracker figure via press, [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78741-animal-hospital-becomes-roblox-s-latest-horror-sensation-here-s-everything-you-need-to-know)). No cozy-only job sim was found charting. The name "Cat Spa" is taken on mobile and no trademark search was done. Last, Roblox's maturity rules treat social hangouts with private spaces such as bathroom stalls as 18+ only, though games where roleplay is central are handled apart (official, [Roblox docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/promotion/content-maturity)); spa rooms need a check in the questionnaire to keep a Minimal or Mild label.

The rules the studio has set do not block the idea. The usual money model in this tier is plain boost passes, such as 170 and 180 Robux in Sell Fries! and 199 and 349 Robux in Cat Battles (fan wikis, Nov 2026, [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/cat-battles)), with no paid random items needed.

The spa idea can be carried three ways: as a stand-alone spa job sim, as the theme on a wash-and-reveal loop or as a real spa room inside the current cat cafe first. The third is the only one that lets a spa ad run against a game that truly holds a spa. An ad for a feature the game lacks risks a rejected ad and a poor play-through, though Roblox's ad standards text could not be read.

## Five other lanes cats fit, from early to empty

Figures and sources for this table are in the sections above and the text below.

| Lane | Stage on Roblox | Proof today | How cats fit | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wash and reveal pets | Cleaning verb is live; washing pets is unused | Cleaning games peak high and rate well; collect-and-earn shell holds third place | Muddy cats lead the pet list | Cleaning waves fade in months; collect copies pile up |
| Co-op trip | Early, one new riser | One long holder and one new riser | A band of cats on a shared trip | Hardest build; needs friends; the holder leans on fear |
| Animal collect by throwing or shooting | Filling fast | Leader held about eight weeks | Cats as the animals to collect | Three to four copies in two months |
| Physics comedy | Mid, copies arriving | Four chart games in three weeks | Cat Battles is already here | Netcode; copies; low likes on the cat entry |
| Cozy collect and decorate | Empty | None on Roblox; proven on mobile | Visiting cats, an album and a yard | Empty looks like low demand |

**Wash and reveal pets** was the pick of the earlier shortlist, under the working name Wash Muddy Pets. The new evidence cuts both ways. The proof that a cleaning verb can chart is no longer one game: at least four cleaning games reached 63K players or more in 2026. But "a verb nobody is using" no longer holds for washing in general, since Wash The House peaked at 69K, and the template's fade is now measured. What stays unused is washing pets with a reveal, joined to the collect, place and earn shell that Throw Food For Animals proves for all ages (94% liked, tracker game page read Nov 22, 2026 per the earlier snapshot; the exact link was not kept, tracker home at [Rotrends](https://rotrends.com)). That shell is itself being copied: Shoot Arrows For Animals! had 22,280 players and 98.8% liked (tracker, read about Nov 22, 2026, [rblxdb](https://rblxdb.com/hidden-gems)).

**Co-op trips** are the earliest lane with a long holder. 100 Miles Down the River was created Oct 12, 2026 and reached the top 40 in week 47 (tracker, [RoStats week 47](https://rostats.io/charts/2026-w47)). The case rests on one week of growth for the new game and on 99 Nights, which is a survival game with fear in it. It is also the hardest build: the earlier shortlist judged shared physics with friends the hardest thing to get right on Roblox.

**Cozy collect and decorate** has the best fit with the studio's crowd and the weakest proof. The model works on mobile: Neko Atsume passed 10M downloads by Dec 4, 2015 with visiting cats, a photo album and no end game (secondary source, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neko_Atsume)). On Roblox the nearest finds are tiny: Find and Pet Cats had 35 players (tracker, read Nov 22 to 23, 2026, [Rotrends](https://www.rotrends.com/game/10241758126/Find-and-Pet-Cats-270)) and a cozy coloring game launched Jun 14, 2026 had 28 (tracker, [Rotrends](https://www.rotrends.com/game/10329989899/Tap-to-Paint-Cozy-Coloring)). No cozy game launched since July 2026 was found holding a steady crowd for a month. Here an empty lane most likely means low demand or weak reach on Home, not a missed gap.

One more tier is worth a note. Small restaurant and food games rate very high but stay small: Run a Restaurant! had 98.7% liked with 4,427 players (tracker, read about Nov 22, 2026, [rblxdb](https://rblxdb.com/hidden-gems)). That is the tier a cat cafe sim lives in, and it shows a well-liked hospitality game can still sit at a few thousand players.

## Conclusion

The theme question and the loop question come apart. The ad result and the cat hits answer the theme question: cats, and likely a cat spa picture, win the first tap. Nothing found answers the loop question, and that is the one Roblox's 28-day signals reward. Spa and wash-and-reveal are near neighbors, so the choice is less "spa or not" than which loop sits under the cats: a slow hospitality sim like the old salon tycoons that stayed tiny, or a short clean-and-collect loop that charts and then fades. The cleaning games show that a 93% like ratio does not stop a fall to 1% of peak, so a plan for month two matters as much as the first minute.

The cheapest missing evidence is the studio's own. Plays, playtime and next-day return from the cat spa ad, set against its other ads in the same weeks, would say more than any tracker. A Roblox search for "cat spa" and "pet spa", a trademark check on the name and a read of the DevForum threads on ad benchmarks and ad standards that could not be opened would close the main gaps. Roblox's 2026 year-end search report is due in December and is the next official signal on what players look for.
