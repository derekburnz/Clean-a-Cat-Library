## What we do not take, and why

- A starter pack offer on the main screen from the first minute (2), a 2x cash offer under the quests (1) and a plus button beside the cash that sells cash (2). Our rule: no offer on screen in the first session and coins are never sold.
- A limited pack with an Only 82 left counter (4). A countdown of stock is pressure on a young player, which our no dark patterns rule forbids.
- A paid pet with +1000% cash and a paid All-Star Staff that maxes every worker (4, 6). Ours sells nothing that skips the game.
- Cash for a like and for a favourite, and a like goal that pays everyone (3, 8). By our reading of Roblox's rules, rewards for likes and favourites are not allowed, so we do not copy it even though they do it.
- Pets that follow you only as a cash bonus, with the first one sold in the shop (9). Ours are strays you wash and keep for free.
- Customer name tags that look like player names (2, 12, 16). Our customers have names we wrote.
- All four menu buttons on screen from the first second (16). Our pick stays one at a time, and this is Derek's call on plan page question `hud`.

## Things to check with Derek or in a playtest

With the bench bought, can you choose which waiting pet goes first, or only take the next one? Added to the optional ten more minutes of My Pet Salon on the plan page.
- A reward for joining the group (3). Derek's group already has players. It might suit us, but Roblox's rules have to be checked first. For Growth-Agent, after the game is picked.
- The rarity of the pet is on the card before the wash (11). Ours hides the coat until the wash is done. That difference is on purpose and is the heart of our collection.
