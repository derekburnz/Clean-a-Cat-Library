Planning-Agent's reading:
- Liked: several kinds of step, praise words with a growing combo, a star rating with a coins and experience breakdown after each pet, tool upgrades, an index, rare customers with rare pets and a lasting reward, hired helpers, clean pop-ups, the half dirty and half clean store picture.
- Disliked: an unskippable cutscene, the same swipe three times, 16 steps in one job, the pet turning by itself, only one customer at a time.
- His lean (an idea, not a decision): the player washes by hand, hired helpers take customers too but slowly, several customers wait so the player picks who is next, and maybe customers who wait too long leave.
- The first fun thing took about 75 seconds there. Our draft puts a cat under the finger at second 0.

## Capybara Onsen (played on November 23, on an iPad)
Plan page answers: you buy the pet. First fun thing at about 40 seconds. No real washing. Timers: short waits, fine.

Derek's notes:
- "game was one of those ones where you step on pads to buy things (capybaras in this case and then can merge once you buy 3) they produce food to collect from a pad then deposit at another pad and collect the money the fruit sold gets turned into… not quite a spa game, tutorial was simple buy your first guy free was the normal transparent arrow yellow lines towards it then collect 3 food sell it watch it turn into money collect money buy 3 guys then merge them and tutorial done."
- "asked for favourite abt 4 min in."
- "(used ipad) Had simple ui small settings/invite button top right bigger more centered home and index on top middle , food amount bottom middle, capybara count bottom right, money bottom left and 2x money on left side also a little icon with 0% that would change if friends joins prob"

Planning-Agent's reading:
- Not a spa game: a step on pads, buy and merge loop with a spa theme. Little to learn for the washing.
- Useful: a short tutorial with a free first buy, a friend bonus shown as a percent on the screen from the start, and a plain screen layout on a tablet.
- It asks for a favourite at about minute 4. Our draft has no like or favourite request in the first 5 minutes.
