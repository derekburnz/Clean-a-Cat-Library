# Game idea shortlist, version 2: cat games (November 22, 2026 ET, Planning-Agent, status: waiting for Derek's pick)

Derek asked for a deeper look at what is trending and what kind of cat game could do well, with a cat spa as his lead idea. This replaces the first shortlist. The research behind it: [Idea-Research/Roblox cat game opportunities.md](Idea-Research/Roblox%20cat%20game%20opportunities.md) and the chart snapshot [Idea-Research/roblox-snapshot-2026-11.md](Idea-Research/roblox-snapshot-2026-11.md).

**Recommended pick: idea 1, a cat spa, built so the hands-on pampering is the first minute and the spa you own and the cats you collect are the long game.**

## What the deep dive found, in plain words
- **Nobody owns cozy cat games on Roblox.** No cat spa, cat care or cat cafe game with a real audience turned up on the trackers. The only pet salon tycoon found (from 2022) never charted. That is room, but it can also mean low demand. Derek's ad is the only demand signal so far.
- **Cats win the first tap but the new cat hits did not hold.** Cat Battles (cat sumo, October 2026) and 7 Days Cat-Sitting (September 2026) each passed 129M visits within about ten weeks and are now 88 to 95% below their peaks. Both sit near 74% liked, low for a hit.
- **Cat games that let you be a cat hold for years.** Warrior Cats: Ultimate Edition (2019) and Kitten Game (2023) each still hold about 5K to 7K players, with 91 to 93% liked and sessions near 28 minutes for Warrior Cats.
- **Cleaning games are loved and then dropped.** The summer 2026 wave (Clean the Library, Clean the Supermarket, Wash The House) reached 63K to 84K players each with 92 to 93% liked, and each is now at about 2% of its peak or less. Clean The Ballpit! is the new riser, up about 430% in a week. Lesson for a spa: washing is a great first minute and a poor reason to stay.
- **What is early and not crowded:** friends on a shared journey (100 Miles Down the River jumped into the top 40 with one clear leader) and a second cleaning wave. **Filling up:** throw food to collect animals (three or four copies two months after the first) and ragdoll physics (four charting games in three weeks). **Crowded or falling:** steal and collect, plus one incrementals, sports and horror after Halloween.
- **Roblox now rewards games that hold players for a month.** Home recommendations moved from 7 day to 28 day retention signals on June 15, 2026 (official). A quick spike helps less than it did.
- **Cozy shop games are liked but small.** The human salon tycoons found are tiny, and a well liked restaurant game (Run a Restaurant!, 98.7% liked) sits near 4K players. The recent job sims that charted added a horror layer, which we will not do. So a slow spa tycoon alone is the weak version of the idea.
- **An ad that wins clicks proves the picture, not the game.** No source shows that a high click rate predicts players staying, and Roblox leaves ad players out of its Home ranking signals. The cat spa ad tells us the idea makes people tap. Whether they stay is what the first build must prove.
- **Outside Roblox the cozy cat formula is proven:** Cats & Soup passed 60M downloads (official, September 2024) and Neko Atsume built a long life on cats that visit and a photo book. PowerWash Simulator (12M players, official, April 2024) shows why washing feels good: instant visible progress, no timers and no enemies.

## Idea 1: Cat spa (recommended). Working title: Creampuff Cat Spa
- **Hook:** muddy, tangled, grumpy cats walk into your spa and you turn each one into a fluffy purring beauty with your own hands.
- **30 second loop:** a cat hops in the tub, you scrub off the mud and brush out the fluff, the cat does a happy reveal and pays coins, and coins buy new stations and decor for your spa.
- **First 20 seconds:** you spawn in your own little spa with a muddy cat already in the tub and a sponge in hand. You drag to scrub and by second 15 a clean white kitten shakes off and purrs.
- **The long game:** cats who love their visit become regulars with a page in your Cat Book. You unlock stations (bubble bath, brushing, blow dry, pawdicure), grow and decorate the spa, and a different visiting cat turns up each day.
- **Reference for mechanics:** PowerWash Simulator for the scrubbing feel, Neko Atsume for the book of cats who visit, and Throw Food For Animals for how fast the first reward lands.
- **Riskiest part:** holding players after the first week. Washing alone fades fast on Roblox, so the build order is scrub feel first, then the Cat Book and the spa you own straight after.
- **Size:** M for a first playable (one tub, one brush, ten cats, the Cat Book and saving). The full four station spa is L and comes later.
- **Why this one:** it is the only idea backed by Derek's own ad result, the space is empty, it fits the Cat Cafe crowd and the Creampuff name, and owning and decorating a spa suits the 28 day retention that Roblox now rewards.
- **Name note:** a small mobile game is already called Cat Spa, so ours needs its own name with the words cat spa still in it for search. Creampuff Cat Spa is the working title.

## Idea 2: Be a cat and knock things over. Working title: Knock It Off, Cat!
- **Hook:** you are a cat alone in a house and everything on every shelf is yours to push off.
- **30 second loop:** swipe a mug off the counter, it smashes for fish treats, chain smashes for a combo, and treats unlock new rooms and new cat looks.
- **First 20 seconds:** you spawn as a kitten on a kitchen counter next to a glass of water. One tap tips it and it smashes.
- **Reference for mechanics:** Kitten Game for playing as a cat in a house and Hyper Realistic Ragdoll for why silly physics gets shared.
- **Riskiest part:** a fast spike and a fast fall, like Cat Battles. Many breakable objects are also heavy for cheap phones.
- **Size:** M.
- **Why it could work:** being a cat is the one cat formula that has held players for years on Roblox, physics comedy is trending and a paw pushing a vase is a strong icon.
- **Not checked yet:** whether Roblox already has a game like this. An older indie PC game used the same idea, so only the mechanic would be borrowed.

## Idea 3: Kittens on a journey with friends. Working title: Kittens' Way Home
- **Hook:** you and your friends are lost kittens crossing a giant backyard and house to get home before dark.
- **30 second loop:** reach the next obstacle, team up to cross it (stack up, push a box, distract the dog), rest at a sunny windowsill and collect yarn to unlock skills for the next run.
- **First 20 seconds:** you spawn as a kitten on a garden wall with friends beside you and pounce across a row of flower pots.
- **Reference for mechanics:** 100 Miles Down the River and 99 Nights in the Forest, without the horror.
- **Riskiest part:** it needs hand-built stretches and friends who show up. That is a lot of content for a first game.
- **Size:** L. Smaller version underneath: one five minute stretch for two to four kittens (M).
- **Why it could work:** shared journeys are the least crowded rising type and playing with friends is a signal Roblox ranks on.

## Three ways to do the spa idea
- **A. A new game with a short hands-on loop** (wash, fluff, reveal, collect) and the spa you own as the long game. This is idea 1 and the recommended way.
- **B. A slow spa tycoon** in the style of the old salon tycoons. Not recommended: every one found stayed tiny.
- **C. A spa room added to Cat Cafe first** as a cheap test of demand. It is the only way a spa ad can honestly run before the new game exists. Worth doing only if Derek wants proof before a new build.

## Why the cat spa over the others
- It is the only one with first-hand evidence from Derek's own ads.
- It is the smallest and safest build, with no heavy physics and no need for friends.
- Its long game (a spa you own, cats you collect) matches what Roblox now rewards. Ideas 2 and 3 are closer to trend games that spike.
- Honest weak spots: the empty space may mean low demand, washing games fade fast and ad clicks do not prove players stay. The plan answers these by testing the scrub feel in the first two days and reading day 1 and day 7 return rates before building the full spa.

## Pressure test of the cat spa
- **Fun inside 20 seconds on a phone?** Yes by design: the first cat is already in the tub and scrubbing is a finger drag.
- **Does the name say what you do?** Yes, and the icon shows a half washed cat.
- **A reason to come back tomorrow that is not a bribe?** Tomorrow's visiting cat is shown as a silhouette, regulars come back and the Cat Book shows who is still missing. Visitors rotate weekly so nothing is ever gone for good. No streaks.
- **Smallest version in about a week of agent work?** Yes: one tub, one brush, ten cats, the Cat Book, coins and saving.
- **Paid random items, trading, stealing or user text?** Which cat walks in is random but free, with the chances shown on the appointment board. Coins cannot be bought. No trading, stealing or typed text.
- **Policy check still owed:** Roblox limits social hangouts with private spaces such as bathrooms to adults. Ours is an open shop floor where only cats are bathed, with no private rooms for player avatars. Review-QA confirms this against the live rules before launch.
- **Is everything original?** Yes: our own cats, names and art.

## Looked at and set aside
- A cat collecting game on the throw food formula: the formula is two months old and already has three or four copies.
- A cat fighting or cat steal game: the recent ones faded or stayed tiny, and stealing is against our rules.
- A cat growing game in the style of Ant Evolution (rising this week): not enough is known about why it works yet. Worth a look later.
- The first shortlist's Wash Muddy Pets: its scrub and reveal lives on as the first treatment in the cat spa.
