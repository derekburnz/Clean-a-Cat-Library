## What we asked him to look for
- How you get a pet without paying: in packs bought with the shop's own cash. The tutorial hands over the first pack at step 3.
- What a pet does: it is stock that is sold to customers. It is not a helper.
- What the staff do: check out customers, stock shelves and feed pets. Sold for Robux in the shop he saw.
- A reason to come back: the seven day check in, the daily quest and the level road.
