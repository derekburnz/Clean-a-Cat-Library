# Everything from Derek's playtests, sorted  (2026-11-24 ET, Planning-Agent)

Derek played six games on 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24 and sent about 80 screenshots, one clip and several chat notes. This file sorts all of it by theme for the team. His own words are in `playtest-notes.md`. The per game lists of what we take and leave, with owners, are in `my-pet-salon-screens-2026-11-24.md`, `restaurant-tycoon-3-screens-2026-11-24.md` and `barber-simulator-screens-2026-11-24.md`. The pictures are in the shared files under `Coordinator/references/` and stay out of the public repos. Nothing here is decided unless it says decided. What waits on Derek is on his plan page, file copy `pre-development-plan.md`.

The six games: My Pet Salon (the closest to ours), Capybara Onsen (a pad and merge game with a spa skin), Save a Horse: Wash & Collect (he calls it Steal a Horse, an egg game with a wash timer), Clean Everything (a sorting game with friends), Restaurant Tycoon 3 (the shop game that lasted longest) and Barber Simulator (a hands-on job game).

## 1. The wash is the game
- Horse game: the wash is a machine with a timer. Derek: "nothing really to do with cleaning". Lesson: our wash is always by hand and a timer never stands in for it. Waits belong to builds and upgrades.
- My Pet Salon: he liked the different kinds of step (swipe, aim water, pull things off, hold to trim, tap to knock off) and the praise words with a growing number. He disliked the same swipe three times, 16 steps in one job and the pet turning by itself.
- My Pet Salon replay (ten more pictures): a step is taught with one line at the top, a hand that shows the move and a card that ticks off the steps. A word marks a finished step, and perfect washes in a row add 10% each. A common pet has three or four steps and a rare cat six.
- Barber Simulator: holding on an area while the right thumb turns the view "was smooth and felt good". Idea for our wash view, plan page `turn`, not decided.
- Restaurant Tycoon 3: the work is one short swipe with a bar and a Cancel button.
- Ours: four to six short steps that differ, never the same twice in a row, a cat that holds still, a view the player turns. Decided: placing the kitten in the tub switches to a close up wash view.

## 2. The first minute
- Bad: about 10 seconds of loading and a cutscene with no skip (My Pet Salon), two loading screens (Clean Everything), three setup screens before the first customer (Restaurant Tycoon 3), a shop with Robux prices opened by the tutorial (horse game).
- Good: a short tutorial with a free first buy (Capybara Onsen), one bold step line with a short line under it and arrows on the floor (horse game).
- Ours, decided: a watched truck intro of about 4 seconds that starts the moment the character loads, a plain brown box, lift the kitten out, carry him to the tub, wash him, he becomes Suds.

## 3. Customers and the line
- My Pet Salon: one customer at a time felt lame, though you can press next pet or choose. Its customers are made from the friend list.
- Barber Simulator: a line of customers waits outside with clocks.
- Restaurant Tycoon 3: a VIP customer is announced with a Show Me button. No friends as customers or waiters.
- Ours: a bench of cats that differ, a Next cat button, rare visitors announced. Friends as customers: our pick is no at launch, waiting for Derek's tap.

## 4. Workers
- My Pet Salon and Restaurant Tycoon 3: named workers with a job, a hire price and a level price. Restaurant Tycoon 3 ties some upgrades to a worker's level.
- Ours, decided: human staff, not cats. Proposed: Jo and Sam, hired and levelled with coins, slower than the player, common cats only.

## 5. Pets
- My Pet Salon: a My Pets window, pets boost cash, the first one is sold.
- Barber Simulator: pets from a Robux roulette with the chances shown, slots sold for Robux. Derek could not find a free way apart from a pet egg promised for tomorrow.
- Derek's idea (open): washing earns the money and the pets the shop owns come from crates that are hard to earn or bought with coins, one pet for the player and one per worker.
- Our picks (open): every pet can be earned, crates never for Robux, chances shown, a sure new pet after a set number, the worker's slot for coins, washed coats still fill an index.

## 6. Things to chase and reasons to come back
- Clean Everything: nine maps and three hidden ones, modes, a rebirth, bars that count every object and shelf. It is the cleaning game that kept its players.
- Restaurant Tycoon 3: a long chain of locked upgrades that each say what they require, and upgrades that add new things to do.
- My Pet Salon: an album of 59 pets with counts per rarity and the next reward.
- Barber Simulator: a pet roll on day 2, a new tool on level up, a sign for the next building.
- Horse game and Clean Everything: cash earned while away, shown on return.
- Ours: the index of coats (about 24 on day one), an upgrade road in view, a tip jar that fills while away, a different visitor each day and, if pets come from crates, a free crate tomorrow. No streak.

## 7. The screen and the guiding
- Bottom help bubble with one button and a result card with stars and a line per reason (My Pet Salon). Goal bar with a count (Restaurant Tycoon 3). Step line and floor arrows (horse game, My Pet Salon). Marker with an icon over the next thing and a card of what the customer wants (Barber Simulator). Friend bonus percent on screen (Capybara Onsen, Barber Simulator). Glowing target for where a thing goes (Clean Everything).
- Derek: "the big black buttons on mobile take up a lot of the screen and block view sometimes (also happens for us in the picking up cat out of box)". Every prompt in ours becomes a small button in our own look.
- Puppy Spa: small prompt pills with one word (Admit, Clean), a ring on the floor at the goal, the owner's wish in a bubble and a star meter with a percent during the groom.
- Catches to avoid: Roblox's badge note covering a button (My Pet Salon, horse game, Barber Simulator) and a bubble under the jump button (My Pet Salon).

## 8. Asking and selling
- Derek: "a lot of these games ... ask for notifications while away almost instantly after starting". Ours: one ask at about minute 1, plan page `notifyask`, open.
- Favourite asked at about minute 4 (Capybara Onsen). Rewards for likes (My Pet Salon, Clean Everything). We do neither.
- Starter packs with countdowns in the first minute (Restaurant Tycoon 3, Barber Simulator, My Pet Salon), Robux offers on the screen from second one (Clean Everything, horse game), watch an ad buttons (Clean Everything, Barber Simulator), a stock counter (My Pet Salon), helpers rented by the minute (Clean Everything). Ours: no offer in the first session, no countdowns, nothing sold that skips the game.
