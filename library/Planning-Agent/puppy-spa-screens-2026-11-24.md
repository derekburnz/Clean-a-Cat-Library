## What we do not take, and why
- A plus button beside the coins (08), which usually sells coins. Ours are never sold.
- A customer with what looks like a player's name and avatar (02, 04, 06). If it is made from friends or other players, it is the friends as customers idea, and our pick on that is no at launch.
- Cleaning the table after each pet with one button press (07). A chore that is only a press adds a wait and no play.
