His words: Planning-Agent/playtest-notes.md. Sorted by theme: playtest-summary-2026-11-24.md there, with the *-screens-2026-11-24.md files per game. Puppy Spa 2026-11-24: felt bad to him (see [[derek-plan-answers]]); its small prompt pills are the model for ours. Playtests CLOSED 2026-11-24 evening at nine games; Clean the Animal Daycare was last ("pretty much the same as Clean Everything unrelated"). No more screenshots or recordings to be asked (his call).

My Pet Salon! (2026-11-23): he liked several kinds of wash step (swipe to scrub, aim water, pull burrs off, hold to trim nails, tap to knock things off), praise words with a growing combo number, a star rating with a coins and experience breakdown after each pet, tool upgrades, an index, rare "influencer" customers with rare pets and a permanent reward, hired helpers, clean pop-ups and a half dirty and half clean store picture. He disliked an unskippable cutscene, the same swipe three times, 16 steps in one job, the pet turning by itself and one customer at a time.

His lean on 2026-11-23, "just throwing ideas", NOT a decision: wash by hand, slower hired helpers, more customers so the player picks who is next, maybe impatient customers who leave.

My Pet Salon replay, 2026-11-24: "the big black buttons on mobile take up a lot of the screen and block view sometimes (also happens for us in the picking up cat out of box)", so every prompt in ours is a small button in our own look.

Horse game (Save a Horse, he calls it Steal a Horse), 2026-11-24: "just another steal an egg copy and nothing really to do with cleaning"; the wash is a wait. So ours is always by hand.

Clean Everything, 2026-11-24: pick a map, put items back.

Barber Simulator, 2026-11-24: "hold on area to cut and use right thumb on right side of screen to turn camera around the head mechanic that was smooth and felt good" (idea for our wash view, `q2-turn`, NOT decided). He could not find a way to get pets without paying, apart from a pop up to "come back tmmr for a pet egg".

Across games, 2026-11-24: many popular games "ask for notifications while away almost instantly" (`q2-notifyask`, our pick: once at about minute 1).

Derek checked 2026-11-24: in My Pet Salon you can press next pet or choose which waiting pet goes next (ours adds cats that differ on the card plus a Next cat button), and its customers are made from your friend list.

Restaurant Tycoon 3, 2026-11-24: "they don't use friends as waiters or customers can take some ideas from them". Friends as customers: our pick is NO at launch (`q2-friendcust`), waiting for his tap; do not build it.

Pet Store Tycoon, 2026-11-24: "uses the same little guys for tips and I like it", first person "is odd", "the tutorial steps are very well done. feels easy to follow" (a step count, one arrow, one lit thing, the rest dimmed), and it "shows the daily check in rewards and that day 2 has a good reward right after tutorial". Its starter bundle pop up after day 2 "might not be a bad idea for starter packs" (`q2-starterpack`, open; ours has fixed contents, no crate, no coins). Takes: Planning-Agent/pet-store-tycoon-screens-2026-11-24.md.

Derek's lean 2026-11-24, a working plan and not locked: washing cats makes the money and is unrelated to the pets the shop owns. Owned pets come from crates that are hard to earn, with human workers; one pet for the player and one per worker (the worker's slot is an upgrade). Washed cats still fill an index. His later answers on this are in [[derek-plan-answers]]; detail in Planning-Agent/gdd-proposed.md, section Owned pets.

**How to apply:** treat the likes and dislikes as strong steer for design, UI and store art. Treat the lean as a direction to design toward and test, and confirm with Derek before locking it.
