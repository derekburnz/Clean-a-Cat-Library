Full notes in Derek's words: Planning-Agent/playtest-notes.md (source is the plan page database, see [[planning-status]]). More games planned for 2026-11-24.

My Pet Salon! (2026-11-23): he liked several kinds of wash step (swipe to scrub, aim water, pull burrs off, hold to trim nails, tap to knock things off), praise words with a growing combo number, a star rating with a coins and experience breakdown after each pet, tool upgrades, an index, rare "influencer" customers with rare pets and a permanent reward, hired helpers, clean pop-ups and a half dirty and half clean store picture. He disliked an unskippable cutscene, the same swipe three times in a row, 16 steps in one job, the pet turning by itself and only one customer at a time.

Capybara Onsen (2026-11-23, iPad): not a spa game, a step on pads, buy and merge loop. Short tutorial, friend bonus percent on screen, asked for a favourite at about minute 4.

His lean on 2026-11-23, in his words "just throwing ideas", NOT a decision: the player washes by hand, hired helpers take customers too but much slower, more customers show up so the player picks who to take next, maybe customers who wait too long get impatient and leave.

**How to apply:** treat the likes and dislikes as strong steer for design, UI and store art. Treat the lean as a direction to design toward and test, and confirm with Derek before locking it.
