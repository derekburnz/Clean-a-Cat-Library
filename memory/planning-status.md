Derek's decisions so far (his own words, 2026-11-22 and 11-23 ET):
- The game must include cats. A cat spa is the direction (a cat spa ad of his did well; no ad numbers needed; Cat Cafe has no spa).
- Goal: a fun first 5 minutes for a very low bounce rate off Home recommendations and a strong reason to return on day 2. He handles day 7 himself with events.
- Loop he wants: get a dirty cat, clean it in different ways, return it to the customer or put it on display for money and index progress. Later upgrades take longer so players come back, with notifications when upgrades finish. Catching cats did poorly in his last game.
- Name LOCKED 2026-11-23 ET: Clean a Cat (over Clean the Cat), slug clean-a-cat. Cat Spaw is dropped (kids will not get it).
- He wants more research and his own playtests of animal spa games before the design is locked.

- Cat source recommended: customers bring cats, and strays turn up at the back door and stay on display.
